# Alcohol

**Applies to:** items

## Description

Everything to do with drink in the valley: bottles, [@name](../items/bimber.md) and the still, drinking places, the people who drink and the people who won't.

## Mechanics

### Sharing a bottle

- **Type:** rule
- At most four drink from one bottle, in equal halves: one player with one NPC, or two players with two NPCs.
- The bottle's [Drunk](../cards/drunk.md) cards are shared out: half to the NPCs drinking, half to the players. The players' half is spread among them as they choose.
- Whoever passes out, player or villager ([Tolerance](villager.md#tolerance)), first does something stupid they have no control over (GM's call), then is out of the scene for the rest of the phase.
- If two or more drinkers pass out in the same round, the stupid things cancel out: they simply pass out, and nobody remembers a thing.
