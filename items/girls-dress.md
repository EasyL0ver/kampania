# The Blue Dress

**Type:** Item — keepsake (a blue dress)
**Source:** The attic wardrobe during [Ciotka Found Dead](../events/ciotka-found-dead.md#open-the-wardrobe)
**Carried:** A folded blue dress. Kept in the house; can be shown to villagers.

## Hook

- A blue dress, folded and kept, lifted from the back of the attic wardrobe.

## Description

A blue dress, worn but clean. It is folded and set at the back of the wardrobe, kept rather than thrown out or handed down, put away with some care. No one wears it in Janina's house now.
