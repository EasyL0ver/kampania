# The Girl's Dress

**Type:** Item — keepsake (a teenage girl's dress)
**Source:** The attic wardrobe during [Ciotka Found Dead](../events/ciotka-found-dead.md#open-the-wardrobe)
**Carried:** A folded teenage girl's dress. Physical proof a girl that age once lived in the house; can be shown to villagers.

## Hook

- A teenage girl's dress, folded and kept, lifted from the back of the attic wardrobe.

## Description

A girl's dress, cut for a teenager, not a grown woman and not a child. It is folded and set at the back of the wardrobe, kept rather than thrown out or handed down. The cloth is worn but clean, put away with some care. No girl of that age lives in Janina's house now.

## Opportunities

- **A girl lived here once** — The dress is a teenager's, folded and kept like something mourned, not stored for use. Someone that age lived in this house and is gone. → Gives: [`girls-dress-in-ciotkas-house`](../clues/clues.md#girls-dress-in-ciotkas-house)
