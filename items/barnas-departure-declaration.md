# Soldier's Departure Declaration

## Hook

A single stamped administrative sheet with a dated signature.

## Setup

A single administrative sheet, official stamps, dated 1954. It records [@name](../characters/soldier.md) surrendering his house, buildings, and land and resettling his household west "of his own free will." At the bottom, his signature. The paper is what let the "they moved away" story stand for thirteen years.

## Content

> **DECLARATION OF DEPARTURE AND SURRENDER OF PROPERTY**
>
> I, the undersigned [@name](../characters/soldier.md), of sound mind and my own free will, declare that I and my household are leaving this village to resettle in the western territories.
>
> I renounce all claim to the house, farm buildings, and land I have held here, and surrender them to the State Agricultural Farm for the good of the collective.
>
> I depart of my own accord and hold no grievance against the village or the state.
>
> *(signed)* [@name](../characters/soldier.md)
>
> Filed: 1954 · Countersigned: gmina clerk
