# Anchor and Hammer

**Type:** Item — clamp and driving hammer for the crux
**Source:** Borrowed from [Michał Pytlak](../characters/foreman.md) if he is cooperative, bonded, or talked round with a decent lie; otherwise packed from the [PGR farm](../locations/pgr-farm.md) with the spare gear for the ridge climb.
**Carried:** Lets the party fix the crux seam when the slab cannot be forced in the rain. It is the fixed anchor a climber can hang off and the hammer needed to set it.

## Description

A short steel clamp and a battered hammer, kept together as a single field set. The clamp is driven into the shale seam under the overhang; the hammer drives it home. Once planted, the seam becomes a fixed point and a party can seat the charge clean rather than free-soloing the slab in the rain.
