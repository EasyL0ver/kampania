# The Geologist's Kit

**Type:** Item — field surveying kit (level, clinometer, drainage tables)
**Source:** Carried from the start by the geologist PC, packed with the committee's gear
**Carried:** Lets a PC put field findings and the master plan's numbers together and rerun the flood projection.

## Description

A worn canvas roll of survey tools: a level and clinometer, a folded copy of the resettlement master plan with its flood-line figures, and drainage tables. It is the equipment a party would use to check terrain and, once the outlets are known, to redo the arithmetic the state got wrong.
