# Jar of Pills

## Hook

An open medicine jar with some pills missing.

## Setup

A jar of pills on the table[](#prescription-label-names-ciotka), cap off. Some are missing.[](#sedative-pills-missing-from-jar)

## Opportunities

### Sedative pills missing from jar
- **Noticed by:** [Medicine](../cards/medicine.md)
- **When:** setup:items/jar-of-pills.md
- **Outcome:** A strong sedative. Enough of it can kill. Pills are missing from the jar.

### Prescription label names Ciotka
- **Noticed by:** [Bureaucracy](../cards/bureaucracy.md)
- **When:** setup:items/jar-of-pills.md
- **Outcome:** The jar's label: strong medication like this comes only from an official pharmacy, prescribed to [@name](../characters/ciotka.md) herself.
