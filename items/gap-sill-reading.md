# Gap Sill Reading

**Type:** Item — field reading of the plug's crest sill
**Source:** Read from the crest during [Climb the Plug](../events/climb-the-plug.md#take-the-reading-with-the-kit).
**Carried:** Half of what settles the ridge gap; read with the [Gap Fill Reading](gap-fill-reading.md) by a Geology holder, or phoned to [prof. Bieńkowski](../characters/professor.md).

## Hook

- A height for the plug's lowest saddle, levelled from the crest.

## Description

Measured from the crest, the plug's lowest saddle, the sill any rising water would have to top to spill through into %BIG-BASIN%, stands above the %NEW_VILLAGE% flood line. Water cannot overtop the plug. This settles only the height, not whether the fill leaks.
