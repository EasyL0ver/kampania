# Gap Sill Reading

## Hook

- A height for the plug's lowest saddle, levelled from the crest.

## Setup

Measured from the crest, the plug's lowest saddle, the sill any rising water would have to top to spill through into [@name](../locations/big-basin.md), stands above the [@name](../locations/new-village/new-village.md) flood line. Water cannot overtop the plug. This settles only the height, not whether the fill leaks.
