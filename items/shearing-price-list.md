# Shearing Price List

## Hook

- A travelling shearer's price list from another state farm.

## Setup

A typed price list for a travelling shearing team, made out to a neighbouring state farm down the valley, folded and soft at the creases.

## Content

```
SHEARING TEAM — PRICE LIST 1967
for: PGR Lutowiska

Spring shearing ............ 100 zł per 50 head
Dipping (optional) .........  40 zł per 50 head
Travel included.
```
