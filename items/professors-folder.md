# Professor's Folder

## Hook

- A grey cardboard folder tied with string, stamped by the Kraków hydrology institute.

## Description

A few typed pages and a hand-drawn sketch of the valley, signed by [@name](../characters/professor.md).

## Content

> The state's safe level assumes two things: that the ground stands at the heights on the map, and that the valley drains.
>
> Test both on the ground. Level the terrain against the map. Check whether water can still get out.
>
> I know of two outlets: the water-gap through the ridge, and the old streambed on the far ridge. I know of no others.
>
> The paperwork does not hold up. I cannot prove it from Kraków.
