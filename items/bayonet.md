# Bayonet

**Type:** Item — weapon (keepsake, hidden)
**Source:** Hidden among Edek's things, found by [searching Edek's room](../locations/ciotkas-house.md#search-edeks-room)
**Carried:** Evidence. Wartime German army kit in [Edek](../characters/glupek.md)'s keeping, impossible from his father's Polish KBW gear.

## Hook

- An old sheathed bayonet, the blade worn and lightly oiled, kept like a treasure.

## Description

An old military bayonet, the blade kept clean and faintly oiled, the grip worn smooth from handling. It was tucked away among [Edek](../characters/glupek.md)'s few things like a prize and kept hidden from [Janina](../characters/ciotka.md). To an untrained eye it is just an old army knife of no particular origin.

## Opportunities

- **Know the pattern** `(requires: Violence)` — The fuller, the muzzle ring, the maker's stamp read at a glance: this is a German wartime bayonet, army issue, nothing like the Polish KBW kit his father carried. It is partisan-era war booty, not something out of his father's house. → Gives: [`edeks-bayonet-is-german`](../clues/clues.md#edeks-bayonet-is-german)
