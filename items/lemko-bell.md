# Lemko Bell

**Type:** Item — evidence
**Source:** Taken down from the attic in [Ciotka Found Dead](../events/ciotka-found-dead.md)
**Carried:** Evidence. An Eastern-rite object out of place in a Roman Catholic home.

## Description

A small brass Greek Catholic liturgical bell, Cyrillic lettering around the rim. It can be compared with the [cerkiew](../locations/old-village-cerkiew.md) or shown to [Stefania Kopacz](../characters/babcia.md) or [Paraskewia Chyłak](../characters/hag.md).
