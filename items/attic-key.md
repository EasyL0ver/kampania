# Attic Key

**Type:** Item — key
**Source:** Left by ks. Władysław Pająk in Janina's house ([The priest leaves them the key](../events/ciotka-found-dead/ciotka-found-dead.md#the-priest-leaves-them-the-key)).
**Carried:** Opens the locked attic hatch in Janina's house without breaking it ([Open the attic with the key](../locations/ciotkas-house/ciotkas-house.md#open-the-attic-with-the-key)).

## Hook

- A small iron key on a loop of string.

## Description

The key to the attic hatch in Janina's house, the one part of the house she guarded.
