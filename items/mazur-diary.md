# Tadeusz Mazur's Diary

**Type:** Item — a settler's private diary, kept from ~1948
**Source:** Given by [Wanda Mazur](../characters/widow.md) once the committee uncovers the pension cover-up and chooses to shield her
**Carried:** Evidence. A contemporaneous settler's record of the valley, including the night the Barnaś family vanished.

## Hook

- A worn cloth-bound notebook, its pages close-written in a careful farmer's hand, years of weather, work, and prices.

## Description

A thick notebook bound in faded cloth, swollen and soft at the corners from years of handling. Most of it is the plain ledger of a settler's life: what was sown and when, prices at market, a cow calved, a roof mended, hard winters marked. The hand is careful and unhurried. Spread through it, a handful of longer entries step back from the weather and the prices to set down what was happening to the valley itself.

## Content

> **[1948]** We have our plot at last. The valley stood near empty when we came, house after house left open with the hearths gone cold. The people here before us were Rusyns, Lemkos, and the word is the army cleared the lot of them out the year before and drove them off west and north. Some of the houses at the far end are burned down to the sills. We are told to ask no questions and take what the state hands us.

> **[1950]** Edward Barnaś has the best of the land in the valley, the flat black soil down by the water that the rest of us would give an arm for. Nobody will say how he came by it, and nobody has a good word for the man. People keep their distance from him. He lives with his woman, though the two were never wed and she keeps her own name. A hard man. He set fence posts with me this spring and does not shirk the work, which is more than most round here will grant him.

> **[1954]** The Barnaś place stands empty. A week past there was smoke from the chimney and washing on the line, and now the house is shut and cold, the whole family gone in a single night. Zbigniew says they moved west for the work. Only the boy is left behind, taken in by Janina next door.
>
> A man does not leave standing crops and a fed pig and walk west in the dark without a word to a neighbour. You do not abandon a thing the week after you build it. I say nothing. Everyone says nothing. That is how it is here now.

> **[1956]** They have made the whole valley into a state farm this year, a PGR, and folded our plots into it. Zbigniew Gajda is the man set over it now, a Party man with the card to prove it. What we broke our backs to clear is the state's again.

> **[1965]** Michał has me on the silo now. He has always kept that job to himself, says it is no place to send a man up alone, but he is run off his feet this season and I am the one he trusts for it. The footing up top of the grain is a treacherous thing. I do not love the work, but a man does what the farm needs of him.
