# PGR Income Journal

## Hook

- The PGR farm's income journal: what the farm sold, to whom, and for how much.

## Setup

A worn ledger kept alongside the expense journal, in the same bookkeeper's hand: deliveries to the state buying points and the odd sale to a neighbouring farm, each with a weight, a price and a total.[](#this-springs-wool)

## Content

```
PGR INCOME JOURNAL — SELECTED ENTRIES
State Agricultural Farm

Date        Buyer / Description                                    Amount
────────    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────   ──────

                         — 1966 —

19.04.1966  Bednarz, Jan — carting with farm horses, 2 days           60 zł

02.08.1966  PGR Lutowiska — hay, second cut, 4 t                    1,600 zł
21.09.1966  State grain purchase, Lesko — rye, 6,500 kg            9,750 zł
17.11.1966  Forest district Baligród — timber hauling, 6 days         540 zł

                         — 1967 —
20.03.1967  GS Lesko — seed potatoes, 2,900 kg                      1,740 zł
11.04.1967  Wróbel, Kazimierz — loan of the cart, 1 day               30 zł
29.05.1967  State wool purchase, Sanok — wool, 180 kg @ 60 zł     10,800 zł
15.06.1967  GS Lesko — scrap iron, old harrow                          85 zł
```

The wool is the only entry that speaks to a count: at two to three kilos a fleece, 180 kg is a flock of roughly 60 to 90 sheep.
