# Bottle of Vodka

**Tags:** [@name](../tags/alcohol.md)

## Hook

- A half-litre of Wyborowa, unopened.

## Setup

Clear glass, a paper label, the state seal intact. Shop vodka: the kind you buy, not the kind you make.

## Mechanics

### Drunk cards

- **Type:** rule
- The bottle holds 2 [Drunk](../cards/drunk.md) cards, shared out among whoever drinks it (see [Drinking rounds](../tags/libation.md#drinking-rounds)).
