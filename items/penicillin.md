# Penicillin

**Type:** Item — drug (glass ampoules)
**Source:** The cabinet in [Helena's store](../locations/the-store.md) — obtained by [her terms](../characters/matrona.md), by [Tadek's word](../characters/wujas.md), or by breaking in.
**Carried:** Given to Pawełek at a child's dose, it cures the water fever. See [Give Pawełek the penicillin](../events/pawelek-falls-ill.md#give-pawełek-the-penicillin).

## Description

A few small glass ampoules of penicillin from the store's locked cabinet, the only stock of it in the village.
