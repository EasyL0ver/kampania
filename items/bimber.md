# Bottle of Bimber

**Tags:** [@name](../tags/alcohol.md)

## Hook

- A bottle of cloudy home-distilled spirit, stoppered with a twist of paper.

## Setup

An old bottle refilled at a still, no label. Strong, rough, and illegal to make or sell.

## Mechanics

### Drunk cards

- **Type:** rule
- The bottle holds 1 to 4 [Drunk](../cards/drunk.md) cards, its strength, set by whoever made it (the GM's call), shared out among whoever drinks it (see [Drinking rounds](../tags/libation.md#drinking-rounds)).
- Whoever made the batch knows its strength exactly, and so does anyone who has drunk from it before. Nobody else knows until they drink.
