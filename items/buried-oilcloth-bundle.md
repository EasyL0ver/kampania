# Buried Oilcloth Bundle

**Type:** Item — evidence
**Source:** Dug up in the backyard of [Ciotka's house](../locations/ciotkas-house/ciotkas-house.md#dig-in-the-backyard).
**Carried:** Evidence.

## Hook

- An oilcloth bundle, stiff with earth.

## Description

A KBW uniform with its insignia, Edward Barnaś's identity documents and service papers for the Bieszczady in 1947, and love letters addressed to "M.K.", all wrapped in oilcloth.
