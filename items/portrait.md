# The Portrait

## Hook

- A portrait of a dark-haired young woman in a blue dress, unwrapped from a bundle hidden in the ruined cerkiew.

## Setup

An oil portrait of a young dark-haired woman in a blue dress, found wrapped and sheltered in a dry corner of the ruined cerkiew at [@name](../locations/old-village-cerkiew.md). It is painted with a care nothing around it gets. No name or label on the face, though the back of the canvas is not blank[](#private-endearment-on-canvas-back). The blue dress is rendered as carefully as her face.[](#fauvist-bold-expressive-colour) A small mark sits low in one corner.[](#small-signature-low-in-corner)

## Opportunities

### Small signature low in corner

- **When:** setup:items/portrait.md

- **Outcome:** Low in one corner sits a small, easily missed signature. A trained eye reads the hand and the mark: this is [@name](../characters/painter.md)'s own work.
- **Gives:** [emil-signed-the-portrait](../clues/clues.md#emil-signed-the-portrait)

### Fauvist bold expressive colour
- **Noticed by:** [Culture](../cards/culture.md)
- **When:** setup:items/portrait.md
<!-- Prompted by: [portrait-hidden-in-cerkiew](../clues/clues.md#portrait-hidden-in-cerkiew) -->
- **Outcome:** The portrait's style is Fauvist: the woman and her dress carried in bold, unnatural, expressive colour, alive and full of light.
- **Gives:** [portrait-is-fauvist](../clues/clues.md#portrait-is-fauvist)

### Private endearment on canvas back
- **When:** setup:items/portrait.md
<!-- Prompted by: [portrait-hidden-in-cerkiew](../clues/clues.md#portrait-hidden-in-cerkiew) -->
- **Outcome:** The back of the canvas bears a line: "For my forget-me-not, the only blue I ever got right." A private endearment, not a commission.
- **Gives:** [portrait-dedicated-to-forget-me-not](../clues/clues.md#portrait-dedicated-to-forget-me-not)
