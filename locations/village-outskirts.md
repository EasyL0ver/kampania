# Village Outskirts

**Type:** Location (revisitable, multi-trip)
**Location:** Terrain around the village — river, hillsides, forest edges
**Present:** Whoever joins the geologist. Michał Pytlak may tag along if asked.
**Available:** Any day. Requires a player with geological knowledge.
**Cost:** 1 action per site surveyed. The full picture is four sites; only the ridge is strictly needed to prove the flood (the other three confirm the map and come back safe). Michał Pytlak or the office maps waive the three easy sites.

## Setup

The geologist heads out with an old military topographic map and instruments. His job is to validate the map against reality — compare marked elevations, river courses, terrain features with what's actually there. The map is outdated. The river has shifted. The terrain tells a story — soft rock, water tables, slope gradients. The river is higher than it should be. The soil near the valley floor is saturated. But proving it takes work — multiple measurement sites, samples, calculations. This isn't a quick look.

The land here has a shape worth reading. This is Carpathian flysch — hard **sandstone** ridges laid over soft, slip-prone **shale** — so the country runs in long parallel ridges with valleys cut between them, and the streams cross from one valley to the next by punching narrow gaps straight through a ridge. %NEW_VILLAGE% sits in one valley; the [old village](old-village-ruins.md) lies in the lower valley next to it, on the other side of a sandstone ridge. On the map, a **water-gap cuts through that ridge** — an open notch where the water drains from the new-village side down into the lower old-village basin. That's the map. The ground, when the geologist finally gets to it, does not match.

The obvious place to worry is the **southern approach** — from the south the land eases down into the valley in a long, mild depression, no steep bank anywhere, the ground the river would spread across if it rose. Everyone's eyes go there; it's the first thing a surveyor would check. The map marks it safe — water spreading into that shallow dip stays shallow and slides off, never climbing to the houses. And the map is right about it.

The survey route takes the geologist across the area over multiple trips. On one of the routes — ruins. Stone foundations, overgrown walls, a collapsed church. The old village. It's on the map. Nobody in the new village mentioned it.

The routes also skirt the village's ragged edge. One passes the last house before the treeline — dark timber, no curtains, dogs that don't bark — where a man is usually out back, busy with his hands. Another crosses the track the drinking men take: on a bad afternoon you'll catch Tadek Gajda and two or three others walking up toward the forest with bottles, not talking.

## Opportunities

- **Foreman's knowledge** — if Michał Pytlak tags along, he points out terrain features, old drainage paths, where the water pools after rain. He knows this ground by instinct — including the notch in the ridge, and that it *used to* run water through before "the hill came down" and choked it years ago. → Bonus info: practical flood knowledge + waives the three holding survey sites (south, both bowls), so only "Survey the ridge and the gap" still needs doing
- **Read the ridge** — on the route between the two valleys, the low saddle in the ridge where the map marks an open water-gap. Up close it's not open — it's a tumble of loose earth and broken rock, an old landslide slumped across the notch. Soft ground, not the hard sandstone of the ridge proper. → **Geology/Engineering:** This is a landslide plug. Where the map shows a drain, there's a dam. It seals the gap — and it's loose debris, not bedrock, so it could be breached. (Full read comes with the "Survey the ridge and the gap" action below; spotting the mismatch is free.)
- **Spot the old village** — ruins visible through the trees on one of the survey routes. Stone, not wood — this was a proper settlement once. → **Gives:** [old village was Lemko](../clues/clues.md#old-village-was-lemko), [old village was burned](../clues/clues.md#old-village-was-burned) · **Unlocks:** [%OLD_VILLAGE%](old-village-ruins.md) as a location
- **Spot Butcher at his house** — on the route, pass by Stanisław Rezeń's place. He's there, alone, doing something. → **Gives:** [butcher-heads-toward-forest](../clues/clues.md#butcher-heads-toward-forest)
- **Spot the drinking crew heading into the forest** — Tadek Gajda and the boys walking toward the treeline with bottles. → **Gives:** [drinking-crew-heads-to-forest](../clues/clues.md#drinking-crew-heads-to-forest)

## Actions

### Survey the southern approach
- **Requires:** Geological knowledge (player ability). The obvious site — the first anyone would check.
- **Cost:** 1 action. Waived if the party has the maps from [Wojewoda's office](pgr-office.md) or Michał Pytlak along — both already agree the south is safe.
- **Outcome (holds — the red herring):** From the south the land runs down in a long, mild depression, no steep ground, the natural place for a rising river to spread. The map says water here stays shallow and drains off, never reaching the houses — and the instrument agrees, exactly. It checks out. A survey that stops here walks home reporting the village is safe. It's the wrong side of the valley to be looking.
- **Gives:** [southern-approach-safe](../clues/clues.md#southern-approach-safe)

### Survey the old-village bowl
- **Requires:** Geological knowledge (player ability).
- **Cost:** 1 action. Waived with the office maps or Michał Pytlak.
- **Outcome (holds):** The low sink next door. The map says lowest ground, the natural drain; the measurements confirm it. Everything the map claims about *this* basin is true — it will take the water whether or not anyone acts.
- **Gives:** [old-village-basin-is-the-low-sink](../clues/clues.md#old-village-basin-is-the-low-sink)

### Survey the new-village bowl
- **Requires:** Geological knowledge (player ability).
- **Cost:** 1 action. Waived with the office maps or Michał Pytlak.
- **Outcome (holds):** The village sits exactly where and how the map says: above the flood line, on firm ground. On paper it is safe — *because the map assumes the water drains off through the gap in the ridge.* Nothing about the village itself is wrong. That assumption is the whole game, and it is not tested here.
- **Gives:** [new-village-sits-above-flood-line](../clues/clues.md#new-village-sits-above-flood-line)

### Survey the ridge and the gap
- **Requires:** Geological knowledge (player ability), and knowing to look here — the "Read the ridge" opportunity, Michał Pytlak's hint about the notch, [prof. Bieńkowski](../characters/professor.md#call-him-for-help--where-to-look) pointing you at the gap over the phone, or simply following the map's marked water-gap.
- **Cost:** 1 action. This is the one site the maps and Pytlak cannot waive; someone has to measure the plug.
- **Outcome (breaks — the mechanism):** The deciding measurement, and the one thing nobody re-checked. The map's open water-gap is **plugged by an old landslide.** The swollen river can't drain through to the lower old-village basin, so it backs up and rises onto %NEW_VILLAGE% instead. The map was true the day it was drawn; the land moved after. Proof, not speculation. The village floods, and *this* is why. The final calculation is the player's own hand on their own instrument — before it, the party could still tell themselves the map was right; after it, no one can.
- **Gives:** [New Village will flood](../clues/clues.md#new-village-will-flood), [flood-cause-plugged-gap](../clues/clues.md#flood-cause-plugged-gap)
- **Unlocks:** "Tell Wojewoda with geological proof" at the [PGR office](pgr-office.md). Feeds Michał Pytlak's engineering plan — the plugged gap is exactly what he wants to blow open (see [Foreman Saves the Village](../events/foreman-saves-village.md)).

### Wander the forest
- **Requires:** Nothing — no thread to follow
- **Cost:** 1 card (repeatable)
- **Outcome:** You strike off the tracks with nothing to go on. This is a no-failure game — there's no "found nothing." The forest is vast and *used*, and it always shows you a thread. But wandering blind is the slow way in: each card buys a single lead that a word from the right villager would have handed you outright. The GM gives whichever thread the party still lacks — or rolls a d6, skipping any lead already held:
  1. **Bottle glass and cold ash** along a worn trail. → **Gives:** [drinking-crew-heads-to-forest](../clues/clues.md#drinking-crew-heads-to-forest)
  2. **Deep boot-prints** cutting off-path toward the ridge. → **Gives:** [butcher-heads-toward-forest](../clues/clues.md#butcher-heads-toward-forest)
  3. **Woodsmoke and burnt herbs** carried on the wind. → **Gives:** [hag-exists](../clues/clues.md#hag-exists)
  4. **Collapsed dugouts and rusted metal** underfoot. → **Gives:** [old-wartime-positions](../clues/clues.md#old-wartime-positions)
  5. **[Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md)**, drifting and watchful, who bolts toward the old village when noticed. → **Gives:** [glupek-drawn-to-well](../clues/clues.md#glupek-drawn-to-well)
  6. **You're being watched** — [Stanisław Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) at the treeline, motionless, unhurried. He doesn't approach. He just marks you. → **Composure −1**; he now counts the party among the things he's noticed (feeds his predatory interest — see his file).
- **Note:** Wander never hands over the payoff, only the thread. Reaching the still, the well-path, the cabin, or the bunker still costs the follow-up action.

### Follow the drinking crew
- **Requires:** [drinking-crew-heads-to-forest](../clues/clues.md#drinking-crew-heads-to-forest) — you know Tadek's crew comes up here
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** Trail leads to the [bimber still](bimber-still.md) — a lash-up of copper and barrels in a clearing, half-hidden, clearly worked for years.
- **Gives:** [bimber-still](../clues/clues.md#bimber-still) · **Unlocks:** [bimber still](bimber-still.md) as a location

### Follow the Butcher's path
- **Requires:** [butcher-heads-toward-forest](../clues/clues.md#butcher-heads-toward-forest) — you've clocked Rezeń taking the treeline
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** His trail heads deeper than expected — past the old village, toward the ridge. Hard to follow without being noticed.
- **Gives:** [butcher-visits-the-well](../clues/clues.md#butcher-visits-the-well)

### Follow the smoke
- **Requires:** [hag-exists](../clues/clues.md#hag-exists) — you know someone lives out in the forest
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** You quarter the woods for the woodsmoke the villagers whisper about. A thread of it, then the smell of burning herbs sharpening, then a narrow footpath through undergrowth. The [hag's cabin](hags-cabin.md) at the end of it.
- **Unlocks:** [hag's cabin](hags-cabin.md) as a location

### Search the old wartime positions
- **Requires:** The military map from [Wojewoda's office](pgr-office.md), or Michał Pytlak's terrain hints
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** A deliberate sweep of the likely ground turns up traces of old wartime positions — collapsed dugouts, rusted metal. Someone dug in here once.
- **Gives:** [old-wartime-positions](../clues/clues.md#old-wartime-positions)

### Look for the UPA bunker
- **Requires:** [old-wartime-positions](../clues/clues.md#old-wartime-positions), or word that a partisan bunker is out here ([upa-bunker](../clues/clues.md#upa-bunker))
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** Working outward from the dugouts, someone with an eye for it catches the tell — ventilation shafts breaking a hillside where no chimney should be, an entrance swallowed by vegetation and fallen timber. [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) is sometimes drifting near the mouth of it, for reasons nobody can explain.
- **Gives:** [upa-bunker](../clues/clues.md#upa-bunker) · **Unlocks:** [UPA bunker](upa-bunker.md) as a location
