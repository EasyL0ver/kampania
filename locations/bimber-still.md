# Bimber Still

**Type:** Location (hidden, discoverable)
**Location:** Hidden clearing off a forest path, roughly halfway between %NEW_VILLAGE% and %OLD_VILLAGE%. Near a stream. **NOT near the well or [Paraskewia Chyłak](../characters/hag.md)'s cabin.**
**Present:** [Tadek Gajda](../characters/wujas.md) and his drinking crew — Szymek Kępa, Romek Głowacz, Franek Mucha. Irregular, mostly evenings.
**Available:** Requires forest exploration or following Tadek/crew (see [Village Outskirts](village-outskirts.md#follow-the-drinking-crew)).
**Cost:** 1 action per visit

## Setup

A crude copper still under camouflage netting, tucked in a clearing by a stream. Empty sugar sacks, barrels, stacked firewood, a small fire pit. The ashes are still warm — this place is worked, and often. Fermentation smell hangs thick. A footpath leads in from %NEW_VILLAGE%, packed hard by daily traffic, subtle but readable to a careful eye. The sacks are stamped — traceable to [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md)'s [store](the-store.md).

**If the crew is here:** Tadek and two or three men around the fire — bottles, cards, low talk. They hear the players before they see them. The conversation stops, heads turn, and the silence goes hostile. As the bottles empty Tadek gets louder — and, late in the game, stranger.

**If the crew is absent:** Fresh boot prints, warm ashes, half-full bottles. Someone was here recently and will be back.

## Opportunities

- **The still, up close** — copper, netting, barrels, and more bottles than any handful of men drink for themselves. This is a worked operation, years old, feeding the village. → **Streetwise:** commercial scale, not one man's habit — and this kind of thing runs on someone's protection. → **Gives:** [bimber-still](../clues/clues.md#bimber-still)
- **The sugar sacks** — store stamps trace the bulk sugar straight to [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md)'s [store](the-store.md). → **Accounting/Streetwise:** she supplies at a markup — profiting off her own brother's crew. Looks conspiratorial; it's only commerce.
- **Nothing else here** — for players hunting a deeper secret: there isn't one. No weapons, no documents, no hidden room, no grave. The forest's hidden thing is just moonshine. → **Observation:** the site is exactly what it looks like, and no more.

## Actions

### Walk in on the crew
- **Requires:** The crew present
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** Instant tension — they take the committee for a bust. Each man reacts to type:
  - **[Tadek Gajda](../characters/wujas.md)** — panics. Begs, bribes, threatens in rapid succession. His fear looks exactly like a guilty conscience, indistinguishable from the lynch guilt unless the players already know the difference.
  - **Szymek Kępa** — belligerent. State people sticking their noses where they don't belong. Tries to intimidate.
  - **Romek Głowacz** — quiet, calculating. Watches faces, gauging what the players know against what they're only fishing for.
  - **Franek Mucha** — aggressive. Stands, blocks the path, most likely to make it physical.
  Deny, deflect, a bottle pressed on the players to buy silence. Press hard and Franek escalates while Szymek moves to flank. The crew has run off curious locals before — a government committee is a new level of threat.
- **World State Change:** The crew knows the committee found the still. Goodwill with Tadek's circle is burned, and word that the outsiders are snooping the forest spreads.
- **NPC State Change:** The crew turns hostile/guarded toward the players. The still is now a thing the players hold over Tadek — enabling the leverage play below.

### Use the still as leverage on Tadek
- See [Tadek Gajda — Leverage — the bimber play](../characters/wujas.md#leverage--the-bimber-play).
- **Gives:** [wujas-is-guilty](../clues/clues.md#wujas-is-guilty)

### Stake out the crew
- **Requires:** Concealment (Discretion helps); the crew present or expected
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** The players watch from cover — drinking, cards, arguments, Tadek getting louder as the bottles empty. What they catch depends on how far the [well's influence](../story-facts/the-well.md) has spread:
  - **Early:** ordinary drunk behaviour. Nothing leaks.
  - **Mid (well active):** Tadek drinks the same but stays sharp-eyed and restless, picks fights he'd normally avoid, grabs Szymek's arm too hard. The crew notices something's off.
  - **Late:** he mutters in his sleep by the fire — names, fragments, a woman, something lost. The crew trades looks; they don't know what he's talking about, but they know it's bad.
- **Gives (late only):** [wujas-mourns-someone](../clues/clues.md#wujas-mourns-someone)
