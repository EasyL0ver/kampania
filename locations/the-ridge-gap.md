# The Ridge Gap

**Type:** Location (revisitable, discoverable)
**Location:** The notch in the ridge between the %NEW_VILLAGE% valley and the [%OLD_VILLAGE% basin](old-village-ruins.md).
**Present:** [Michał Pytlak](../characters/foreman.md) (if brought on the survey)
**Available:** After reaching the gap from the survey routes ([Village Outskirts](village-outskirts.md))
**Cost:** 1 action per interaction

## Setup

- The gap is the low notch where the ridge dips between the two valleys.
- The state map draws it as an open channel, the valley's main drain.
- An old landslide has choked the notch with fallen rock and earth.
- From the base the fill looks like loose rubble floodwater would seep straight through.
- The plug is a steep bank about two storeys high: wet clay and broken sandstone.
- The clay is greasy after rain and the loose rock shifts underfoot.
- The top of the plug overlooks the [old-village basin](old-village-ruins.md), the low ground the map says the water should drain into.
- Nothing but this fill stands between the two valleys.

## Opportunities

- **The fill won't pass water** `(requires: holding [`gap-fill-examined`](../clues/clues.md#gap-fill-examined) and Geology)` — Up close the packed clay and shattered rock read as impermeable; this plug is a seal, not a filter. → Gives: [gap-is-blocked](../clues/clues.md#gap-is-blocked)

## Actions

### Climb the plug
- **Requires:** [landslide-in-the-gap](../clues/clues.md#landslide-in-the-gap), and physically climbing the fallen fill in the notch
- **Prompted by:** [landslide-in-the-gap](../clues/clues.md#landslide-in-the-gap)
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** You scramble up the plug and get your hands into the fill. From below it looked like loose rubble; up close it is dense clay and shattered rock packed tight. Whether that seals the gap is a read for a geologist on-site or for [prof. Bieńkowski](../characters/professor.md) down the phone.
  - **With Physique or Survival:** You pick a clean line up the greasy bank and reach the fill without trouble.
  - **Without either:** You still make it up, slower, sliding back once or twice on the wet clay; the GM may charge an extra card or a scrape.
- **Gives:** [gap-fill-examined](../clues/clues.md#gap-fill-examined)
