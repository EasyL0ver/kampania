# The Rectory (Plebania)

**Type:** Location (revisitable)
**Location:** %NEW_VILLAGE% — plebania adjacent to [the church](the-church.md).
**Present:** [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md) (inside, usually)
**Available:** Any day
**Cost:** 1 action per visit

## Setup

- The plebania stands adjacent to the church.
- The plebania has a stone foundation older than %NEW_VILLAGE%, a kitchen, a book-lined study, a spare room with a cot, and a cellar.
- The study is small and cold, with one lamp and the parish ledger.
- The cellar has stone walls, old liturgical supplies, jars, dust, and a door with a newer padlock.
- A thread of stale tobacco hangs in the study, though no ashtray is in sight.

## Opportunities

- **The smoke under the damp** `(requires: Chainsmoker)`: the stale smell and a smoker's stain on his fingers give him away. The priest smokes, and hides it. The brand does not show from this alone. → Gives: [priest-smokes](../clues/clues.md#priest-smokes)

## Actions

### Search the rectory
- **Requires:** Priest absent or distracted
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** The parish ledger records Hania Barnaś's First Communion in the early 1950s; after the priest shelters Edek, the cellar also holds a straw mattress, blanket, food scraps, and a water jug.
- **Gives:** [barnas-had-a-daughter](../clues/clues.md#barnas-had-a-daughter); NPC State Change: if Edek is in the cellar, players can confront the priest about hiding him.

### Find the hidden cigarettes
- **Requires:** Priest absent or distracted
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** Tucked out of sight is a pack of Carmen, the premium brand. He smokes the same brand the village would name at Janina's door, and hides it.
- **Gives:** [priest-smokes-carmen](../clues/clues.md#priest-smokes-carmen)
