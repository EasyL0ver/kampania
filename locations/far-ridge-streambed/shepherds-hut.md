# The Shepherd's Hut

## Hook

- An abandoned Lemko shepherd's hut high on the far ridge, above the dry streambed.

## Setup

An abandoned shepherd's hut stands hidden in the gorse high on the slope, its low doorway still up, a weathered ram's skull fixed over the lintel and marks cut into the frame, facing out.[](#smoke-blackened-room-warded-against-outward-threat) Inside is a single smoke-blackened room: ash crosses on the walls[](#herders-wards-against-wolves-and-the-dead), Cyrillic scratched among them[](#scratched-cyrillic-names-and-plea), the whole room turned against the dark.[](#overwhelming-sense-of-the-wards-purpose)

## Opportunities

### Smoke-blackened room warded against outward threat
- **Noticed by:** [abandoned-house-by-streambed](../../clues/clues.md#abandoned-house-by-streambed)
- **When:** setup:locations/far-ridge-streambed/shepherds-hut.md
- **Outcome:** Ducking inside the hut, you find a single smoke-blackened room under the ram's skull, empty for decades yet armoured against the dark: ash crosses smeared across a long-cold hearth where the shepherd's watra once burned, iron driven into the threshold, the black remains of herb bundles hanging from the rafters, three-barred crosses cut so deep and so often into the timber that whole boards are furred with them. The wards all face outward, to keep something out.
- **Gives:** [three-barred-cross-in-abandoned-house](../../clues/clues.md#three-barred-cross-in-abandoned-house)

### Herders' wards against wolves and the dead
- **Noticed by:** [Culture](../../cards/culture.md)
- **When:** setup:locations/far-ridge-streambed/shepherds-hut.md
- **Outcome:** Greek Catholic hill-herders' warding against wolves and the restless dead, the same tradition that mourns the unburied. Whatever they feared up here, they lined every surface against it, then one season walked down the mountain and never came back. → No clue; understanding.
