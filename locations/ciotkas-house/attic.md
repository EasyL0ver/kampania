# Ciotka's House — The Attic

**Part of:** [Ciotka's House](ciotkas-house.md)
**Type:** Location (revisitable)
**Location:** Up the ladder through the hatch in [Ciotka's house](ciotkas-house.md)
**Present:** —
**Available:** While Janina is absent or distracted, or once [the attic hatch](ciotkas-house.md#the-attic-hatch) is open
**Cost:** 1 action per visit

## Hook

- The hatch in the ceiling of Janina's house, a short ladder folded beside it.

## Setup

- Low under the roof, dusty, out of sight of the rooms below.
- Edek's spare toys and Janina's stores near the hatch.
- Further in, a dead man's things left where no one looks.
- A pile of rubbish with a box marked "EDEK" in it.
- A big wardrobe.
- A small child's rattle, kept with the rest.
- Icons stacked up, not hung.
- A small brass bell high up on a beam.

## Opportunities

- **The roof is wrong** `(noticed by: [Handiwork](../../cards/handiwork.md))` `(prompted by: [ciotka-is-dead](../../clues/clues.md#ciotka-is-dead))` — Up under the rafters the shingles and boards don't match the roof: pieces cut and curved to skin a dome, reused flat. A builder sees it at once, this timber was made for something round, not this house. → Gives: [`roof-built-for-a-dome`](../../clues/clues.md#roof-built-for-a-dome)
- **The wrong icons** `(noticed by: [Devotion](../../cards/devotion.md))` — Icons are stacked up in the attic, out of sight of the rooms below. A believer sees it at once: these are Eastern-rite, not the Roman Catholic images hung downstairs. They do not belong in this house. → Gives: [`icons-in-attic-not-catholic`](../../clues/clues.md#icons-in-attic-not-catholic)

## Actions

### Search the attic
- **When:** Janina absent or distracted
- **Prompted by:** [glupek-forbidden-from-attic](../../clues/clues.md#glupek-forbidden-from-attic)
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** Up past Edek's toys and Janina's stores, among the dead man's things left where no one looks, is an unopened [letter](../../items/barnas-letter.md) addressed to Edward Barnaś, postmarked 1955 and never opened. Janina keeps the hatch shut and Edek barred from it, so going up risks being caught, and [Ryszard Dudka](../../characters/neighbour.md) is across the road.
- **Gives:** [Edward Barnaś's unopened letter](../../items/barnas-letter.md), aware:items/barnas-letter.md
### Search the pile of rubbish
- **Prompted by:** [`ciotka-is-dead`](../../clues/clues.md#ciotka-is-dead)
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** A box in the rubbish is marked "EDEK" and reads at a glance like the boy's things. It is not: the belongings are a grown man's, wrong size and wrong age for [Edek Barnaś](../../characters/glupek.md). Edward Barnaś went by Edek too. The box is thick with dust and has sat untouched for years. Among the belongings is an old iron front-door key.
- **Gives:** [box marked "EDEK"](../../items/edek-box.md)
### Open the wardrobe
- **Prompted by:** [`ciotka-is-dead`](../../clues/clues.md#ciotka-is-dead)
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** Inside the big wardrobe, a blue dress, folded and kept.
- **Gives:** [blue dress](../../items/girls-dress.md)
### Examine the child's rattle
- **Prompted by:** [`ciotka-is-dead`](../../clues/clues.md#ciotka-is-dead)
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** A small child's rattle, kept with the rest. A baby lived in this house once.
- **Gives:** [childs-rattle-in-ciotkas-house](../../clues/clues.md#childs-rattle-in-ciotkas-house)
### Take down the bell
- **Prompted by:** [`ciotka-is-dead`](../../clues/clues.md#ciotka-is-dead)
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** A small brass Greek Catholic liturgical bell with Cyrillic lettering sits high up, out of place in a Roman Catholic home. Reaching it knocks it loose and it falls, ringing and clattering.
- **Gives:** [Lemko Bell](../../items/lemko-bell.md), [lemko-bell-in-ciotkas-house](../../clues/clues.md#lemko-bell-in-ciotkas-house)
