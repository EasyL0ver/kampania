# %OLD_VILLAGE% — The Well

**Type:** Location (critical)
**Location:** [%OLD_VILLAGE%](old-village-ruins.md) — central or prominent ruin site.
**Present:** Nobody (day), [Stanisław Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) (regular visits), [Paraskewia Chyłak](../characters/hag.md) (night rites)
**Available:** After reaching %OLD_VILLAGE%; partially obscured by debris.
**Cost:** 1 action to find or examine; additional actions to excavate

## Setup

- The well is stone-lined.
- The top is partially collapsed or sealed with debris.
- Moss and age are visible on the stone.
- The interior is dark.
- Depth is unknown.
- Water level is obscured by debris and collapsed masonry.
- Some debris has been cleared recently.
- Other debris has been piled back.
- Candle wax is present near the disturbed debris.
- The well contains 1947 massacre victims: twelve Lemko villagers from %OLD_VILLAGE% plus Dmytro Kosach.
- The well contains the 1954 lynch victim [Edward Barnaś](../characters/soldier.md).
- Animal remains have been put into the well over the years.
- If dam floods reach %OLD_VILLAGE%, the well goes underwater.
- Hazards include unstable stone, unknown depth, decomposition, water, and unsafe excavation.

## Opportunities

- **Cleared and piled debris** `(requires: Survival)` — One person has been clearing the well while another has been blocking it and leaving candle wax. → Gives: [butcher-vs-hag](../clues/clues.md#butcher-vs-hag)

## Actions

### Investigate the well after confrontation
- **Requires:** Hag is dead after [Well Confrontation](../events/well-confrontation.md)
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** A light shows dark water and shapes below the waterline; something fresh went in.
- **Gives:** [someone-thrown-in-well](../clues/clues.md#someone-thrown-in-well)

### Track the attacker
- **Requires:** Aftermath visible at the well: blood and drag marks
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** Boot prints and dog tracks lead from the well toward [Rezeń's house](butchers-house.md).
- **Gives:** Scene Unlock: confront [Stanisław Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) about the well attack.

### Excavate the well
- **Requires:** Time, tools, and safe access to the shaft
- **Cost:** 2 actions
- **Outcome:** Excavation reveals remains from both the 1947 massacre and the 1954 lynch.
- **Gives:** [massacre-bodies-in-well](../clues/clues.md#massacre-bodies-in-well); [lynch-body-in-well](../clues/clues.md#lynch-body-in-well)
