# The Rectory (Plebania)

## Hook

The plebania beside the church in [@name](../new-village.md).

## Setup

- The plebania stands adjacent to the church.
- The plebania has a stone foundation older than [@name](../new-village.md), a kitchen, a book-lined study, a spare room with a cot, and a cellar.
- The study is small and cold, with one lamp and the parish ledger.
- The cellar has stone walls, old liturgical supplies, jars, dust, and a door with a newer padlock.
- A thread of stale tobacco hangs in the study[](#priest-smokes-and-hides-it), though no ashtray is in sight.

## Opportunities

### Priest smokes and hides it
- **Noticed by:** [Chainsmoker](../../../cards/chainsmoker.md)
- **When:** setup:locations/new-village/the-church/the-rectory.md
- **Outcome:** the stale smell and a smoker's stain on his fingers give him away. The priest smokes, and hides it. The brand does not show from this alone.
- **Gives:** [priest-smokes](../../../clues/clues.md#priest-smokes)

## Actions

### Search the rectory
- **When:** Priest absent or distracted
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** The parish ledger records [@name](../../../characters/jagna.md)'s First Communion in the early 1950s; after the priest shelters [@first-name](../../../characters/glupek.md), the cellar also holds a straw mattress, blanket, food scraps, and a water jug.
- **Gives:** [barnas-had-a-daughter-hania](../../../clues/clues.md#barnas-had-a-daughter-hania)

### Find the hidden cigarettes
- **When:** Priest absent or distracted
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** Tucked out of sight is a pack of Carmen, the premium brand. He smokes the same brand the village would name at [@first-name](../../../characters/ciotka.md)'s door, and hides it.
- **Gives:** [priest-smokes-carmen](../../../clues/clues.md#priest-smokes-carmen)
