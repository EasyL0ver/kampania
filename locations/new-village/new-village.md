## Hook

- [@name](new-village.md), the resettled village at the end of the valley road.

## Setup

- One muddy road runs the length of the valley, lined with low wooden houses.
- A shop with a bench out front stands halfway along the road.[](#a-shop-halfway-along-the-road)
- A church stands on the hill above the roofs.[](#a-church-on-the-hill)
- At the lower end of the valley stand the barns and the grain silo of the state farm.[](#barns-and-a-silo-down-the-valley)
- Down the valley, the river runs high and brown.
- The forest closes in on every side.
- At the edge of the village stands the only red-brick house; washing hangs under its eaves.[](#the-red-brick-house)
- Next to it, a bare bachelor's house; a heavy curtain is drawn across the window facing the road.[](#a-bare-house-with-a-drawn-curtain)
- Across the road stands the best house on the best plot, solid and well kept, two locks on its front door.[](#the-best-house-on-the-best-plot)
- Near the shop, a whitewashed house with a swept path and a flower box, a small shed in the yard behind it.[](#a-whitewashed-house-near-the-shop)
- The road ends at a two-storey house with a fenced yard, the largest in the village.[](#the-two-storey-house-at-the-end-of-the-road)
- Past the last houses, a dark unpainted house stands alone at the treeline.[](#a-dark-house-at-the-treeline)
