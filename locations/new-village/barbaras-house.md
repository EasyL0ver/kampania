# Barbara's House

## Hook

The edge of [@name](new-village.md), beside [@name](../../characters/neighbour.md)'s house.

## Setup

- **House:** One-room house with a curtain dividing the sleeping area.
- **House:** Wood stove, table, two chairs.
- **House:** Clean but bare.
- **House:** Built of red brick by[](#red-brick-house-exceeds-barbaras-means) [@name](../../characters/wojewoda.md); the other village houses are timber.
- **House:** Catholic cross on the wall.
- **Mirrors:** Every mirror and reflective surface is covered with old cloth.
- **Mirrors:** The cloth coverings are yellowed with age.[](#mourning-coverings-are-systematic-and-old)
- **Babcia:** [@name](../../characters/babcia.md) sits by the stove or window.
- **Babcia:** She mutters non-Polish fragments, songs, and prayers.[](#muttering-sounds-like-unfamiliar-ukrainian-variant)
- **Babcia:** A small three-barred crucifix hangs above her corner.[](#three-barred-crucifix-in-babcias-corner)
- **[@first-name](../../characters/pawelek.md):** [@name](../../characters/pawelek.md) usually plays near Babcia and repeats some of her words.
- **[@first-name](../../characters/pawelek.md):** He may be outside playing during the day.
- **[@first-name](../../characters/barbara.md):** [@name](../../characters/barbara.md) offers tea and food when home.
- **[@first-name](../../characters/barbara.md):** During the day, [@first-name](../../characters/barbara.md) works at the [@name](pgr-farm/pgr-farm.md).
- **Neighbour:** [@name](../../characters/neighbour.md) is often visible at the fence.
- **Neighbour:** He brings firewood and checks on[](#neighbours-help-reads-as-guilt) [@first-name](../../characters/pawelek.md).
- **Night:** Overnight guests hear Babcia praying, pacing, and muttering in the dark.
- **Night:** Overnight guests may hear [@first-name](../../characters/pawelek.md) talk in his sleep.
- **Night:** On still nights, overnight guests hear a woman's singing carried from the forest on the wind, far off and alone.
- **Progression:** On later days, Babcia becomes more lucid.
- **Progression:** After [@name](../../characters/hag.md) is killed, Babcia becomes agitated and watches the forest.
