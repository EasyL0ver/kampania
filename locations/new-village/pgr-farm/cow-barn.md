# The Cow Barn

**Tags:** [@name](../../../tags/pgr-location.md)

## Hook

- One of the two long barns on the PGR farm: the cow barn.

## Setup

A long, low barn with stalls down both sides and a feed passage between them. A name board hangs over each stall; milk cans wait by the door. It smells of straw, dung and milk.[](#a-hen-about)
