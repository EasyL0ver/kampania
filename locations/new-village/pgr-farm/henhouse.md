# The Henhouse

**Tags:** [@name](../../../tags/pgr-location.md)

## Hook

- The PGR farm's henhouse.

## Setup

A small timber henhouse with perches and nesting boxes along the walls. By day the chickens are out between the farm buildings; at dusk they come back in to roost.

## Actions

### Count the chickens
<!-- Prompted by: [pgr-has-chickens](../../../clues/clues.md#pgr-has-chickens) -->
- **Cost:** 3 time
- **Outcome:** They will not hold still. You count them in the yard, lose track when two run under the cart, start again, chase a speckled one out from behind the henhouse, and count the same brown hen three times. By the end the light is going and you have it: eleven. Possibly twelve.
