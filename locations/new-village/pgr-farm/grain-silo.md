# The Grain Silo

**Tags:** [@name](../../../tags/pgr-location.md)

## Hook

- The PGR farm's concrete grain silo.

## Setup

A tall concrete silo with an iron ladder up its side to a hatch at the top. One side carries a patch of newer, cruder concrete. Inside, the stored grain lies in a deep pale mass, and the only way to it is down from the hatch.[](#a-hen-about)
