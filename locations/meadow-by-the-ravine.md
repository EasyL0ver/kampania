# Meadow by the Ravine

**Type:** Location (hidden, discoverable)
**Location:** Deep in the forest between the villages, off an old hunting track — an open clearing on the lip of a steep ravine.
**Present:** Nobody. A remote grave and the sound of water below.
**Available:** Requires the lead that [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) buried remains out here ([neighbour-believes-jagna-dead](../clues/clues.md#neighbour-believes-jagna-dead)). Reached from [Village Outskirts](village-outskirts.md).
**Cost:** 1 action to reach

## Setup

The dark press of the forest opens without warning into light. A meadow — tall grass gone to seed, wildflowers, the hum of insects — running to the edge of a ravine where the ground simply drops away. Somewhere below, out of sight, water moves over stone. A single old tree stands off to one side, and under it a low cairn of stones, moss-grown, deliberate. Nobody stacks stones like that by accident.

It's the one place in these woods that feels at peace. Given what's under the cairn, that peace is its own kind of wrong.

## Opportunities

- **The cairn** — a careful stack of moss-covered stones under the lone tree. Not a rockfall, not a boundary marker. A grave, and an old one.
  - **Read:** Whoever built this did it slowly, by hand, alone. No priest, no marker, no name. This is grief, not disposal — someone came out here to *bury* a stranger properly and tell no one.
- **The ravine** — the drop is sudden and steep, the water a long way down. **Read:** A girl running from the village in the dark, off any path, would never see it coming. It's a place a body could end up without anyone's help.

## Actions

### Open the grave
- **Requires:** Found the cairn (you're here)
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** The stones lift away easily; they were never packed to keep anything in. Underneath, shallow: a rotted coat, its colour long gone. Some bones, scattered by soil and time. No skull to speak of — no face, nothing that names them.
- **Gives:** Item / Evidence — [Remains from the Ravine](../items/ravine-remains.md).
