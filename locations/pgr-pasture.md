# The Pasture

## Hook

- The state farm's pasture on the slopes outside the farm.

## Setup

Open grazing on the slope beyond the farm buildings, fenced with posts and wire patched more than mended; tufts of grey wool hang on the barbs. The grass runs up to the forest. Near the trees it is flattened in a dark, wide patch, and something was dragged from it into the undergrowth.[](#wolves-have-been-taking-the-flock) The flock grazes bunched tight in the middle of the pasture, every head turning at once when a bird lifts from the trees.
