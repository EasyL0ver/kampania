# Barbara's House

**Type:** Location (revisitable)
**Location:** Edge of %NEW_VILLAGE%, beside [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md)'s house
**Present:** [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) (mornings and evenings), [Stefania Kopacz](../characters/babcia.md), [Pawełek Kopacz](../characters/pawelek.md) (usually)
**Available:** Any time; Barbara home mornings and evenings
**Cost:** 1 action per visit

## Hook

The edge of %NEW_VILLAGE%, beside Ryszard Dudka's house.

## Setup

- **House:** One-room house with a curtain dividing the sleeping area.
- **House:** Wood stove, table, two chairs.
- **House:** Clean but bare.
- **House:** Built of red brick by [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md); the other village houses are timber.
- **House:** Catholic cross on the wall.
- **Mirrors:** Every mirror and reflective surface is covered with old cloth.
- **Mirrors:** The cloth coverings are yellowed with age.
- **Babcia:** [Stefania Kopacz](../characters/babcia.md) sits by the stove or window.
- **Babcia:** She mutters non-Polish fragments, songs, and prayers.
- **Babcia:** A small three-barred crucifix hangs above her corner.
- **Pawełek:** [Pawełek Kopacz](../characters/pawelek.md) usually plays near Babcia and repeats some of her words.
- **Pawełek:** He may be outside playing during the day.
- **Barbara:** [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) offers tea and food when home.
- **Barbara:** During the day, Barbara works at the [PGR](pgr-farm.md).
- **Neighbour:** [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) is often visible at the fence.
- **Neighbour:** He brings firewood and checks on Pawełek.
- **Night:** Overnight guests hear Babcia praying, pacing, and muttering in the dark.
- **Night:** Overnight guests may hear Pawełek talk in his sleep.
- **Night:** On still nights, overnight guests hear a woman's singing carried from the forest on the wind, far off and alone.
- **Progression:** On later days, Babcia becomes more lucid.
- **Progression:** After [Paraskewia Chyłak](../characters/hag.md) is killed, Babcia becomes agitated and watches the forest.
