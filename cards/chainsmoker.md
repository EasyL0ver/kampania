# Chainsmoker

**Pool:** Starting card

**Type:** Both

**Composure:** none

## Card

You read a person by their tobacco the moment you meet them: the cheap Sport most of the valley smokes, the rough strong stuff, or the rare premium brand a vain man buys. You make [Carmen](../items/cigarette-butts-from-ciotkas.md) on sight or scent, instantly, without comparing a thing.

## Special rules

You cannot think straight without one going. As long as you have cigarettes, a smoke break steadies you: your first composure [recovery](../story-facts/game-system.md#recovery) each day costs no card. Run out and the shakes set in — when a scene turns tense and your hands are empty, GM's call, **1 composure**.
