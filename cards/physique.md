# Physique

**Pool:** Starting card

**Type:** Both

**Composure:** +1

## Card

Strength, stamina, and what your body will absorb. Rough terrain, weather, hauling, digging, carrying a grown adult out of the forest in the dark. Matching a village drinker glass for glass and still being the one asking the questions at the end of the night. The grit to keep going after the work turns grim.
