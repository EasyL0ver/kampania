# Loaded

**Pool:** Starting card

**Type:** Key

**Composure:** none

## Card

You came into this valley with real money in your pocket: hard currency, not the wages anyone here earns. But a poor village sees a full wallet from a long way off.

## Special rules

**Once per game** you can make it count: a bribe nobody can refuse, or a thing bought that money should not be able to buy out here. It opens a door force and talk could not. Spend where the wrong person is watching and you stop being a committee clerk and become the outsider with cash — GM's call: suspicion, resentment, or a hand held out that never closes again.
