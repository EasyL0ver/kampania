# Survival

**Pool:** Starting card

**Type:** Key

**Composure:** none

## Card

Staying alive and oriented where nobody is coming to help. Which way is out, what the weather is about to do, where the ground turns bad, where a person would shelter. Reading what has been through here lately and how long ago. **Physique is whether your body can take the forest; this is whether you know what you are looking at.**
