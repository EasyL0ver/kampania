# Bruised

**Pool:** Acquired in play

**Type:** Modifier

**Composure:** none

## Card

You took a beating in a fight nobody meant to kill in: a fistfight, a drunk brawl, being shoved around. Nothing is bleeding, but you feel every step.

## Special rules

- No death clock.
