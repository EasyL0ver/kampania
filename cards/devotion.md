# Devotion

**Pool:** Starting card

**Type:** Both

**Composure:** +1 (only while in a state of grace)

## Card

Not a box on a form — you believe, and you practise. In 1967 that is rarer than it looks, and it is never free. ks. Pająk can tell the difference between a man who crosses himself and a man who means it, and so can Janina, Babcia and Wanda Mazur. It opens the confessional as something other than a place to collect evidence. And the Party expects better of its servants: somebody always notices.

## Special rules

The **+1 composure** holds only while you are in a state of grace, and it is the only thing in the pool that moves during play.

- **You sin.** The +1 is suspended at once; if that puts you below 0, you [panic](../story-facts/game-system.md#below-zero-panic) like anyone else.
- **You confess it to [ks. Pająk](../characters/priest.md).** 1 card. The point comes back, and he now knows what you did.
- **No priest, no absolution.** If he is dead, broken, or has refused you, the point stays gone for the rest of the game.

A committee member kneeling in his confessional and meaning it counts **+1 on the [Faith in Redemption](../story-facts/spiritual-endings.md) score**. Full rule: [game-system](../story-facts/game-system.md#devotion--the-state-of-grace).
