# Culture

**Pool:** Starting card

**Type:** Key

**Composure:** none

## Card

The Lemko and Greek Catholic worlds — icons, rites, prayers, what a symbol cut into a headstone means, what a funeral is supposed to look like. **And the forms that come with them:** when the hat comes off, when to wait at the treeline instead of walking up, when the glass set in front of you is a test, which questions cannot be asked standing up. What a community does, and why. **Charms, omens and the warding-off of bad luck belong to Superstitious, not here.**
