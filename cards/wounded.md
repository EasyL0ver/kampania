# Wounded

**Pool:** Acquired in play

**Type:** Modifier

**Composure:** none

## Card

You are bleeding and it is not stopping on its own: a knife cut, dog bites, cracked ribs. You can still talk, investigate, interview and observe. You cannot run, fight or endure.

## Special rules

- **Blocks [Physique](physique.md) and [Violence](violence.md)** while you hold this card.
- **Death clock.** If untreated by the end of the game, the character dies.
- **Treatment:** 1 time from someone with [Medicine](medicine.md), a player or an NPC with medical knowledge. Discard this card. See [Wounds](../story-facts/game-system.md#wounds).
