# Finesse

**Pool:** Starting card

**Type:** Key

**Composure:** none

## Card

Getting past a thing by touch instead of force. Quick hands, quiet feet. Locks, latches, drawers, a window that was supposed to be shut — **Handiwork fixes and builds; this opens and takes.** Following without being made, searching a room and leaving it exactly as you found it, lifting something off a table while its owner is still talking, lying smoothly enough that nobody reaches for the door. And knowing the shape of a room: who really decides here, who is frightened of whom, where the danger is.
