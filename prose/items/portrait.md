## Source

A wrapped canvas hidden in the ruined cerkiew, found during [Open the wrapped bundle](../locations/old-village-cerkiew.md#open-the-wrapped-bundle)

## Carried

An oil portrait of an unnamed young woman. Can be shown to villagers or matched against other evidence.

## Description

An oil portrait of a young dark-haired woman in a blue dress, found wrapped and sheltered in a dry corner of the ruined cerkiew at [%OLD_VILLAGE%](../locations/old-village-cerkiew.md). It is painted with a care nothing around it gets. No name or label on the face, though the back of the canvas is not blank. The blue dress is rendered as carefully as her face.


## theSignature

Low in one corner sits a small, easily missed signature. A trained eye reads the hand and the mark: this is [Emil Rzepka](../characters/painter.md)'s own work.

## theStyle

The portrait is Fauvist: the woman and her dress carried in bold, unnatural, expressive colour, alive and full of light.

## theDedicationOnTheBack

Turn the canvas over and there is a line written on the back: "For my forget-me-not, the only blue I ever got right." A private endearment, not a commission.

## compareTheDressToThePortrait

Set the portrait beside the blue dress taken from the wardrobe: the same cut, the same blue. The woman he painted wore it.
