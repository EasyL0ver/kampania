## Type

Named character — sibling (brother)
**Freudian role:** [Ego](../story-facts/freudian-triangle.md) — the failing mediator, cracking under the weight of what he cannot reconcile

## Vital Statistics

- **Born:** 1925
- **Age in 1967:** 42
- **Lives in:** [Bimber still](../locations/bimber-still.md) area / drifts between siblings' homes
- **Settled:** ~1948 — first wave, with siblings

## Character

Alcoholic wreck and the weakest link in the cover-up. He helped in the 1954 lynch, let Hania run, and has drowned the guilt in bimber for thirteen years.

## Appearance

- **Clothes:** Checked shirt with rolled sleeves, old marynarka with elbow patches, maciejówka cap cocked forward.
- **Hair & face:** Still handsome under the wear; unshaven, bloodshot eyes visible up close.
- **Carriage:** Lean and rangy; moves loose and easy, like he is still the life of the party.

His raspy warm voice gets louder and funnier with drink. The morning shakes and the silence after the charm runs out are private.

## Opinions

- **[Helena Rzepka](matrona.md)** — She watches me like a hawk. I hate it because I know she is waiting for me to crack.
- **[Janina Gajda](ciotka.md)** — She still leaves food out for me. I cannot stand her kindness because I do not deserve it.

## Mechanics

### Drinking Buddy

Tadek's guard only drops for people who drink with him. Unlike a [Bond](#bond), this is not exclusive and not hidden — **any** number of PCs can become drinking buddies, and a player can feel themselves being pulled into his circle.

**Becoming a buddy** (GM tracks silently, no announcement):
- Share a full drinking session with him — Drink skill or the Alcoholic card. Nursing a glass or refusing does not count. The entry point is [Drink with the crew](../locations/the-store.md#drink-with-the-crew) at the store, or drinking with him at [dinner](../events/dinner.md).
- Do it without judging him, pulling rank, or steering straight to interrogation.

One qualifying session makes a PC a drinking buddy for the rest of the game. It cannot be lost.

## smokingWhileDrinking

Requires (original): Tadek present and drinking at the [bimber still](../locations/bimber-still.md) or the [store](../locations/the-store.md), and Chainsmoker.

With a bottle in hand he chain-smokes. A smoker reads the brand: the cheapest Sport, never Carmen.

## censusNerves

He is not nervous about the census; he is nervous about being asked anything at all.

## drunkCensusPerformance

Requires (original): Census interview and Tadek is drunk.

He turns the answers into absurd village theatre.

## drunkPropertyPerformance

Requires (original): Property assessment and Tadek is drunk.

He claims a fake grand estate made of the still, his brother's house, and moonlight.

## getShitfacedWithTadek

The committee drinks Tadek past the point of guard. He gets loud, then maudlin, and grief for a woman he won't name slips out before he can catch it.

## askAboutTheButcher

Loose with drink, Tadek remembers [Rezeń](butcher.md) sitting in on the crew's sessions years back, bottle in hand like any of them. Then one day he just stopped, and never came back to the fire.

## leverageTheBimberPlayFirstVisit

He is relieved that the players are only pressing him about the still. He gives gossip, complaints, and minor dirt.

Gives (original): NPC State Change: Tadek owes the players and will keep talking if they return.

## leverageTheBimberPlaySecondVisit

Requires (original): First leverage visit completed and well influence active.

The bottle is not working, and half-sentences slip out around Hania before he catches himself.

## leverageTheBimberPlayThirdVisit

He can no longer hold the story together.

## censusInterview

He gives his name and age, reaches for the bottle, and tries to end the questions quickly.

## propertyAssessment

He says he owns nothing and drifts between siblings' kitchens and the still.

## getPawelekThePenicillin

Faced with the sick boy being his own, Tadek goes to his sister [Helena](matrona.md). She opens the cabinet for her brother and counts out a child's course, and no census changes hands.

## Bond (full wording)

- Share a drink with him and match his pace — do not nurse it, do not judge.
- Ask about his youth or his music — show genuine curiosity about who he was before the bottle.
- Encounter his still and say nothing to anyone.

## Grudge (full wording)

- Pour out his bottle, cut him off, or refuse to let him drink in front of you.
- Moralise at him about the drinking — lecture, pity, or try to "fix" him.
- Pull rank on him — threaten him with the committee's authority to make him talk.
