**Present:** [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) (after [Janina Gajda](../characters/ciotka.md)'s death)
**Available:** After [Ciotka Found Dead](ciotka-found-dead.md), when players follow the Edek route to the bunker.

## Trigger

- [Janina Gajda](../characters/ciotka.md) is dead and [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) has fled ([`edek-ran-to-upa-bunker`](../clues/clues.md#edek-ran-to-upa-bunker)).
- Players track the footprints into the forest ([`glupek-fled-into-forest`](../clues/clues.md#glupek-fled-into-forest)) or already know the bunker.
- Players reach the [UPA bunker](../locations/upa-bunker.md).

## Hook

- From the bunker mouth: movement in the dark, heavy breathing, a large shape pressed back into the earth.

## Setup

- [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) is inside the bunker, near the entrance, pressed back against the earth wall.
- He has been here since the night [Janina Gajda](../characters/ciotka.md) died.
- He is cold and has not eaten.
- He watches the players' hands and does not bolt.
- He goes still when frightened and will not say his aunt's name.
- The ceiling is low and the cut is tight; the lower sections are flooded.
- Crowded, grabbed, or faced with the butcher, dogs, or raised voices, he panics.
- Approached slowly, alone, without raised voices, he calms and can be reached.
- He trusts [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md) and the church.

## heHasNotRunFromYou

He is pressed to the wall watching hands, not fleeing; he is hiding from the village and from what he was told, not from the players.

## tooBigForTheTunnel

In the low flooded cut his panic would be lethal, to him and to anyone between him and the dark.

## daysWithoutFood

Cold and unfed since the night she died, he cannot last much longer in the bunker.

## reachHim

Requires (original): approach alone, without crowding or raised voices

He goes still and lets the players near. He knows his aunt is dead and will not say her name. He calms enough to be spoken to and led.

Gives: NPC State Change: [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) is calmed and will talk; Scene Unlock: [Coax what Janina told him](#coax-what-janina-told-him), [Take him to the priest](#take-him-to-the-priest), [Take him to Helena](#take-him-to-helena).

## coaxWhatJaninaToldHim

In broken fragments he gives up what his aunt told him before she died: the village killed his father, and he is what is left of it. Saying it breaks him again.

## takeHimToThePriest

Named the priest, or led toward the church, he follows. [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md) takes him in and hides him in the [rectory](../locations/the-rectory.md) cellar.

Gives: World State Change: [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) is sheltered at the priest's (see [Shelter](../characters/glupek.md#shelter)).

## takeHimToHelena

**Requires:** Edek calmed (see [Reach him](#reach-him)); committee authority

Led under committee authority, he follows to [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md)'s. She takes the living evidence into her own keeping.

Gives: World State Change: [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) is sheltered at Helena's (see [Shelter](../characters/glupek.md#shelter)).

## forceHimOut

Grabbed, cornered, or driven, he panics in the tight flooded cut, breaks past into the dark lower tunnels, and does not come back up.

## Mechanics

- Calmed, he will talk and can be led, but only to someone he trusts (the priest) or under committee authority (Helena).
- Offered no trusted destination, or told to leave alone, he refuses and stays in the bunker.
- Crowded, grabbed, or faced with the butcher, dogs, or raised voices, he panics and bolts into the flooded dark (see [Force him out](#force-him-out)).

## Exits

- To [The Rectory](../locations/the-rectory.md) if he is taken to the priest.
- To [Helena Rzepka & Emil Rzepka's house](../locations/matronas-house.md) if he is handed to Helena.
- Back to the village; the [operator](operator-refuses-help.md) route turns on his survival.
- Leave him; he stays hidden in the bunker.

## If Missed

- If the players never follow the Edek route, he stays hidden in the bunker, cold and unfed.
- Nobody else looks for him.
- The [operator](operator-refuses-help.md) route still turns on his survival.
