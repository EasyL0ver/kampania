## Header

**Location:** [Zbigniew Gajda's office](../locations/wojewodas-house.md)
**Present:** [%OPERATOR%](../characters/secondary/operator.md#operator-the-telephone-exchange-operator), [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) (optional)
**Available:** Day 3 onward, during [the flood](the-flood.md), when players call outside for rescue.

## Trigger

- The village is cut off by floodwater.
- The players use the phone in Zbigniew's office.
- The players ask the exchange to connect them to outside help.
- The call routes through the same town exchange used by earlier long-distance calls.

## Setup

- The operator answers clearly despite the storm.
- The line is working.
- The operator hears the players' request for rescue.
- The operator responds with procedure instead of help.
- The operator never says no.
- The operator never hangs up.
- The operator gives no name, no dispatch, no timeline, and no accountable official.
- The operator keeps the channel open and useless as long as the players keep talking.

## aPersonOnTheLine

The line is not failing; the operator is present, lucid, and choosing how much help to provide.

## procedureWithoutAction

Every answer records or routes the request without sending help.

## theMissingRefusal

The operator avoids a clear no because a clear refusal could be challenged.

## nameHer

Requires: On the line with her.

The committee puts her name to the voice: Hania. If they are only guessing, saying her name, her father's, or the well buys one caught breath before she resumes procedure, deniable and not repeatable. Once they know the operator is [Hania Barnaś](../characters/jagna.md) and name her as such, she stops pretending: she does not deny it, does not hang up, and the flat clerk's indifference turns quiet, personal, and vengeful. The rescue does not change; every word after is between the committee and Hania.

Gives: World State Change: the operator drops the clerk and answers as Hania (see Mechanics)

## herBrotherIsInTheWater

Requires: The committee can give her an account she cannot dismiss as a stranger's cruelty — that the operator is [Hania Barnaś](../characters/jagna.md) herself ([`operator-is-jagna`](../clues/clues.md#operator-is-jagna)), that the village idiot [Edek](../characters/glupek.md) is her own brother, [Edward Barnaś](../characters/soldier.md)'s son ([`edeks-father-left`](../clues/clues.md#edeks-father-left)) and so sibling to Edward's daughter ([`barnas-had-a-daughter-hania`](../clues/clues.md#barnas-had-a-daughter-hania)), and that he is alive and trapped now, fled to the [UPA bunker](../locations/upa-bunker.md) after [Ciotka Found Dead](ciotka-found-dead.md)

She buried the whole family in her mind in 1954, the baby included. Hearing, and believing, that her brother lived breaks the procedure for good. She stops withholding and relays the call without confirming her identity.

Gives: World State Change: the line relays real calls, but no rescue arrives before the flood's own timeline (see Mechanics)

## turnHerOnTheVillage

Requires: The committee knows the operator is [Hania Barnaś](../characters/jagna.md) ([`operator-is-jagna`](../clues/clues.md#operator-is-jagna)) and can name every one of her family's killers properly — [Helena](../characters/matrona.md), who aimed the men ([`matrona-orchestrated-lynch`](../clues/clues.md#matrona-orchestrated-lynch)), and the three who carried it out: [Tadek](../characters/wujas.md) ([`wujas-participated-in-lynch`](../clues/clues.md#wujas-participated-in-lynch)), [Zbigniew](../characters/wojewoda.md) ([`wojewoda-participated-in-lynch`](../clues/clues.md#wojewoda-participated-in-lynch)), and [Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) ([`butcher-participated-in-lynch`](../clues/clues.md#butcher-participated-in-lynch)). A partial list she does not trust.

Given the full, correct roster of the 1954 lynch, she believes the committee knows what was done to her family. She turns the line into a weapon, relaying the calls that expose the village's crimes because they serve retribution, not rescue.

Gives: World State Change: the truth can leave the valley through [The Report](the-report.md) (see Mechanics)

## Mechanics

**Anonymity:** anonymous until the committee knows the voice is [Hania Barnaś](../characters/jagna.md) (**Name her**), which turns her indifference vengeful.
**Willingness:** withholding, and opens only after she is known as Hania, then **Her brother** opens rescue calls or **Her family's killers** opens exposure calls ([The Report](the-report.md)).

## Exits

- Return to [The Flood](the-flood.md) with the ordinary rescue route closed.
- Continue to [The Report](the-report.md), if the players use the line to put the truth outside the valley.
- Follow the Edek route toward the [UPA bunker](../locations/upa-bunker.md), if the call turns on his survival.

## If Missed

- If the players never call out, the operator never has to refuse them directly.
- If the players beg without reaching her through Edek or retribution, the line remains formally open and practically useless.
- The flood eventually brings down a pole and ends the connection.
