## Available

The morning after [The Wolf Attack](wolf-attack.md), after [Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) authorizes Rezeń.

## Trigger

- Rezeń arrives at the PGR gate with three dogs.
- Rezeń enters the village for the first time in thirteen years.
- Rezeń starts tracking the wolves from the farm mud.

## Setup

- Rezeń handles the dogs through small hand signals and quiet sounds.
- Rezeń reads wolf sign before anyone gives him instructions.
- Rezeń keeps a knife in his hand and turns it idly.
- Rezeń is calmer in the forest than in the village.
- Rezeń's route drifts toward the deeper forest and the old village.

## theDogs

The dogs move as an extension of Rezeń's body.

## theKnife

Rezeń is not nervous; his hands want something sharp.

## theCalmHunter

Rezeń is most relaxed while tracking prey in the forest.

## followHimIntoTheForest

Rezeń finds scat, scrapes, wool on brush, likely denning ground, and passable routes without hesitation.

## stayWithHimIfHeMakesAKill

Rezeń carries the carcass deeper toward the [old village](../locations/old-village-ruins.md) and drops it into the well.

## Exits

- Return to the [PGR Farm](../locations/pgr-farm.md).
- Continue toward the [old village ruins](../locations/old-village-ruins.md).
- Trigger [The Hunters Cross Paths](hunters-cross-paths.md), if Rezeń's dogs disrupt Dudka's shot.

## If Missed

- Rezeń hunts alone and locates the den on high ground.
- If Rezeń kills a wolf, he puts the carcass in the well without witnesses.
