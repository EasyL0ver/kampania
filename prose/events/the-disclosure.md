## Header

**Present:** [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md), [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md), [Michał Pytlak](../characters/foreman.md), [Zofia Pytlak](../characters/zofia.md), [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md), [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md), [Marek Gajda](../characters/junior.md), [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md), villagers
**Available:** Day 4 onward, after proof that %NEW_VILLAGE% will flood exists and the truth breaks.

## Trigger

- The village learns %NEW_VILLAGE% will flood.
- The truth breaks by one of three routes.
- **Enlisted:** players convinced Zbigniew to reveal it through [Convince him to reveal the flood](../characters/wojewoda.md#convince-him-to-reveal-the-flood).
- **Forced:** players used [Force the disclosure — the ultimatum](../characters/wojewoda.md#force-the-disclosure--the-ultimatum).
- **Broken:** players announced it, the truth leaked, a villager panicked, [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) pieced it together, or someone overheard.
- Without flood proof, this event does not fire.

## Hook

- Voices gather in the rain.
- Doors open across %NEW_VILLAGE%.
- People call toward the sołtys office.
- The sound is audible from anywhere in %NEW_VILLAGE%.
- By the time players arrive, most of the village is present.

## Setup

- The scene happens in the muddy yard and square outside the sołtys office.
- **Form A:** Zbigniew called the gathering.
- In Form A, Zbigniew stands on the office step above the crowd.
- In Form A, he says %NEW_VILLAGE% will flood.
- In Form A, he does not say how long it was known.
- In Form A, he promises compensation, relocation, and state help.
- If enlisted, he names the players as the ones who found the danger in time.
- If forced, he does not name the players.
- In Form A, the village still listens to him.
- **Form B:** the crowd assembled itself.
- In Form B, Zbigniew is behind a shut door or forced into a yard that is turning on him.
- In Form B, the crowd accuses the state and the committee of lying.
- In Form B, [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) is loudest.
- In either form, the rising water gives the scene a time limit.
- [Stefania Kopacz](../characters/babcia.md) is not present; she stays in her house.

## Exits

- Toward evacuation through [Operator Refuses Help](operator-refuses-help.md) and army contact.
- Toward the engineering stand in [Foreman Saves the Village](foreman-saves-village.md).
- Toward the climax of [The Flood](the-flood.md) if the crowd breaks into panic.

## If Missed

- If players never surface the flood truth and it never leaks, there is no disclosure.
- The village treats the flood as weather and cooperates with the census.
- The village remains inside the lie until the water arrives.
- If the truth arrives too late, no orderly response forms.

## theCrowdFracture

[Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) wants someone to hit; [Marek](../characters/junior.md) sees a way out; [Zofia](../characters/zofia.md) is ready to leave; [Pytlak](../characters/foreman.md) wants to fight the water; [Barbara](../characters/barbara.md) is terrified; [Helena](../characters/matrona.md) follows Zbigniew's cue.

## zbigniewsSpentAuthority

`(requires: Finesse and Form A)` — he is spending authority he cannot replace. Public contradiction can break the gathering.

## theUnaskedQuestion

The crowd wants to know how long the danger was known. The honest answer can turn the gathering into a mob.

## thePriestsWeight

The crowd checks [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md). A word from him can bless evacuation or violence.

## backZbigniewsLine

**Requires:** Form A and players standing with Zbigniew.

Players reinforce his framing: there is a plan, the state will help, and people must move in order.

## tellTheWholeTruth

**Requires:** Being heard by the crowd.

A player says the state knew the danger and reassured the village anyway.

**Gives:** World State Change: the disclosure curdles toward panic and every-family-for-itself; NPC State Change: [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) becomes dangerous

## faceTheCrowdWithAPlan

**Requires:** Form B, or the truth has already broken in Form A; a credible evacuation route, army rescue, or place to send people.

Players take the anger and give the crowd logistics instead of blame.

## faceTheCrowdWithoutAPlan

**Requires:** Form B, or the truth has already broken in Form A; no credible route or deliverable promise.

Players admit the lie or redirect blame without offering safety.

## forceZbigniewToAnswerTheCrowd

**Requires:** Form B and players aim responsibility at the sołtys.

The crowd's question turns onto Zbigniew.

## calmItDown

**Requires:** A credible way out: evacuation plan, the [phone line to the army](operator-refuses-help.md), or somewhere people can go.

Players turn the gathering into evacuation logistics: who goes first, where people muster, and when they move.
