**Available:** Day 6 late afternoon, if at least one player has a [Bond](../characters/barbara.md#bond) with [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md).

## Trigger

- [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) has stopped talking and started preparing.
- The drinking circle at [Tadek Gajda](../characters/wujas.md)'s has turned purposeful.
- [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md)'s rifle is down from its pegs.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) has watched [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) from the other side of his fence.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) seeks the committee member who treated her like a person.

## Hook

- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) comes to the committee or catches a trusted player in a yard.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) has no cardigan.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) has mud to the shins.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) is out of breath from half-running.

## Setup

- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) keeps looking toward the fence line and [Ryszard Dudka's house](../locations/neighbours-house.md).
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) grips the trusted player's arm tightly.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) says [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) has been drinking.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) says men have been at [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md)'s place.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) says [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) took down, cleaned, and did not replace his rifle.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) says [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) spoke about someone finally answering for what they did.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) says [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) did not come to the fence tonight.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) does not know she has been passing the committee's information to [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md).
- **Composure:** 1.

## thePreparation

[Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) is describing preparations, not a mood.

## theVerdictPhrase

The phrase about someone answering for what they did sounds like a verdict, not grief.

## barbarasFear

[Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) is afraid for [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md), not afraid of him.

## getTheTimingOutOfHer

[Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) confirms the men gathered tonight, the rifle is gone, and [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) is not at the fence.

Gives: Scene Unlock: [the lynch](punishment-lynch.md) with advance warning before nightfall.

## sendHerHome

[Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) goes home, locks the door, and keeps [Pawełek Kopacz](../characters/pawelek.md) inside.

## Exits

- Go to [Ryszard Dudka's house](../locations/neighbours-house.md) before dark.
- Prepare for [the lynch](punishment-lynch.md).
- Keep [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) and [Pawełek Kopacz](../characters/pawelek.md) at [Barbara's house](../locations/barbaras-house.md).

## If Missed

- If no player has a [Bond](../characters/barbara.md#bond) with [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md), this event never happens.
- The first sign of [the lynch](punishment-lynch.md) is lamps on the road and dogs in the dark.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) stands with [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) when the mob forms.
