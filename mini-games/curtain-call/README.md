# Curtain Call — a 30-minute one-shot

A standalone murder mystery built on the **same engine** as the main campaign (no-failure GUMSHOE 1-2-1, time-as-cards, Opportunities vs Actions, atomic clues, stacked endings) but sharing none of its story. It is tuned to run in about **half an hour**.

## Premise

A repertory theatre, opening night, 7:30 PM. Thirty minutes before curtain, the leading man **Julian Frost** is found dead at his dressing-room mirror. Nine hundred people are filing into the house. The management wants tonight kept quiet — so you, the investigators, have until the curtain rises at **8:00** to find who killed him. After that, the cast scatters into the crowd and the truth goes cold.

## The players

1–3 investigators (house detectives, or discreet friends of the management). Each picks two skills from the engine's lists — the useful ones here are **Observation, Read, Medicine, Accounting, Streetwise, Sweettalk, Intimidate**. As always, skills never decide success — they decide what you *notice* and how much a question gives you.

## The clock — retuned for 30 minutes

The campaign's 7-day / 12-card economy is campaign tuning, not the engine. This one-shot keeps the engine and swaps the numbers:

- **One window: Pre-Curtain.** A single **5-card** budget for the whole party (or 3 cards each if you want independent players).
- **Everything costs 1 card:** a full interview, searching a room, a second trip to the body.
- **Free:** observing (Opportunities), quick follow-up questions mid-conversation, moving between the two backstage rooms.
- **Hard stop:** the curtain rises at 8:00. When the 5th card is spent — or the players choose to accuse — go to [Curtain](events/02-curtain.md).

There are more than five useful things to do. That gap **is** the game: you cannot see everything before the lights go down.

### What's dropped (and why)

Composure, Wounds, and Bonds are campaign systems that need time and repetition to matter. In a single 30-minute window they add nothing, so this one-shot omits them — a legitimate tuning choice, the same way the campaign omits things it doesn't need. The kept spine is **no-failure + time economy + Opportunities/Actions + clues + endings**.

## The solution (GM eyes)

- **Killer:** [Mara Voss](characters/mara.md), the stage manager. She has been skimming the production's money for years.
- **Motive:** Julian audited the books, found the leak ([`julian-found-the-skim`](clues/clues.md#julian-found-the-skim)), and told her he'd hand her to the board and the police after tonight ([`julian-threatened-mara`](clues/clues.md#julian-threatened-mara)).
- **Method & access:** She laced Julian's throat-spray ([`spray-is-poisoned`](clues/clues.md#spray-is-poisoned)) — the one item she alone maintains ([`spray-is-handled-by-stage-manager`](clues/clues.md#spray-is-handled-by-stage-manager)).
- **Red herrings:** [Danny Pike](characters/danny.md) gains the lead but dreaded it ([`danny-feared-the-lead`](clues/clues.md#danny-feared-the-lead)); [Vivienne Marsh](characters/vivienne.md) fought Julian loudly ([`director-fought-julian`](clues/clues.md#director-fought-julian)) but needed him alive to keep her backers ([`director-needed-julian-alive`](clues/clues.md#director-needed-julian-alive)).

To convict, players need **any two of**: the spray is poisoned, the spray is Mara's to maintain, Mara was skimming. See [Mara — Accuse her](characters/mara.md#actions).

## Endings

| Ending | How it's reached |
|---|---|
| **Name the Killer** | Accuse Mara with two of the three pillars before curtain. Justice, on the clock. |
| **Wrong Curtain** | Accuse Danny or Vivienne. An innocent is disgraced; Mara walks. |
| **The Show Goes On** | Curtain rises with no accusation. The case goes cold. |

## Running it in 30 minutes

1. Read the [The Body](events/01-the-body.md) setup aloud. Establish the 5-card clock.
2. Let players spend cards between the two rooms and three suspects. Deliver Opportunities free on each visit.
3. Track cards out loud — the pressure is the point.
4. On the 5th card (or an early accusation), cut to [Curtain](events/02-curtain.md) and deliver the ending they built.
