# The Body

**Location:** [Dressing Room 1](../locations/dressing-room.md)
**Present:** The body of Julian Frost. [Mara Voss](../characters/mara.md) is first on the scene, then withdraws to call the house.
**Available:** Opening scene — 7:30 PM, thirty minutes before curtain.

## Trigger

The dresser's scream. The investigators are already in the building (house detectives, or friends of the management asked to keep tonight quiet). They reach the star's dressing room first.

## Setup

Half past seven. The corridor is bright and too warm, the tannoy calling *"half an hour, ladies and gentlemen."* The dresser stands frozen in the doorway of Dressing Room 1. Past her shoulder: Julian Frost, folded over his mirror, a cravat in his fist and the bulbs blazing on a face that will not turn. Under the powder and spirit-gum there is a thin, bitter, almond smell.

Mara Voss appears at your elbow with her clipboard, takes it in, and says only: "The house is in at eight. Nine hundred seats. If we cancel, we cancel everything." Then, quieter: "So — what do you need, and how fast?"

That is the game. **You have until the curtain rises at 8:00.** Five things you can properly do, and more than five doors. Choose.

## Opportunities

- **The room** — Everything in [Dressing Room 1](../locations/dressing-room.md) is available now: the body, the atomizer, the face-down ledger. (See the location file.)
- **Mara's first words** — She frames the whole night as *keep the show running*, not *find who did this*. **Read:** an odd instinct from the first person to stand over a colleague's corpse. → Gives nothing alone; a flag.
