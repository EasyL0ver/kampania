# Curtain

**Location:** The wings, then the stage.
**Present:** The whole company; nine hundred people in the house.
**Available:** 8:00 PM — when the 5-card budget is spent, or the players call for the curtain to be held no longer.

## Trigger

The clock runs out. Either the investigators spend their fifth card, or they choose to make an accusation and end the night early. The tannoy calls *"places"* and the house lights dim.

## Setup

The tannoy stops calling numbers and calls the word every theatre lives and dies by: *"Places, please. Places."* Out front, nine hundred people settle and the lights go down. Backstage, the truth of tonight is whatever the investigators have managed to fix in place before this moment. The curtain will rise on one of three theatres.

## Actions

### Name the killer — correctly (Mara)
- **Requires:** The [Accuse her](../characters/mara.md#actions) action resolved against [Mara Voss](../characters/mara.md)
- **Cost:** Ends the game
- **Outcome:** Mara sets down her clipboard and the level voice finally goes. The poisoned spray was hers to fill; the stolen money was hers to hide; the threat that night was real. She is walked out through the stage door as the overture starts. The show goes on without her calling it, and everyone feels the gap.
- **Gives:** Ending: **Name the Killer** — justice done, on the clock.

### Name the wrong suspect (Danny or Vivienne)
- **Requires:** An accusation against anyone but Mara
- **Cost:** Ends the game
- **Outcome:** You point at the understudy in the dead man's clothes, or the director who screamed at him — the easy answers. They break down or rage; either way it's a wrong door, and while you push it, the calm woman with the clipboard calls "places" and slips out into the night. An innocent is disgraced. The killer walks.
- **Gives:** Ending: **Wrong Curtain** — the real killer escapes; an innocent carries the blame.

### Let the curtain rise unsolved
- **Requires:** The budget spent with no accusation
- **Cost:** Ends the game
- **Outcome:** The lights go down with nothing fixed in place. The play runs. By the interval the police have taken over, the cast has scattered to nine hundred alibis and one crowd, and the ledger has quietly gone missing from the dressing room. The case goes cold in the dark.
- **Gives:** Ending: **The Show Goes On** — the night swallows the truth.

## If Missed

There is no missing this. When the curtain rises, the game ends — the only question is which of the three theatres the players built.
