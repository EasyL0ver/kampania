# Clues — Curtain Call

Player-discoverable knowledge. Each clue is an atomic fact. Scene and character files link here when they can reveal it.

---

## The Death

### julian-was-poisoned
Julian Frost didn't collapse from a heart attack. Foam at the lips, a blue tinge to the skin, and a faint bitter-almond smell mean poison — he was killed.

### spray-is-poisoned
Julian's throat-spray atomizer on the dressing table is laced. He spritzed it before every entrance; one press tonight was enough. The bottle, not the man, is the murder weapon.

### spray-is-handled-by-stage-manager
Every actor's personal kit — including Julian's spray — is laid out and refilled before each performance by the stage manager, [Mara Voss](../characters/mara.md). Backstage, only she routinely handles it.

---

## Motive

### julian-found-the-skim
Julian discovered the production's petty-cash and prop-hire ledgers were short. Money had been walking out of the show for months.

### mara-was-skimming
[Mara Voss](../characters/mara.md) had been quietly taking that money. She keeps the books nobody else reads.

### julian-threatened-mara
An hour before curtain, Julian told Mara this was her last night — after the show he'd hand the ledgers to the board and the police.

---

## The Red Herrings

### danny-feared-the-lead
[Danny Pike](../characters/danny.md), the understudy, never wanted the part. He idolized Julian and is terrified to go on. He gains the role tonight but dreaded it.

### director-fought-julian
[Vivienne Marsh](../characters/vivienne.md) and Julian screamed at each other an hour before curtain — loud enough that half the wings heard it.

### director-needed-julian-alive
The show's backers are in tonight's audience. If Julian didn't perform, Vivienne lost the money and her name. She needed him breathing on that stage more than anyone.
