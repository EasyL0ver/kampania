# Mara Voss

**Type:** Named character — stage manager (the killer)

## Vital Statistics

- **Age:** 44
- **Lives in:** A rented flat two streets from the theatre — alone.
- **Settled:** Fifteen years running the backstage of this house. She knows every rope and every account.

## Character

The stage manager who makes every show run to the second — and who has been quietly draining its money for years. Julian found the leak and threatened to name her tonight. She controls the props, the kit, and the spray. Calm, precise, and cornered.

## Appearance

- **Clothes:** Black slacks, black roll-neck, headset around her neck, a clipboard never out of hand.
- **Hair & face:** Grey-streaked hair scraped into a tight knot; reading glasses pushed up; a face that gives nothing.
- **Carriage:** Economical, unhurried, always moving with purpose. The one still point in a panicking building.

She calls the show in a low, level voice through the tannoy. She is unfailingly polite and answers questions fast and flat — the calm is the tell. Press her on the money and the clipboard stops ticking.

## Opportunities

- **The steady hand** — While everyone else shakes, she keeps calling the time. **Read:** it isn't composure, it's control — she's managing the room, watching what you look at. → Gives nothing on its own (atmosphere), but flags her as worth pressing.

## Actions

### Ask about the kit and the spray
- **Requires:** Nothing
- **Cost:** Free (quick question in passing)
- **Outcome:** "Every actor's kit is mine to set. I lay it out, I refill it, I check it before the half. Julian's spray same as anyone's." She says it without thinking — then realizes what she's admitted.
- **Gives:** [`spray-is-handled-by-stage-manager`](../clues/clues.md#spray-is-handled-by-stage-manager)

### Press her on the money
- **Requires:** [`julian-found-the-skim`](../clues/clues.md#julian-found-the-skim)
- **Cost:** 1 card
- **Outcome:** She holds the flat line as long as she can. Shown the margin note in Julian's ledger, the mask cracks a hair. "He had no idea what it costs to keep this place alive. He was going to hand me to the board tonight like a thief."
  - **Read / Accounting:** she doesn't deny taking it — she justifies it. That's a confession of motive.
- **Gives:** [`mara-was-skimming`](../clues/clues.md#mara-was-skimming), [`julian-threatened-mara`](../clues/clues.md#julian-threatened-mara)

### Accuse her
- **Requires:** at least two of [`spray-is-poisoned`](../clues/clues.md#spray-is-poisoned), [`spray-is-handled-by-stage-manager`](../clues/clues.md#spray-is-handled-by-stage-manager), [`mara-was-skimming`](../clues/clues.md#mara-was-skimming)
- **Cost:** 1 card
- **Outcome:** Confronted with the poisoned spray she alone maintained and the money she alone took, the level voice finally breaks. "He'd have taken everything. The show, my name, all of it — over bookkeeping." She sets the clipboard down. It's over.
- **Gives:** Ending Progress: **Name the Killer** (see [Curtain](../events/02-curtain.md))
