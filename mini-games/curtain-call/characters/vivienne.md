# Vivienne Marsh

**Type:** Named character — director (red herring — the loud suspect)

## Vital Statistics

- **Age:** 51
- **Lives in:** A flat above a bookshop across town.
- **Settled:** Ten years directing at this house; this production is the one meant to save it.

## Character

The director who staked her name and her backers on tonight — and who was heard screaming at Julian an hour before he died. The fight makes her look guilty. But Julian alive on stage was the only thing that paid her debts; dead, he ruins her. She had every reason to keep him breathing and none to poison him.

## Appearance

- **Clothes:** A good coat worn over opening-night clothes, a scarf she keeps twisting.
- **Hair & face:** Sharp silver bob, strong jaw, eyes rimmed red from either crying or fury.
- **Carriage:** Restless, commanding even in panic — she directs a room by instinct, then remembers she can't direct her way out of this.

She speaks in clipped, theatrical bursts and hates being interrupted. Under the temper she is frightened in a way that has nothing to do with guilt and everything to do with the men sitting in row F.

## Opinions

- **[Julian Frost](julian.md)** — Impossible, vain, and the finest actor I'll ever direct. We fought because he threatened to walk. I needed him on that stage tonight. Alive. Do you understand? *Alive.*
- **[Mara Voss](mara.md)** — Runs my theatre better than I do. I don't look at the books; I don't have the stomach. That's her world.
- **`director-fought-julian`:** Yes, we fought. Half the wings heard it. He wanted more money and threatened the backers' night. I talked him down. He was going on.
- **`director-needed-julian-alive`:** The backers are in row F tonight. Without Julian's name they walk, and the theatre closes. Killing him would be killing myself.

## Opportunities

- **Eyes on the house** — She watches the auditorium doors, not the corridor. **Observation:** she's tracking specific seats — the backers' row — the way a woman watches the thing that can ruin her. → Gives nothing alone; press her below.

## Actions

### Ask about the fight
- **Requires:** Nothing
- **Cost:** Free
- **Outcome:** "You heard about that. Everyone did." She twists the scarf. "He wanted his fee doubled or he'd walk on opening night. I begged him. I did not raise a glass to him — I raised my voice." She gestures at the auditorium. "He was going on. I made sure of it."

### Press her on why she needed him
- **Requires:** [`director-fought-julian`](../clues/clues.md#director-fought-julian)
