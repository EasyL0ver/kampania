# Julian Frost

**Type:** Named character — leading man (the victim)

## Vital Statistics

- **Age:** 49
- **Lives in:** A townhouse he could no longer afford, near the park.
- **Settled:** The name that sells this theatre's tickets. Was.

## Character

The star of the show, found dead at his mirror thirty minutes before curtain. Vain, exacting, and — lately — dangerous to the wrong person: in auditing the production's money he found it was being stolen, and meant to name the thief tonight. He never made his entrance.

## Appearance

- **Clothes:** Half into his first-act costume, cravat unfinished in his dead hand.
- **Hair & face:** Leading-man handsome gone slack; stage powder, a bluish tinge under the greasepaint.
- **Carriage:** None now — folded over the dressing counter where he seized and fell.
