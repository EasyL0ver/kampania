# Danny Pike

**Type:** Named character — understudy (red herring — the obvious suspect)

## Vital Statistics

- **Age:** 26
- **Lives in:** A shared house with two other jobbing actors.
- **Settled:** Second season with the company, always in the wings, never on.

## Character

The understudy who just inherited the lead — and is falling apart because of it. On paper he has the clearest motive: Julian's death is Danny's big break. In truth he worshipped Julian, never wanted the part this way, and is terrified to walk on. The obvious answer, and the wrong one.

## Appearance

- **Clothes:** Half-laced into Julian's spare costume, collar undone, hands not steady enough to finish it.
- **Hair & face:** Young, open face gone pale; hair already sweated flat under where the wig will go.
- **Carriage:** Folded small on the sofa, knees together, a man trying to disappear from a room he's about to be shoved to the front of.

He talks in a rush when nervous and keeps apologizing. Ask him a hard question and he answers three you didn't ask.
