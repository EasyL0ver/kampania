# Dressing Room 1 (Julian's)

**Type:** Location (revisitable — the crime scene)
**Location:** End of the backstage corridor, star's dressing room, door ajar.
**Present:** The body of Julian Frost, slumped at the mirror. No living NPC lingers here.
**Available:** From the moment the body is found until curtain.
**Cost:** 1 card per visit

## Setup

A narrow room walled in mirrors and bulb-light. Julian Frost is folded forward over the counter, one arm hanging, a half-knotted cravat still in his fingers. The bulbs are hot; the room smells of powder, spirit gum, and — under it — something faintly bitter, like crushed almonds. Costumes hang ready. A half-drunk glass of water sweats on the counter beside a small brass atomizer, a call sheet, and an open ledger someone left face-down.

## Opportunities

- **The body** — He's clearly dead, mouth slack. **Read:** the posture is wrong for a heart attack — he seized, clawed at the counter. **Medicine:** foam at the lips, blue at the nails, the almond smell — this is poison, not a bad heart. → Gives: [`julian-was-poisoned`](../clues/clues.md#julian-was-poisoned)
- **The brass atomizer** — An actor's throat-spray, the kind sprayed before an entrance. **Observation:** the almond smell is strongest right at its nozzle. → Gives: [`spray-is-poisoned`](../clues/clues.md#spray-is-poisoned)
- **The face-down ledger** — Someone left an account book open and turned it over. Nobody keeps books in a star's dressing room. (Atmosphere — the read costs an action below.)

## Actions

### Examine the ledger
- **Requires:** Nothing
- **Cost:** Free (you're already standing over it)
- **Outcome:** Columns of prop-hire and petty-cash figures, and in Julian's own margin scrawl: *"short again — who?"* He was auditing the production's money and found it bleeding.
  - **Accounting / Observation:** the shortfalls are regular, monthly, hidden inside inflated prop-hire invoices — someone who controls the books.
- **Gives:** [`julian-found-the-skim`](../clues/clues.md#julian-found-the-skim)

### Search the counter and costumes
- **Requires:** Nothing
- **Cost:** 1 card
- **Outcome:** Under the call sheet, a refill bottle for the atomizer sits in a stage-management kit box stamped with the show's prop-department tag — the box only the stage manager carries.
- **Gives:** [`spray-is-handled-by-stage-manager`](../clues/clues.md#spray-is-handled-by-stage-manager)
