# The Green Room

**Type:** Location (revisitable — where the cast waits)
**Location:** Off the wings, opposite side from the dressing rooms. Sofas, a tea urn, the tannoy speaker crackling calls.
**Present:** [Danny Pike](../characters/danny.md) (half in costume, shaking), [Vivienne Marsh](../characters/vivienne.md) (pacing), [Mara Voss](../characters/mara.md) (in and out with her clipboard).
**Available:** Until curtain.
**Cost:** 1 card per visit

## Setup

A low room that smells of cold tea and hairspray. The tannoy on the wall hisses out the stage manager's calls — *"twenty minutes"* — and each one makes someone flinch. Danny Pike sits half-laced into Julian's spare costume, staring at his own hands. Vivienne Marsh wears a track in the carpet between the door and the window, watching the corridor. Mara Voss passes through on the quarter, ticking her clipboard, calm in a room that isn't. A poster of Julian smiles down from the wall.

## Opportunities

- **Danny in costume** — He's wearing the lead's clothes and looks like he might be sick in them. **Read:** this isn't a man who got what he wanted — it's dread. → Gives: [`danny-feared-the-lead`](../clues/clues.md#danny-feared-the-lead)
- **Vivienne at the window** — She keeps glancing not at the corridor but at the auditorium doors, and the house beyond. **Observation:** she's watching a specific row — the house seats where the money sits.
- **Mara's calm** — Everyone in the room is unravelling except the stage manager, who keeps calling the time as if it were any other night. (Atmosphere.)
