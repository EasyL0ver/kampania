# Clues — The Meridian Express

Player-discoverable knowledge. Each clue is an atomic fact. Scene and character files link here.

---

## The Death

### halloway-had-angina
Victor Halloway had a bad heart. He carried a small brown bottle of nitroglycerin tablets and kept it at his bedside every night; one under the tongue stops an angina attack. Anyone who travelled or dined with him knew this.

### died-reaching-for-pills
Halloway died half out of his bunk, one arm stretched toward the nightstand, the little brown bottle open and tipped over, three white tablets spilled on the floor. He knew he was having an attack and reached for the cure.

### pills-are-blanks
The tablets in Halloway's bottle are not medicine. They're inert — pressed chalk, sweet-less, they don't fizz or bite under the tongue the way real nitro does. Someone swapped his life-saving pills for fakes. This was murder, dressed as a bad heart.

### door-was-bolted
The corridor door of Halloway's compartment was bolted from the inside; the window was frozen shut. No one forced their way in that night. Whoever killed him never had to be in the room when he died — the pills were already switched.

## Motive

### halloway-was-blackmailer
Halloway was a newspaper man who collected people's secrets and squeezed them. He kept a slim black notebook of leverage — names, dates, prices. Three of the passengers on this train are in it.

### ashby-in-the-notebook
The notebook has an entry for Major Edmund Ashby: a battle, a ridge, a date in 1944, and the line "the truth about who ran." Halloway meant to print it. It would have destroyed Ashby's hero's name and his commission.

### delphine-in-the-notebook
The notebook has an entry for Delphine Marceau: her late husband, the "fallen hero" her whole legend rests on, was a deserter Halloway could prove it. Exposure would end her career and her pension.

### iris-in-the-notebook
The notebook has an entry for Iris Fenwick: Halloway had been quietly bleeding the wealthy widow for two years over an old indiscretion.

## Who Had The Chance

### ashby-knew-the-pills
Major Ashby knew exactly what the nitro tablets were and where Halloway kept them — he'd fetched the bottle for him once before during an attack, and handled it again at the card table that very evening.

### delphine-was-singing
Delphine Marceau was in the lounge car, singing for the stranded passengers, through the whole window when the bottle could have been switched. A dozen people watched her.

### iris-secured-a-settlement
Iris Fenwick had just forced Halloway to sign over a settlement that bought her freedom from him — signed and witnessed that afternoon. Dead before the ink cleared the lawyers, it might be worth nothing. She needed him alive a few more weeks, not dead tonight.

### porter-has-the-keys
The porter, Ferris, holds the master key to every compartment on the car. He's the only one who could unlock a bolted door — which is exactly why the obvious suspicion lands on him, and exactly why it's wrong: the killer never needed a key.
