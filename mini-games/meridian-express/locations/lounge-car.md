# The Lounge Car

**Type:** Location (revisitable — where the passengers wait)
**Location:** Mid-train, the observation lounge. Velvet seats, a cold bar, frost creeping up the windows.
**Present:** [Major Edmund Ashby](../characters/ashby.md), [Delphine Marceau](../characters/delphine.md), [Iris Fenwick](../characters/iris.md). Ferris the porter comes and goes.
**Available:** Until dawn.
**Cost:** 1 card per visit

## Setup

The one warm-ish room left on a dead train. Oil lamps, because the electricity went with the engine. Outside, a wall of avalanche snow buries the track and the dark, and the only sound is the wind and the tick of cooling metal. Three first-class passengers are marooned here in their evening clothes and their fear: a straight-backed army man nursing a brandy he doesn't drink; a striking older woman at the shuttered piano; a pale, expensively dressed widow twisting a wedding ring she no longer wears. They all know Halloway is dead in Compartment 7. None of them look surprised — only careful.

## Opportunities

- **The Major, too still** — Ashby sits at attention with a full glass, watching the door, not the room. **Read:** everyone else is rattled; he's rehearsing calm like a man who prepared for questions. → reveals he's worth pressing (no clue on its own).
- **The widow's ring** — Iris Fenwick keeps turning a ring she's taken off her finger. **Observation:** it's a nervous grief, not guilt — she looks like a woman who lost something, not one who won. → reveals she's worth hearing out.
- **The singer's poise** — Delphine is composed in the way of someone used to a stage, greeting your eye directly. (Atmosphere.)
