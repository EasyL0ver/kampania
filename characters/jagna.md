# Hania Barnaś

**Type:** Named character — deceased daughter of [Edward Barnaś](soldier.md)

## Vital Statistics

- **Born:** 1935
- **Died:** 1954 (age 19)
- **Lived in:** %NEW_VILLAGE% — with [Edward Barnaś](soldier.md) and family
- **Settled:** ~1948 — arrived with father

## Character

Missing since 1954 and presumed dead, though no one truly knows. She was proud, sharp, and observant; she knew the Gajdas' secret, never used it, and fled the lynch into the forest.

## Appearance

- **Clothes:** Better-kept than the village expected from the Barnaś house; plain skirts and blouses worn with deliberate neatness.
- **Hair & face:** Dark hair pinned or braided tight; bright, watchful eyes; a face that looked older when she was listening.
- **Carriage:** Quick, upright, and slightly defiant; she held eye contact too long for village comfort.

People remembered her as clever before they remembered her as kind. Around Edek she softened; around everyone else she looked ready to leave.
