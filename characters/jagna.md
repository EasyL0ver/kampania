## Character

Missing since 1954 — presumed dead, though no one truly knows. Edward Barnaś's eldest daughter, and cut from finer cloth than her father: proud, quick, certain she was cleverer than the valley that resented her family. A first-rate mind with nowhere to put it — no future, no power, nothing for a sharp girl to do in a poor mountain village but watch, listen, and notice what people would rather keep buried. She had a nerve for risk — the forbidden affair, the edge she liked to stand on, the feeling that danger was the one thing that made her more than nothing. But she was no schemer: what she noticed, she kept to herself. She knew the Gajdas' secret and never breathed it to a soul. Not cruel, not calculating — bright, hungry, and far too sure the valley couldn't touch her. It could. Attacked at the well in the 1954 lynch — killed, in every way that mattered, for a threat she never made; she broke free during the struggle and fled into the forest. Never seen again.

*GM only — what really lit the fuse:* Emil, in the trust of the affair, let slip the one thing his family kept buried — the Gajda siblings were secretly Lemko, passing as Poles. Hania knew. She was observant and she had it from Emil's own mouth — but she **never used it.** No threat, no price, no word to anyone. She was nineteen and in love, and the secret sat with her, harmless. When [Helena Rzepka](matrona.md) quietly asked her to give Emil up, Hania simply wouldn't — and that refusal was all it took. Helena looked at a rival who knew too much and *presumed* a blackmail that never came, deciding the girl was a danger that could never be out-waited. The "blackmail" is Helena's fabrication; in 1954 Hania was innocent of everything but loving the wrong man and knowing the wrong secret. That is what got her father killed and her little brother left with half a mind. **Emil never knew he was the leak** — and it changed nothing, since she never wielded it. She survived (see below); the girl who was murdered for a threat she never made is now the faceless [telephone-exchange operator](secondary-characters.md#operator-the-telephone-exchange-operator) who hears every secret the valley whispers down the wire. See [the sealed answer](../story-facts/the-lynch.md#open-questions).

## Opinions

- **[Edward Barnaś](soldier.md)** — Her father, and the person she most took after. Killed trying to save her — dead because of a game she chose to play.
- **[Edek Barnaś](glupek.md)** — Younger brother, four years old. The one person she was simply, uncomplicatedly gentle with. Survived — brain-damaged by the night that took the rest of her family.
- **[Emil Rzepka](painter.md)** — Her secret lover — Helena Rzepka's fiancé at the time. She wanted him, and the danger of him. He told her the family's secret never dreaming she'd use it. The affair that started everything.
- **[Tadek Gajda](wujas.md)** — Hopeless admirer. She felt nothing for him and never sent him away either — a man that devoted is useful. She let him hope.
- **[Helena Rzepka](matrona.md)** — The fiancée who asked her, politely, to step aside. Hania answered with a threat that could destroy the whole family — and made herself something Helena could only solve permanently.
