## Character

Missing since 1954 — presumed dead, though no one truly knows. Edward Barnaś's eldest daughter, and cut from the same cloth: proud, quick, certain she was cleverer than the valley that resented her family. A first-rate mind with nowhere to put it — no future, no power, nothing for a sharp girl to do in a poor mountain village but watch, listen, and collect what people would rather keep buried. So she made her own stakes. Risk was the one thing that made her feel like she wasn't nothing: the forbidden affair, the secrets she hoarded, the edge she liked to stand on. Not cruel — hungry, reckless, and far too sure of her own cleverness. Attacked at the well in the 1954 lynch; broke free during the struggle and fled into the forest. Never seen again.

*GM only — what really lit the fuse:* Emil, in the trust of the affair, told her the one thing his family kept buried — the Gajda siblings were secretly Lemko, passing as Poles. When [Helena Rzepka](matrona.md) quietly asked her to give Emil up, Hania didn't back down and didn't fight fair: she turned Emil's secret into a weapon and **blackmailed the family** — give me Emil, leave me be, or the whole village learns what you are and the state finishes what it started in '47. She was playing the most dangerous game she'd ever found and never respected that it could go off in her hands. It did — it got her father killed and her little brother strangled to half a mind. **Emil never knew she used what he gave her.** She survived (see below); the reckless girl who traded in other people's secrets is now the faceless [telephone-exchange operator](secondary-characters.md#operator-the-telephone-exchange-operator) who hears every one the valley whispers down the wire. See [the sealed answer](../story-facts/the-lynch.md#open-questions).

## Opinions

- **[Edward Barnaś](soldier.md)** — Her father, and the person she most took after. Killed trying to save her — dead because of a game she chose to play.
- **[Edek Barnaś](glupek.md)** — Younger brother, four years old. The one person she was simply, uncomplicatedly gentle with. Survived — brain-damaged by the night her recklessness brought down.
- **[Emil Rzepka](painter.md)** — Her secret lover — Helena Rzepka's fiancé at the time. She wanted him, and the danger of him. He told her the family's secret never dreaming she'd use it. The affair that started everything.
- **[Tadek Gajda](wujas.md)** — Hopeless admirer. She felt nothing for him and never sent him away either — a man that devoted is useful. She let him hope.
- **[Helena Rzepka](matrona.md)** — The fiancée who asked her, politely, to step aside. Hania answered with a threat that could destroy the whole family — and made herself something Helena could only solve permanently.
