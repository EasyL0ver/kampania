# Wanda Mazur

**Type:** Named character — grieving widow

## Vital Statistics

- **Born:** ~1912
- **Age in 1967:** ~55
- **Heritage:** Polish settler
- **Lives in:** Small house near the church — alone
- **Settled:** After 1954 — arrived with husband Tadeusz Mazur to work the PGR farm
- **Husband:** Tadeusz Mazur, dead ~1965 in a grain silo accident

## Character

Quiet, devout, harmless widow living on phantom wages from a dead man's PGR ledger entry. She does not know her husband's death was covered up.

## Appearance

- **Clothes:** Same brown wool coat regardless of season; faded floral headscarf tied tight under the chin.
- **Hair & face:** Thin grey hair under the scarf; deeply lined face, pale watery eyes slightly unfocused.
- **Carriage:** Small and thinning; moves as if afraid of disturbing someone.

Speaks barely above a whisper with a slight tremor. Smells of tallow candles and damp wool.

## Opinions

- **[ks. Władysław Pająk](priest.md)** — He is my lifeline. I come for God, but I also come because he asks how I am managing.
- **[Michał Pytlak](foreman.md)** — He was Tadeusz's boss, and I bear him no grudge. Accidents happen on farms, and I am grateful the pension still comes.
