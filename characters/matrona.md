**Type:** Named character — sibling (sister) / true architect of the lynch

- **Born:** 1922
- **Age in 1967:** 45
- **Heritage:** Lemko (hidden) — see [Zbigniew Gajda](wojewoda.md) for shared sibling background
- **Lives in:** [Helena Rzepka & Emil Rzepka's house](../locations/matronas-house.md) — with [Emil Rzepka](secondary-characters.md), [Ewa Rzepka](secondary-characters.md), [Krystian Rzepka](secondary-characters.md)
- **Settled:** ~1948 — first wave, with siblings

## Character

Devoutly religious on the surface, impenetrable beneath. The true architect of the lynch — sober, deliberate, never touched anyone. The hardest NPC to crack.

*GM only — why she did it:* Three things, none of them the excuse she gives. **Jealousy** — Emil was hers, and Hania wouldn't let go of him. **Hatred** — Hania was Edward Barnaś's daughter, and Edward had marched with the soldiers who slaughtered Helena's own people in 1947; killing the girl was a debt from '47 collected on the child. And **fear of a threat that was never real** — Hania knew the family was Lemko, and Helena *presumed* she would use it, though the girl never made a move, a threat, or a demand. Helena asked her to step aside; the girl simply wouldn't give up Emil, and that refusal was enough. The "blackmail" is Helena's fabrication, told to her brothers in 1954 and to anyone who corners her since. The morality-mob she aimed at Hania was cover; the girl she had killed was guilty of nothing but loving the wrong man and knowing the wrong secret. She does not know Emil was the one who leaked that secret in the first place — and it never mattered, because Hania never used it.

*GM only — how she carries it:* **She knows it was a sin. She knows the girl was innocent.** She is not a woman who tells herself she did right — that is Zbigniew. Helena believes she is damned and has chosen to stay damned, swallowing the guilt in silence for thirteen years to protect what the confession would cost: her children's name, Emil, the village order she holds upright. Daily Mass without fail is not a hypocrite's mask — it is a guilty woman who cannot stay away from an altar she will not kneel at. This makes her the pivotal, brutal node of the **[Grace ending](../story-facts/spiritual-endings.md)**: she is the one soul contrite enough to be absolved who *refuses absolution* — because contrition demands restitution, and restitution means letting the truth stand. Only the flood, by dissolving the order she damned herself to protect, can free her to confess the real sin.

*GM only — the forged paper:* Weeks after the lynch, with Marta already gone, the disappearance had to be made official. Helena had it done, but she didn't hold the pen — she gave it to **Emil**, whose painter's hand could copy Edward Barnaś's signature onto an administrative departure declaration handing the house to the PGR (see [the lynch](../story-facts/the-lynch.md), [departure-declaration-forged](../clues/clues.md#departure-declaration-forged), [Emil's file](painter.md)). It is the pattern of everything she does: she directs, someone else's hand acts, and she stays one step removed. The [PGR office](../locations/pgr-office.md) property records prove the paper is forged; they do **not** point to her. Any thread from the forgery leads to *Emil*, not Helena — pinning it back on her is left open, a hook for a later pass.

## Appearance

- **Clothes:** Immaculate dark clothes — cardigan buttoned to the throat, always an apron, small crucifix at her collar
- **Hair & face:** Dark hair pinned up tight beneath a headscarf, not a strand loose; thin lips in a permanent line, pale grey unblinking eyes
- **Carriage:** Tall, straight-backed, deliberate economy of movement — never hurries, never startles; hands always occupied with rosary, knitting, or wiping something already clean

People instinctively lower their voices in her presence. Voice low and measured — never raises it, never needs to.

## Opinions

- **[Emil Rzepka](secondary-characters.md)** — Her husband. Her insurance. A broken man who needs her more than he'd ever admit. She married him after the lynch despite what he did — not forgiveness, but ownership. He'll never leave, never talk, never recover enough to be a threat. That suits her.
- **[Zbigniew Gajda](wojewoda.md)** — Brother. He manages the village; she manages the story. They don't discuss it — haven't needed to in years. The understanding is older than words. She trusts his competence, not his nerve.
- **[Tadek Gajda](secondary-characters.md)** — Brother. A liability. Weak, drunk, guilt-ridden. If anyone cracks, it'll be Tadek. She watches him the way you watch a cracked dam.
- **[Janina Gajda](ciotka.md)** — Sister. The soft one. Janina's silence comes from love — protecting the boy. Helena's comes from will. They don't speak of it. Don't need to.
- **[ks. Władysław Pająk](priest.md)** — Her unwitting shield. Confessions are masterpieces of misdirection — real guilt wrapped so he sees a struggling soul, not a murderer. He will instinctively defend her. That's the point.
- **[Irena Gajda](wife.md)** — Quiet contempt. Knows her sister-in-law suspects something. Keeps her at arm's length with controlled warmth. If Irena became a real threat, she'd act — but so far, the woman is too powerless to worry about.
- **`ciotka-not-mother`:** Icy deflection. Turns it back on the players — why are you digging? A strange question for a census.
- **`wujas-is-guilty`:** Sighs with practised compassion. "My brother is a troubled soul. I pray for him every day." Dismissive concern — but her eyes sharpen, calculating how much he said and to whom.
