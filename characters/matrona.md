# Helena Rzepka (née Gajda)

**Type:** Named character — sibling (sister) / true architect of the lynch

## Vital Statistics

- **Born:** 1922
- **Age in 1967:** 45
- **Heritage:** Lemko (hidden) — see [Zbigniew Gajda](wojewoda.md) for shared sibling background
- **Lives in:** [Helena Rzepka & Emil Rzepka's house](../locations/matronas-house.md) — with [Emil Rzepka](secondary-characters.md), [Ewa Rzepka](secondary-characters.md), [Krystian Rzepka](secondary-characters.md)
- **Settled:** ~1948 — first wave, with siblings

## Character

Devout in public and impenetrable in private. She is the sober architect of the 1954 lynch, the hardest sibling to crack, and the one most able to turn guilt into order.

## Appearance

- **Clothes:** Immaculate dark clothes — cardigan buttoned to the throat, always an apron, small crucifix at her collar.
- **Hair & face:** Dark hair pinned tight beneath a headscarf; thin lips, pale grey unblinking eyes.
- **Carriage:** Tall, straight-backed, and economical; hands busy with rosary, knitting, or wiping something already clean.

People lower their voices around her. Her own voice stays low and measured, so others lean in instead of asking her to speak up.

## Mechanics

### Grace ending node

Helena knows Hania was innocent and knows the lynch was a sin. She can be absolved only if she lets the truth stand, because contrition demands restitution; that makes her pivotal to the [Grace ending](../story-facts/spiritual-endings.md).

### Forged paper thread

Weeks after the lynch, Helena directed **Emil** to copy Edward Barnaś's signature onto the administrative departure declaration that sent the house to the PGR. The [PGR office](../locations/pgr-office.md) property records prove the paper is forged and point first to Emil, not Helena; see [the lynch](../story-facts/the-lynch.md), [departure-declaration-forged](../clues/clues.md#departure-declaration-forged), and [Emil's file](painter.md).
