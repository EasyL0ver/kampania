# Janina Gajda

**Type:** Named character — sibling (sister)

## Vital Statistics

- **Born:** 1912
- **Age in 1967:** 55
- **Heritage:** Lemko descent, hidden
- **Lives in:** [Janina Gajda's house](../locations/ciotkas-house.md) — with [Edek Barnaś](glupek.md)
- **Settled:** ~1948, first wave with siblings

## Character

Devout, fearful, and honest when her guard drops. She is the moral heart of the 1954 tragedy: the person who saved [Edek Barnaś](glupek.md) and has lived with the cost ever since.

## Appearance

- **Clothes:** Shapeless dark dresses buttoned to the throat; headscarf outdoors
- **Hair & face:** Thin grey hair hidden under the scarf; gaunt cheeks, permanent dark circles
- **Carriage:** Shoulders hunched, eyes down, flinches at raised voices; steadier at home with [Edek Barnaś](glupek.md)
- **Voice:** Thin and breathy in public; silence when frightened

At home she moves around Edek with practiced care. In public she makes herself smaller and lets other people fill the space.
