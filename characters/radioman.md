## Character

The village drunk — but he was once its schoolteacher, until the state took the post and gave him a few years inside for "agitation." Now he drinks Tadek's bimber, listens to Radio Wolna Europa through the jamming, and tells anyone who'll listen that the authorities are lying to them. He is right about more than the village credits — and drunk enough that no one believes a word.

To the players he is **openly hostile from the first word** — they are a government committee, the same state that broke him, walked into his village wearing its face. He heckles, he insults, he baits them to prove him right. But he is fearless and he is loud: he'll fling what he knows about the sołtys and the bezpieka *at* them as an accusation. The truth is on offer; his cooperation is not — not until they show him they aren't the sołtys's creatures.

## Appearance

- **Clothes:** A grey suit jacket gone shiny at the elbows — the last relic of the teacher he was — worn over peasant trousers and a collarless shirt; nothing fits, nothing is clean
- **Hair & face:** Grey stubble he shaves twice a week; wire spectacles mended with a twist of copper wire; broken veins across the nose; eyes still sharp when the drink hasn't dulled them
- **Carriage:** Stands too straight when he's making a point — the old lectern habit — then folds again; hands that tremble until the first glass steadies them

Talks in a teacher's cadence, still reaches for the clever phrase, half-remembers a Latin tag, quotes a broadcast he heard through static and swears it verbatim. Reeks of bimber and cold ash. In a room he is either the loudest man in it or asleep in the corner — rarely anything between.

## Mechanics

### Signal in the noise

He broadcasts constantly and mixes truth with rubbish. The GM should keep the ratio deliberate — most of what he says is froth, a little of it is gold, and he cannot tell the difference himself. Players must sift.

**True (and safely contained):**
- The sołtys reports privately to the man in the black car → [`wojewoda-talks-to-sb`](../clues/clues.md#wojewoda-talks-to-sb)
- Tadek's crew runs a still out in the forest → [`drinking-crew-heads-to-forest`](../clues/clues.md#drinking-crew-heads-to-forest)
- The man in the black car is secret police — he **asserts** it, correctly, but from his mouth it's only another claim; it becomes [`officer-is-sb`](../clues/clues.md#officer-is-sb) only when corroborated elsewhere

**Garbage (pure noise):**
- Radio Wolna Europa said Gomułka is finished / the Americans are coming / the border is about to open
- The priest is a Soviet plant; the census men are foreign spies; whatever the drink invents tonight

He knows **nothing** of the old village, the massacre, or the well. His quarrel is with the living state, not its buried crimes — chase his conspiracies and you burn time on graft and rumour, never on 1947.

## Opportunities

- **He's mid-tirade** — Whoever comes near catches him holding forth about the government, the radio, the man in the black car. Atmosphere; most of it is drink talking.
- **The suit jacket, the phrasing** — **Bureaucracy / History:** you clock what he was before the bottle — an educated man, a teacher, someone the state broke on purpose. Treating him as that man, not as the village sot, opens him up.

## Actions

### Hear him out over a bottle
- **Requires:** Nothing (a [Bond](#bond) makes him precise instead of ranting)
- **Cost:** 1 card
- **Outcome:** You sit through the froth — the broadcasts, the prophecies, the paranoia. Under it, one claim never changes and never slurs: the sołtys meets the man in the black car alone, every single time he comes, and comes back changed.
  - **Streetwise / Reassurance, or Bond:** he drops the performance and gets specific — where Gajda walks, that he goes out to the car by himself, that it's been years.
- **Gives:** [`wojewoda-talks-to-sb`](../clues/clues.md#wojewoda-talks-to-sb)

### Ask where the good bimber comes from
- **Requires:** Nothing
- **Cost:** Free
- **Outcome:** He lights up — the one subject that isn't politics. He'll happily point you toward Tadek's crew and the treeline, no coaxing needed.
- **Gives:** [`drinking-crew-heads-to-forest`](../clues/clues.md#drinking-crew-heads-to-forest)

## Bond

- [ ] Hear out a full tirade without mocking him or walking off

- [ ] Take the sołtys's or the authorities' side in front of him
- [ ] Repeat what he told you to someone who could report it — expose him as a Wolna Europa listener
