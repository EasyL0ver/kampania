## Trigger

- [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) remains in the village after the wolf job.
- [@name](../characters/wojewoda.md) authorized him to hunt wolves through [@name](../characters/wujas.md).
- [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) treats the request as permission to reintegrate.

## Hook

- Players near the village centre see [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) walking toward [@surname](../characters/butcher.md).
- His pace is controlled and direct.

## Setup

- [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) confronts [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) in public.
- [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) orders him to go home.
- [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) does not comply.
- [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) smokes and handles his knife.
- [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) watches [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) without deference.
- [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) repeats the order.
- [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) stays.
- [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) leaves first.
- Villagers who see it understand that [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) failed to move him.
