# The Lynch

**Type:** Event — endgame catastrophe
**Location:** Two beats — the yard before the [PGR office](../locations/pgr-office.md) in %NEW_VILLAGE%, then [the well](../locations/the-well.md) in %OLD_VILLAGE%
**Present:** [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) at the front, rifle in hand; men from [Tadek's](../characters/wujas.md) drinking circle, drunk and purposeful; [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) (**his state runs Part 1** — see [the fork](#part-1--the-pgr-office)); [Barbara](../characters/barbara.md) **only if she was never warned** ([see below](#barbara)); a target set by [Dudka's targeting score](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets)
**Available:** **Night of Day 6.** This is not optional and it is not preventable in full. The valley's reflex fires. **Someone goes into the well tonight.** What the players decide is *who* — and what the killing is wrapped in.

## Trigger

Thirteen years of listening through the wall and doing nothing. The flood, the well, the dreams, and now a fresh body already taken to the water — it turns [Dudka's](../characters/neighbour.md) guilt into something he can no longer lie still under, and it comes out as a rifle, not a confession. He gathers the men who are already angry and the valley does what it did in 1954.

And because it is 1954 running again, it starts where 1954 started: at the sołtys's door. In this valley the well only ever took a body when [Zbigniew](../characters/wojewoda.md) walked it there. The mob comes to the centre first — to the office, for his word — before it goes to the water.

This is the opening blow of the endgame, not its close. The body drops tonight; the [village's reckoning with its dead](the-ritual.md) and its [reckoning with the flood](the-flood.md) both play out *after*, in the morning that follows, in the shadow of who the well just took.

## Hook

For a day beforehand the drinking circle gets louder and turns from miserable to purposeful. If a player [was warned by Barbara](barbara-warns-the-players.md), they saw this coming and have the evening to prepare. If not: one night, with no warning — lamps moving on the road, boots on gravel, dogs going off down the valley, [Dudka's](../characters/neighbour.md) rifle gone from its pegs, all of it converging on the yard in front of the office. Perceivable anywhere in %NEW_VILLAGE% after dark.

**Composure: 2**

## Part 1 — The PGR Office

The mob doesn't march straight to the water. It comes to the centre first, to the office, because in this valley the well only ever took a body when the sołtys walked it there — and in 1954 he did. They've come for the same thing they got then: his word. To bless it, or to lead it.

What they get was decided days ago, at [Irena's door](irena-confronts-wojewoda.md) — whether she reached him or not (see [her Phase 2](../characters/wife.md#mechanics)). This is where the players read which Zbigniew the village has tonight.

### If Zbigniew is braced

Irena out-argued his certainty. The office is dark, the door shut, and it stays shut. He is inside, awake, listening — and he does *nothing*, because she taught him the one thing his pride had hidden: a crowd with no head burns itself out on whoever is nearest, and the man who walks into the front of it is the nearest. So he doesn't walk into it. He lets them have their night. The crowd, finding no one to bless it and no door to break, mills and curses and turns toward the well to do the thing itself — leaderless, drunk, [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) driving. → He follows only *after*, once the body is down: see [Part 2](#part-2--the-well) and [Aftermath](#aftermath). The brace saved the man. It did not save whoever the well takes.

### If Zbigniew is breaking

She never reached him — or the players interrupted [her confrontation](irena-confronts-wojewoda.md) before she could land it. He comes out. Onto the steps, into the lamplight, upright and certain — the 1954 man, sure the village is his and will fall in behind him the way it did then. He tries to take the crowd: to own it, aim it, or send it home.

**He fails. He always fails.** Thirteen years of authority is worthless against a crowd the well has curdled into wanting a body, and his certainty — the last thing he has left — only marks him as a man who thinks he still gives the orders. **What the mob does with him then is not his to decide. It is [Dudka's](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets).**

- **If Dudka is [Humiliated](../characters/neighbour.md#humiliated) or has already [acted at the well](well-confrontation.md):** the crowd turns on the sołtys. The man who ran the valley becomes a body it can reach — **Zbigniew enters the [targeting](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets) as a live candidate for the well, high.**
- **Otherwise:** the crowd shoulders past him. He is swept aside on his own steps and follows the mob to the well stripped of every ounce of authority — alive, and no longer the sołtys in any way that matters.

Either way he does not die *for* talking to them and he does not save anyone by it. His stand only decides whether he goes to the well as a leader who failed or as a name on the list.

## Opportunities (Part 1)

- **The office** — dark and locked, or the man on the steps. **Read:** which Zbigniew the village has tonight — the one who knew to vanish, or the one who still thinks he commands. This is the tell of whether [Irena reached him](irena-confronts-wojewoda.md), and it tells the players which endgame they're standing in.

## Actions (Part 1 — only if Zbigniew is breaking and out on the steps)

### Back him
- **Requires:** a player lends the sołtys a second voice — stand at his shoulder, echo the order to go home
- **Cost:** 1 composure
- **Outcome:** Two authorities are not one authority. He still fails — he always fails — but a crowd that sees the outsiders siding with the sołtys hesitates a beat longer, and that beat can matter at the water. **This does not save him.** It cools the mob by a notch (GM: treat as one step down on [Dudka's drive](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets) — the crowd is more turnable at the well).
- **World State Change:** the mob is a touch more turnable in Part 2; if Zbigniew lives, he remembers who stood with him.

### Feed him to them
- **Requires:** [`wife-protects-husband`](../clues/clues.md#wife-protects-husband) — or other proof Zbigniew was one of the killers — thrown to the crowd now, on his own steps
- **Cost:** —
- **Outcome:** You hand a mob that came for a body the name of the man who used to send it home. The 1954 debt lands on the one who ran 1954. **Zbigniew enters the [targeting](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets) high — often as *the* target — regardless of Dudka's state.** This is the players choosing the scapegoat, and it is the well eating the superego itself: whatever Zbigniew was containing has no one left to hold it after tonight.
- **World State Change:** the aim swings toward Zbigniew; the leash on [Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) is cut in the same stroke.

## Part 2 — The Well

The crowd moves off down the road toward %OLD_VILLAGE% and the well at the end of it — the same darkness, the same drink, the same water as 1954. No phone, no road out, no witness who'll talk. [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) at the front, rifle across him, quiet and white-faced (he goes quieter, not louder, when it's real). Someone is being dragged along or barricaded against — the target.

Dudka's state colours the walk:
- **If he acted at [the well](well-confrontation.md) already** (held Rezeń at rifle-point): harder and more certain — he's crossed into *doing* once, and this is the next step.
- **If [Humiliated](../characters/neighbour.md#humiliated):** colder, past words, out to prove he isn't nothing. Hardest to turn.
- **If a player holds his [bond](../characters/neighbour.md#bond):** he clocks that player in the lamplight — the one voice that can still reach him.

### Barbara

- **If Barbara was [warned and sent home](barbara-warns-the-players.md):** she is not here. The mob is drunk men and a rifle — breakable.
- **If no player ever bonded with her:** **she is standing at Dudka's shoulder.** Not chanting, not pointing — miserable, arms crossed, eyes down, but *present.* She won't cross the man who protected her for the people who never protected her. Her presence gives the mob what drink can't: **legitimacy.** With Barbara there, "you're just drunk, go home" doesn't work — the kindest soul in the valley is standing with them, and everyone can see it. Turning the mob is far harder while she stays.

## Opportunities (Part 2)

- **The front of the mob** — **Observation:** a handful of men, not the village; most are drunk followers who'd fold if the nerve went out of them. Only Dudka is driving it — unless [Barbara](#barbara) is there lending weight.
- **The target** — **Read / Milicjant:** whether they've got a guilty man (Rezeń, a sibling, a broken Zbigniew) or the wrong one (Marek, Barbara, the hag, a player) — and that to the mob it makes no difference.
- **Dudka's face** — **Read:** not bloodlust — a man who cannot stand his own inaction one more night. He isn't doing this because he's sure. He's doing it because doing *nothing* is finally worse.
- **The script** — **History / Culture:** this is 1954 running again, beat for beat — same drink, same dark, same well. The valley is not choosing. It is repeating.

## Actions (Part 2 — the well)

**The killing cannot be called off — only aimed, or absorbed.** Every action below changes *who* goes into the well and what the death means. None of them sends everyone home.

### Braced Zbigniew arrives (GM beat, not a player action)
- **When:** the body is already in the well — the kill is done, the crowd blown out and slack
- **What happens:** the sołtys walks into the ruins alone, unhurried, out of the dark. Not to stop the lynch — he timed it not to. He stops what comes *after.* A few flat words — *go home, it's finished* — and the crowd that a rifle drove here folds without one, because the head it went looking for at the office has finally appeared to claim it. He sends them down the road, sees the body is his village's secret and not its scandal, and by the walk back he is the sołtys again as if he never wasn't. **This only happens if Zbigniew is [braced](#if-zbigniew-is-braced).** If he was breaking, no one arrives; the mob disperses on its own, ragged, with no one in charge of the morning.
- **World State Change:** the lynch is sealed under Zbigniew's authority; the village folds back under him (see [Aftermath](#aftermath)).

### Turn the aim onto a perpetrator
- **Requires:** proof that points at one of the guilty (Rezeń, a sibling, Helena, a breaking Zbigniew)
- **Cost:** —
- **Outcome:** Hand the crowd the name it half-wants and it carries that name to the well. **This feels like justice and is 1954 again** — a guilty person killed by a drunk mob in the dark, the dead still unnamed, the reflex proven eternal. If the truth was only half-out, you may be wrong about who's guilty.
- **World State Change:** the lynch completes on a perpetrator. Colours the [morning after](#aftermath) as grim vindication.

### Turn the aim off an innocent
- **Requires:** the current target ([score](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets)) is the wrong person, and you have a *different* name with weight behind it
- **Cost:** 1 composure — you're arguing a drunk crowd off a kill
- **Outcome:** You pull the mob off the innocent it was fixed on by giving it someone else. You have saved one life by choosing another. The well takes a body regardless; you decided which.
- **World State Change:** the target shifts. You own the choice.

### Put yourself between them and the target
- **Requires:** a player physically steps in front of the rifle
- **Cost:** grave — **the mob may take the player instead** ([players are a valid target](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets); a score high enough, or a drunk crowd denied its kill, can turn on the one blocking it)
- **Outcome:** You can save the target with your body. Whether the crowd backs down or **puts the player in the well instead** depends on Dudka's state, the player's standing, and whether [Barbara](#barbara) is there to stop it going that far. This is how a player dies tonight.
- **World State Change:** target saved; a player may be killed in their place.

### Convince Dudka justice will be delivered
- **Requires:** you hold [his bond](../characters/neighbour.md#bond) **or** he isn't [Humiliated](../characters/neighbour.md#humiliated) **or** you've [Uplifted him](../characters/neighbour.md#uplift-ryszard) — **and** you can *prove* the guilty will answer: the truth documented, word already down the wire, an authority that will act on it
- **Cost:** 1 composure — stepping in front of a loaded rifle
- **Outcome:** Dudka isn't after blood. He's after the certainty that these men will *finally answer* — because in thirteen years no one ever made them. He lynches only because he believes the alternative is nothing. **Show him the alternative is real — that justice is already moving and will land — and he stands down.** He lowers the rifle and says what he's carried for thirteen years, out loud, to the committee, on the record. The truth gets out.

  **And then the mob he raised takes him.** Drunk, denied its target, and looking at the man who just broke ranks and sided with the outsiders — the crowd turns. The redeemer becomes the body. **[Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) goes into the well tonight, in the target's place** — the coward who did nothing for thirteen years, killed the one time he did the right thing. His testimony survives him; he does not.
- **Requires (to even attempt):** the justice structure built *before* tonight, not improvised at the well. Without real proof that the guilty will answer, the words bounce off and the rifle stays up.
- **World State Change:** the intended target lives; the truth is on the record ([toward Justice](../story-facts/game-system.md#endings)); **Dudka is dead.** This is the narrow exit — and it still costs a life. Just his.

### Let it run
- **Requires:** —
- **Cost:** —
- **Outcome:** You don't step in. The mob goes to the well and the [targeting score](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets) decides who. See [Aftermath](#aftermath).

## Aftermath

Unless [Dudka was convinced justice is coming](#convince-dudka-justice-will-be-delivered), **the lynch completes and a body goes into the well.** Who, is decided by [Dudka's targeting score](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets) — the person the players' own leaks (mostly through [Barbara's fence](../characters/barbara.md)) made look guiltiest, plus whatever the players did on the office steps in [Part 1](#part-1--the-pgr-office).

- **A perpetrator** (Rezeń, a sibling, Helena): the village calls it justice; it is the same crime, thirteen years on.
- **The wrong person** (Marek, Barbara, the hag): the committee's carelessness put an innocent in the well.
- **A player:** the outsiders who came to judge became the guiltiest-looking thing in the valley — or one of them stepped in front of the rifle.
- **[Dudka himself](#convince-dudka-justice-will-be-delivered):** if the players convinced him justice was coming, he stood down, testified — and his own mob killed him for it. The truth is on the record; the coward died the one time he did right.

**Zbigniew, after:**
- **If he was braced:** he never came out at the office, and he is untouched. Once the body is down he walks into the ruins alone and disperses the spent crowd on the spot — a few flat words, men sent down the road, the killing sealed as the village's secret. He let the well feed to keep his throne, and it worked. He rules the village that killed tonight. This is the darkest Forget reading of him: the murderer who kept order by feeding the order a victim.
- **If he was breaking and the crowd turned on him:** the sołtys is the body in the well. The village wakes with no head at all — and nothing holding [Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md).
- **If he was breaking and shouldered aside:** he lives, but the authority is gone. A man who walked out to command a mob and got walked around. He is no longer the sołtys in any way the village will honour, and everyone saw it.

Afterward [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) is hollowed out, not relieved — he has become the thing he spent thirteen years hating. The well takes another body, and [what that does](../story-facts/well-influence.md) follows.

**And then it is morning, and the endgame is not over.** The lynch is the wound the last day is lived inside. Whoever is left faces both reckonings at once — the [dead](the-ritual.md) and the [flood](the-flood.md) — one day, too few hands, and a fresh body in the well changing the weight of every choice. If the lynch killed a player, the party enters that final day short-handed. What the mob took, it took from the living who still have to choose.

## Exits

- **To the final day** — the simultaneous reckoning with the dead (the [rite](the-ritual.md), grace, or the cover-up) and the living (the [flood](the-flood.md): escape, fight, or drown).
