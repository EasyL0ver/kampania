# The Punishment Ending — Ryszard Dudka's Lynch

**Type:** Event — ending, late game
**Location:** %NEW_VILLAGE% — the road and yards after dark, ending at [the well](../locations/the-well.md) in %OLD_VILLAGE%
**Present:** [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) at the front, rifle in hand; a handful of men from [Tadek](../characters/wujas.md)'s drinking circle, drunk and purposeful; a target — whoever [Dudka's targeting score](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets) has landed on
**Available:** Late game, after dark — the truth is partially out but no justice structure is in place (no call out of the valley, no documented confession, no one in authority answering for it). Comes **sooner and harder if [Dudka is Humiliated](../characters/neighbour.md#humiliated)** or the [well confrontation](well-confrontation.md) went badly.

## Trigger

Thirteen years of listening through the wall and doing nothing. The flood, the well, the dreams turn that guilt into something he can't lie still under — and it doesn't come out as confession, it comes out as rage. **Ryszard Dudka takes his rifle, gathers the men who are already angry, and the valley does what it did in 1954.**

Who he points at isn't random: it's whoever the leaked clues have made look guiltiest ([Vigilante Targeting](../characters/neighbour.md#vigilante-targeting) → the running [Lynch Targets](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets) score — Rezeń by default, but the siblings, the wrong people, or even the players, wherever the evidence pointed). If he's [Humiliated](../characters/neighbour.md#humiliated), the threshold is lower and he is past reasoning.

## Hook

For days beforehand the drinking circle at [Tadek](../characters/wujas.md)'s and the store gets louder, its tone shifting from miserable to purposeful. Then one night: lamps moving on the road, boots on gravel, dogs going off down the valley, and Dudka's rifle gone from its pegs. Perceivable anywhere in %NEW_VILLAGE% after dark.

## Setup

A knot of men with lamps and bottles, no longer slumped — moving with intent. Dudka at the front, rifle across him, quiet and white-faced (he goes quieter, not louder, when it's real). Someone is already being dragged along, or barricaded against — the target. The valley has the muscle memory: the same darkness, the same drink, the same well waiting at the end of the road. No phone, no road out, no witness who'll talk.

His state colours the night:
- **If he acted at the well** (held Rezeń at rifle-point): harder and more certain now — he already crossed into *doing something*, and this is the next step.
- **If [Humiliated](../characters/neighbour.md#humiliated):** colder, past words, looking to prove he isn't nothing. Hardest to stop.
- **If a player holds his [bond](../characters/neighbour.md#bond):** he clocks that player in the crowd — the one voice that can still reach him.

## Opportunities

- **The front of the mob** — **Observation:** it's a handful of men, not the village; most are drunk followers who'd fold if the nerve went out of them. Only Dudka is driving it.
- **The target** — **Read / Milicjant:** whether they've got a guilty man (Rezeń, a sibling) or the wrong one (Marek, Barbara, the hag) — and that to the mob it makes no difference.
- **Dudka's face** — **Read:** this isn't bloodlust, it's a man who cannot stand his own inaction one more night. Take that away and the whole thing collapses.
- **The script** — **History / Culture:** this is 1954 running again, beat for beat — same drink, same dark, same well.

## Actions

Stopping the lynch is the line between the [Punishment ending](#outcome) and the [Justice ending](../story-facts/game-system.md#endings). Each action below knocks out one leg the lynch stands on; enough of them and it never happens.

### Talk Dudka down
- **Requires:** you hold [Dudka's bond](../characters/neighbour.md#bond), **or** he isn't [Humiliated](../characters/neighbour.md#humiliated), **or** you've [Uplifted him](../characters/neighbour.md#uplift-ryszard) — plus a way for him to *act* that isn't killing (testify, put it on the record)
- **Cost:** Free, but you're stepping in front of a loaded rifle and a drunk crowd — 1 composure
- **Outcome:** You give him the thing he actually wants — to stop being the man who did nothing — without a body. He lowers the rifle; without its head the mob loses its nerve. **World State Change:** the lynch is called off and Dudka testifies instead (toward [Justice](../story-facts/game-system.md#endings)).
  - **If Humiliated and you hold no bond and haven't restored his face:** words bounce off him. [Uplift him](../characters/neighbour.md#uplift-ryszard) first, or knock out another leg below.

### Break the mob's nerve
- **Requires:** Committee authority; **Delegate** or **Intimidate** enriches
- **Cost:** 1 composure
- **Outcome:** You turn on the drunk followers rather than Dudka — name them, invoke the state, the phone, the district, what happens to men who lynch under a government commission's nose. **Delegate:** you scatter them clean. **Intimidate:** you frighten them off. Either way the crowd peels away and Dudka can't do it alone. **World State Change:** the mob dissolves (toward Justice).

### Show justice is already coming
- **Requires:** you built the [Justice chain](../story-facts/game-system.md#endings) earlier — cracked witnesses, documented the truth, got word out of the valley by telephone
- **Cost:** Free
- **Outcome:** You tell them the truth is already on paper and already gone down the wire — the district knows, and they are coming. The well can't bury what Warsaw already has, so the lynch has no purpose. **World State Change:** defuses cleanly (Justice).

### Zbigniew takes responsibility
- **Requires:** you reached [Zbigniew](../characters/wojewoda.md) before tonight and he's willing to own it publicly
- **Cost:** Free (his to pay)
- **Outcome:** The sołtys steps into the lamplight and takes it on himself. Structure reasserts — a village doesn't lynch while its own authority is standing there answering for it. **World State Change:** the mob disperses (Justice).

### Feed the mob a target
- **Requires:** proof that points at someone
- **Cost:** —
- **Outcome:** **This does not stop the lynch — it aims it.** Hand the crowd a name and they carry it to the well. If it's a guilty man, you've chosen blood over law; if the truth was only half-out, you've helped kill the wrong person. **World State Change:** the lynch completes on your target (Punishment). Use only if you know that is what you're doing.

### Let it run
- **Requires:** —
- **Cost:** —
- **Outcome:** You don't step in. The mob goes to the well — see [Outcome](#outcome).

## Outcome

If no leg is knocked out, the lynch completes. **Who goes into the well is decided by [Dudka's targeting score](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets)** — the person the leaked evidence made look guiltiest. A body in the well, in the dark, with drink and no witnesses: 1954 repeats exactly. **This is the Punishment ending.**

- Target was a **perpetrator** (Rezeń, a sibling, Helena): the village calls it justice, and it isn't — it's the same crime again, thirteen years on.
- Target was the **wrong person** (Marek, Barbara, the hag) or a **player**: the committee's failure put an innocent in the well.

Afterward [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) is hollowed out, not relieved — he has become the thing he spent thirteen years hating. The well takes another body, and [what that does](../story-facts/well-influence.md) follows.

## If Missed

This event fires *because* the players failed to build a justice structure. If they completed the [Justice chain](../story-facts/game-system.md#endings) — witnesses cracked, truth documented, a call made out of the valley, and Dudka kept stable — the mob never forms and this event simply never triggers.
