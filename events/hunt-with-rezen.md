# The Hunt with Rezeń

**Location:** [PGR Farm](../locations/pgr-farm.md) → forest
**Present:** [Stanisław Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) (three dogs), players (optional)
**Available:** The morning after [The Wolf Attack](wolf-attack.md), once [Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) has authorized Rezeń.

## Setup

[Stanisław Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) shows up at the PGR gate with his three dogs — not at the forest edge where he belongs, but among workers, among people, first time in thirteen years. He doesn't explain himself. *"I'll find them."* He's already reading the tracks in the mud before anyone answers. The dogs are calm and disciplined; he moves them with a finger, a chin-tilt, a hiss through his teeth.

He was told to kill wolves. He heard *we need you.*

## Opportunities

- **The dogs** — trained to an extension of his body, moving as a unit off small signals. **Read:** This man has done this hundreds of times.
- **The knife** — always in his hand. Not drawn — turning, the way another man fidgets with a pen. **Observation / Milicjant:** Not nerves. His hands need to hold something sharp.
- **The man himself** — **Read:** the tension that radiates off him in the village is gone out here. He's relaxed, present, at home. The village's most dangerous man is calmest when he's hunting. It's deeply wrong to watch.

## Actions

### Follow him into the forest
- **Requires:** Nothing
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** He doesn't search — he **navigates**. Which path leads where, which stream crossing is passable, where the ground holds. He finds wolf sign fast — scat, a scrape, sheep wool on brush — and reads it without effort. If asked, he's short and accurate and unsettling: *"Two, maybe three. They'll den up during the day. Come back tonight."* He drifts toward the deeper forest — the direction of the [old village](../locations/old-village-ruins.md) — not on purpose, just because that's where the woods run. Players who know what's out there will clock the heading.
- **Gives:** [`rezen-hunts-wolves`](../clues/clues.md#rezen-hunts-wolves), [`butcher-knows-forest-intimately`](../clues/clues.md#butcher-knows-forest-intimately)

### Stay with him if he makes a kill
- **Requires:** Followed the hunt to a killed wolf
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** He doesn't carry it back to the farm. He takes the carcass deeper — toward the [old village](../locations/old-village-ruins.md) — and drops it into the well. No ceremony, no hesitation, the same motion he uses for everything. The well gets fed. If the players don't follow, he comes back empty-handed: *"The forest takes care of its own."*
- **Gives:** [`butcher-compelled-to-feed`](../clues/clues.md#butcher-compelled-to-feed), [`butcher-visits-the-well`](../clues/clues.md#butcher-visits-the-well)

## Exits

- Back to the [PGR Farm](../locations/pgr-farm.md)
- Deeper toward the [old village ruins](../locations/old-village-ruins.md), if the players push past where the hunt leads

## If Missed

Rezeń hunts alone. He locates the den on high ground but is in no hurry to finish — the hunt is his excuse to stay in the village. If a wolf dies, it goes in the well whether anyone sees it or not.
