# The Family Takes the House

**Location:** [Ciotka's house](../locations/ciotkas-house.md)
**Present:** [Janina Gajda](../characters/ciotka.md) (dead), [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md), [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md), [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md), [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md)
**Available:** Shortly after [Ciotka Found Dead](ciotka-found-dead.md), when the priest returns with the family; Day 3 or later.

## Trigger

- The committee is still at [Ciotka's house](../locations/ciotkas-house.md) when [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md) returns with [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md).
- Fires whether or not the attic was opened.
- Committee already gone: see If Missed.

## Hook

- Voices and footsteps come up the path to the house.
- [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) and [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md) arrive with [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md).
- [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) comes to the fence from the next yard.
- Perceivable from inside the house or the yard.

## Setup

- The priest steps back and says little from here.
- [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) takes charge of the house as family and sołtys.
- [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md) composes the body and starts arranging the church and funeral.
- [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) stands at the fence, not crossing into the yard.
- The wrecked corner, burned-out candles, and open back door are as the committee found them.
- If the attic was opened, [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) sees it, calls the contents his sister's stored junk, and closes it.
- [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md) presses [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md): old, frail, her heart gave out, God's will; find the boy and bring him home.
- [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) does not believe it but holds his tongue, as he did in 1954.

## Opportunities

- **Who decides** `(requires: Empathy)` — Helena leads, Zbigniew follows her.
- **Dudka at the fence** `(requires: nothing)` — the quiet neighbour is holding back anger and will not enter the yard with the family in it.
- **The overdose they are burying** `(requires: holding [`ciotka-overdosed`](../clues/clues.md#ciotka-overdosed) or [`ciotka-hurt-before-death`](../clues/clues.md#ciotka-hurt-before-death))` — Helena's weak heart and Dudka's foul play are both wrong; only the committee knows what killed her.

## Actions

### Say what does not fit
- **Requires:** present; the committee raises any of [`ciotka-house-wrecked`](../clues/clues.md#ciotka-house-wrecked), [`ciotka-hurt-before-death`](../clues/clues.md#ciotka-hurt-before-death), or [`ciotka-had-a-visitor`](../clues/clues.md#ciotka-had-a-visitor) in front of the family
- **Outcome:** Naming the wreck, the bruise, or the second cup breaks [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md). He accuses the sołtys's son to his face: the boy came the day before she died and they fought. [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) goes still; [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md) buries it as a grieving man's nonsense. The ruling does not change.
- **Gives:** [`junior-pressed-ciotka`](../clues/clues.md#junior-pressed-ciotka); counts toward [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md)'s [Bond](../characters/neighbour.md#bond); NPC State Change: [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) sets a [Grudge](../characters/wojewoda.md#grudge); World State Change: foul-play rumours sharpen.

## Mechanics

- Fixed outcome. [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) rules the death natural and backs [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md). [Marek Gajda](../characters/junior.md) is never questioned.
- No search for the boy; the flood is the real crisis. The family asks the committee to look for [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) and bring him home if they find him. Framed as care, not a manhunt.
- The body is moved to [the church](../locations/the-church.md). The family takes the house; the attic, if opened, is closed.
- [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) leaves angry. The smashed house and sudden death set foul-play rumours moving whether or not he spoke.
- Confronted with the body or house evidence, [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md) has a ready answer for each; see her [Opinions](../characters/matrona.md#opinions).

## Exits

- [Funeral Mass](funeral-mass.md), Day 5.
- Look for [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) in the forest.
- Revisit [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) at [his house](../locations/neighbours-house.md).

## If Missed

- Committee already gone: the siblings settle it. Death ruled natural, body to [the church](../locations/the-church.md), house and attic pass to the family unseen.
- The committee hears the result and the rumours second-hand.
- [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md)'s account stays available at [his house](../locations/neighbours-house.md).
