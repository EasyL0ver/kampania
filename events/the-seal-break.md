# The Seal-Break — The Priest Names the Dead

**Location:** [The church](../locations/the-church.md) — Day 7, the water reaching the door
**Present:** [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md) (drunk, broken), and whoever the [lynch](punishment-lynch.md) left alive: [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md), [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) (if he survived Day-6 night), [Tadek](../characters/wujas.md), [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md), [Emil](../characters/painter.md), the whole village. [Stanisław Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) only if the mob did not take him.
**Available:** Day 7. Fires when the [Faith in Redemption](../story-facts/spiritual-endings.md) score is **below** its threshold at the end of Day 6 — his faith lost the judgment-vs-mercy fight. If the score cleared, the day goes to [The Odpust](the-odpust.md) instead.

## Trigger

No one offered him the merciful reading, or the guilty never knelt, or the players pushed him to weaponise the confessional, or the valley lynched a man on Day-6 night and proved to him it is still 1954. His faith loses. He does not conclude the valley can be saved — he concludes it is **damned**, that God is drowning it exactly as He drowned the world, and that he has spent thirteen years keeping the secret of *why*. And a man who has decided he is going down with the valley has no more reason to keep it.

So he does the one unforgivable thing left to him. He gets drunk enough to break the seal.

## Hook

The bell — rung wrong, too long, a man hauling on the rope past any rhythm, and then it just stops mid-strike. Audible across %NEW_VILLAGE%. The village comes not knowing what it means, only that the sound was wrong.

## Setup

The church is packed, the black water already under the door and spreading over the flagstones. The floor candles have drowned, the light dying from the edges inward. And their priest is **sitting on the altar table** — the one surface in the valley no layman may touch — a bottle of bimber in his hand, his collar off and hanging over the tabernacle, his vestments half-undone. He is drunk. Before he says a word, every soul in the church knows something has broken that does not mend.

Then, calm and slurring, unhurried, he begins to talk. Not the Mass. He tells them he has decided to stop lying. He says the water is judgment — that this valley was built over a grave and God has come to collect — and that he is done carrying the reason for it alone.

And he starts naming them.

One at a time, in the soft baritone that has heard every confession in this valley for thirteen years, he says what each of them did. The night at the old well in 1954. Who held the girl. Who brought the rifle. Whose hands were on the soldier. Who carried the body to the water. What was done to the child. He names Zbigniew. He names Tadek. He names Rezeń. He grieves Janina, who told him all of it and begged him every year to let her say it aloud. He does **not** name Helena — her thirteen years of masterful confessions kept even this from him; the one who planned it is the one name the drunk on the altar cannot say, because he never knew it. The congregation cannot stop him, because every word is true, and a drunk man telling the truth is the one kind of preacher no one can pretend is only preaching.

**Composure: 2** — the collapse of the last man holding the line, breaking the most sacred vow he has, to make sure the whole valley drowns knowing exactly why.

## Opportunities

- **The man on the altar** — drunk, collar off, sitting where no one may sit.
  - **Read:** This is not ecstasy and it is not madness. He needed to be this drunk to do it. He knows precisely what breaking the seal costs him — it is among the gravest things a priest can do — and he is doing it anyway, on purpose, with his eyes open.
  - **Culture:** A priest violating the seal of confession is, in his own faith, forfeiting his soul. He is not saving anyone tonight. He is choosing to be damned alongside them so the truth does not drown unspoken.

- **The names, as they land** — the guilty in the pews as their crime is read.
  - **Read:** Zbigniew goes still and calculating even now, looking for the exit. Tadek weeps openly, saying *Hania* into his hands. Helena braces to hear her own name — and it never comes. The architect is passed over, her misdirection holding even through the seal-break. Whether that lands as reprieve or as a fresh, private damnation is hers to play. Each face is a confession the words don't need.

- **The valley built on a grave** — where his account turns from 1954 back to the ground it stands on.
  - **History / Culture:** He does not stop at the lynch. He reaches under it — the old village, the people who were here before, the ones the state said were *resettled*. The blood the whole valley was built over. This is the deeper thing the flood is answering.

## Actions

### Let him finish
- **Requires:** Nothing
- **Cost:** —
- **Outcome:** He names all of it, to the last act, and falls silent, spent, the bottle still in his hand and the water at the altar steps. The whole truth of 1954 — and the shape of 1947 beneath it — is now spoken aloud, in public, in front of the entire surviving village. It cannot be un-said.
  - **Gives:** the full lynch account as spoken testimony — the sealed clue [`priest-knows-everything`](../clues/clues.md#priest-knows-everything) detonated in the open.
  - **World State Change:** the guilty drown **unabsolved** — no odpust follows a priest who has damned himself. Grace is foreclosed.

### Carry the testimony out (arm the report)
- **Requires:** A player present who survives the flood, with the [ledgers](../story-facts/the-committee.md) or the means to file
- **Cost:** —
- **Outcome:** A player who hears the seal-break and lives carries it into the [report](../story-facts/the-committee.md). The priest's naming is public testimony that the valley's "resettled" and "departed" dead are dead *here* — it corroborates the Property ledger's field 3 wholesale, the entire cover-up surfaced at once instead of dug parcel by parcel. This is the exposure path the whole judgment fork was for: the truth outlives the valley on paper the state cannot un-write.
  - **World State Change:** the report is armed with the full account; whatever the players choose to file, they file with the truth in hand.

### Stop him / pull him down
- **Requires:** A player physically intervenes — drags him off the altar, silences him
- **Cost:** 1 composure
- **Outcome:** A guilty NPC will bless the player for it; the rest of the village will never forget that an outsider silenced the truth at the end of the world. You can stop him — but he has usually said enough before you reach him that the names are already in the air. You cannot make the church un-hear what it has heard.
  - **NPC State Change:** whichever guilty souls remain owe the player; the honest villagers turn cold.
  - **World State Change:** partial — what he said before being stopped still stands as testimony.

## Exits

- **Into the flood** — the seal-break is the last thing that happens in the church. After it, the valley faces the water (escape, fight, or drown — see [the flood](the-flood.md)) with the truth spoken and no absolution given.
- **To [The Odpust](the-odpust.md)** — foreclosed. His faith lost; there is no mercy coming from this altar.
- **To [Rest](the-ritual.md)** — still open. The seal-break does not name the dead *to* the dead; the well rite remains the only thing that gives them peace.
