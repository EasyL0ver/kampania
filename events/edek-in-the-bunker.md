# Edek in the Bunker

**Location:** [UPA Bunker](../locations/upa-bunker.md)
**Present:** [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) (after [Janina Gajda](../characters/ciotka.md)'s death)
**Available:** After [Ciotka Found Dead](ciotka-found-dead/ciotka-found-dead.md), when players follow the Edek route to the bunker.

## Trigger

- [Janina Gajda](../characters/ciotka.md) is dead and [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) has fled ([`edek-ran-to-upa-bunker`](../clues/clues.md#edek-ran-to-upa-bunker)).
- Players track the footprints into the forest ([`glupek-fled-into-forest`](../clues/clues.md#glupek-fled-into-forest)) or already know the bunker.
- Players reach the [UPA bunker](../locations/upa-bunker.md).

## Hook

- From the bunker mouth: movement in the dark, heavy breathing, a large shape pressed back into the earth.
