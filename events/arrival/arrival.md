## Hook

A car stopping on the muddy road as Zbigniew Gajda comes forward to meet the committee.

## Setup

- The car stops on the muddy road in %NEW_VILLAGE%.
- [Zbigniew Gajda](../../characters/wojewoda.md) is waiting for the committee.
- [Zbigniew Gajda](../../characters/wojewoda.md) gives a firm handshake and walks the committee toward his house.
- Men from [Tadek Gajda](../../characters/wujas.md)'s crew drink in daytime along the route.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../../characters/barbara.md) and [Pawełek Kopacz](../../characters/pawelek.md) are visible along the route.
- [Barbara Kopacz](../../characters/barbara.md)'s house sits at the edge of the village, beside [Ryszard Dudka](../../characters/neighbour.md)'s.
- The village [store](../../locations/the-store.md) is visible along the route, men from the crew loitering by its steps.
- The church on the hill is unusually well maintained — fresh repairs, a sound roof, firewood stacked high.
- Dogs bark toward the forest.
