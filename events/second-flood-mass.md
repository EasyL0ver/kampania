## Hook

- Church bells call the village to Mass in the morning rain.
- Villagers gather because the flood has trapped them and fear is rising.

## Setup

- [@title](../characters/priest.md) [@surname](../characters/priest.md) preaches on Habakkuk 2:11-12.
- The sermon says a town built on blood becomes its own witness.[](#sermon-blames-a-bloody-settlement)
- He says stones and beams cry out against a guilty foundation.
- He is no longer speaking only about 1954.
- [@name](../characters/wujas.md) sits at the back, as he does at every Mass.[](#wujas-attends-every-mass)
- He points toward the old village, the buried dead, and the valley's deeper debt.
- The village hears the flood framed as reckoning.[](#parish-seeks-a-body-to-blame)
- This sermon fuels the mob that forms that night.
