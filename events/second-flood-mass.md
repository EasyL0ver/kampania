# Second Flood Mass — Against the Water

**Location:** [The church](../locations/the-church.md)
**Present:** [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md) (fraying), [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md) (front pew), [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) (back), [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md), most of the village. [Tadek](../characters/wujas.md) — likely, and drunk. [Stefania Kopacz](../characters/babcia.md) is **not** here — she's stopped coming.
**Available:** Day 6 morning. The fourth day of flood. ks. Pająk calls a second mass because the first one didn't work — the water is still rising.

## Trigger

The rain has not stopped. Day 4's prayers changed nothing. Fear has curdled into something that needs a target, and the flood is pressing on every guilty conscience in the valley at once. If [Rezeń took the body](rezen-takes-the-body.md) in the night, the villagers arrive to a church with an empty coffin at the front — and a name already in their mouths.

## Hook

The bell again — but wrong. The ringer is rushing it, strikes crowding each other, no rhythm. Audible across %NEW_VILLAGE% through the rain. Two masses in three days. Everyone knows what that means: the priest is frightened too.

## Setup

The church is fuller than Day 4 and meaner for it. Men who came once out of fear now come out of dread, and dread has started looking for a face to pin itself on. Coats soaked through, water tracked up the aisle, the flagstones slick. The organ is still dead. The hymns start ragged and stay ragged — half the village singing, half just moving their lips and watching each other.

ks. Pająk is not the man from Day 4. The vestments are the same; the certainty is gone. He rushes the Latin, then loses his place and stops, then starts again too loud. His hands shake when he lifts the chalice.

**If the coffin was taken in the night:** [Janina](../characters/ciotka.md)'s closed pine coffin should be at the front where it was left after her [funeral](funeral-mass.md). It isn't. The trestles are there. The black cloth is there, dragged half off. The coffin is gone. The village files in, sees it, and instead of grief the room fills with something uglier — a low, hunting murmur, everyone already deciding who did it.

The mood is the thing to watch. These are people who've been good neighbours for years. This morning they aren't. They snipe at each other in the pews, shush children too hard, turn cold eyes on the committee — outsiders who showed up right before everything went wrong. The flood is squeezing the valley and what's coming out is rot.

**Composure: 1**

## Opportunities

- **The empty trestles** *(if the body was taken)* — the cloth half-dragged to the floor, the coffin simply gone.
  - **Observation:** A wet drag-mark leads from the trestles down the aisle to the door. Nobody carried her out. She was hauled.
  - **Read:** Zbigniew sees it the moment he enters and his face shuts like a door. He already knows something the room doesn't.
  - **Gives:** → [ciotka-body-taken](../clues/clues.md#ciotka-body-taken)

- **The priest coming apart** — rushing, losing his place, too loud then too soft.
  - **Read:** This isn't grief and it isn't the flood. The man has stopped believing his own words and is saying them anyway, faster, as if speed will hide it.
  - **Culture:** At the intercessions he pauses again — longer than Day 4. His lips move once on a name that is not a Roman saint before he catches himself. Something old is surfacing through the Latin.

- **The village turning** — sniping, cold looks, a congregation that came to be comforted and stayed to blame.
  - **Read:** Fear alone doesn't do this. There's a nastiness moving through the room, drawing out the meanest version of every person in it — old grudges, old cruelties, close to the skin now.
  - **Streetwise:** They need a culprit and they're auditioning candidates: Rezeń first, but also the hag, the boy who ran, and — glances toward the pews — the strangers who arrived with clipboards. An accusation would catch here like dry straw.

- **The name in the pews** — muttering under the hymn, heads turning.
  - **Streetwise:** Tadek's graveside cry has spread. *Rezeń* moves pew to pew. With the coffin gone it has a target now — a dead woman stolen, and a monster everyone already feared.

- **Tadek, back wall** — drunk before noon, swaying, staring at the empty trestles.
  - **Read:** He's past guilt into something else. He looks at the missing coffin like he expected it. Like the valley is finally doing what he always knew it would.

## Actions

### Ask about the missing coffin
- **Requires:** Noticed the empty trestles
- **Cost:** Free
- **Outcome depends on who you ask:**
  - **ks. Pająk:** He didn't know until he walked in. Genuine horror — he crosses himself and can't finish the gesture. *"Who would... in God's house..."* He has no answer and it terrifies him more than the flood.
  - **Helena:** Tight, fast, containing. *"It's the water. The ground shifted, someone moved her to higher ground, that's all."* She does not believe it and needs the players to.
  - **Zbigniew:** *"I'll deal with it."* Flat. He wants the room cleared and the players elsewhere.
  - **Dudka:** *"Rezeń."* One word, no doubt. His hand is already looking for a rifle that isn't there.
- **Gives:** → [ciotka-body-taken](../clues/clues.md#ciotka-body-taken)

### Follow the drag-mark
- **Requires:** Noticed the wet trail from the trestles
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** The trail runs out the church door and toward [%OLD_VILLAGE%](../locations/old-village-ruins.md) — the same furrows described in [Rezeń Takes the Body](rezen-takes-the-body.md). Following it leads to the well.
- **Gives:** → [ciotka-body-taken](../clues/clues.md#ciotka-body-taken)

### Cool the room
- **Requires:** The congregation is sliding toward naming a culprit
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Skills:** **Law**, **Streetwise**, **Sweettalk**
- **Outcome:** Step on the muttering before it becomes a decision — remind them there's no proof, redirect the grief, give the fear somewhere else to go. Success cools it for now. Failure, or standing aside, and the blame sets like concrete a full day early.
  - **NPC State Change:** Success delays the [Punishment](punishment-lynch.md) mob. Failure advances it toward Day 7.

### Steady the priest after mass
- **Requires:** Nothing
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** He lets the players walk him to the sacristy. He's shaking. He says the prayers aren't landing — *"I say the words and they fall on the floor."* He doesn't confess anything, but the crack from Day 4 is a fissure now. A kind or faithful approach gets further into what he's carrying.
  - See [priest character file](../characters/priest.md) for the full interaction tree.
- **NPC State Change:** The priest is closer to breaking — and closer to the point where he will consider a remedy that isn't his own. *(Grace: this is his Day-6 "fraying" stage. Whether he breaks toward mercy (the odpust) or judgment (collapse) is set by the "Faith in Redemption" score — backing his faith and bringing the guilty to confess pushes toward mercy. See [spiritual-endings.md](../story-facts/spiritual-endings.md).)*

## If Missed

The village faces the empty coffin without the committee, and no one cools it. Blame hardens into fact: Rezeń stole her. The mob timeline advances toward the [Punishment](punishment-lynch.md) ending whether the players are there or not. ks. Pająk, unsteadied, spirals alone toward Day 7.
