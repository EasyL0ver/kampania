# Rezeń Takes the Body

**Location:** [The church](../locations/the-church.md) (the body), then [the well](../locations/the-well.md)
**Present:** [Stanisław Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) — alone, with his dogs
**Available:** Night of Day 5 into Day 6 morning. **Requires:** the [flood](the-flood.md) has postponed Janina's [burial](funeral-mass.md#the-flood-postpones-burial) (body left in the church), **and** Rezeń is alive and loose — not jailed, not killed, not yet dead at the well.

## Trigger

The Day 5 [funeral](funeral-mass.md) could not finish — the flood made burial impossible, so [Janina Gajda](../characters/ciotka.md)'s coffin was left in the church. That night, with a body above ground and no one guarding it, the well's pull takes Rezeń to the church. He carries her out and puts her in the well.

If the players chose to **keep vigil over the coffin**, this becomes a live confrontation instead of a next-morning discovery.

## Hook

Reaches anyone near the village centre or awake at night:

- The dogs. Rezeń's dogs, out in the dark, moving — not barking, just the wet patter of paws and the occasional whine, tracking from the direction of the church toward the forest.
- Under the rain: a slow, dragging sound. Something heavy pulled over a wet threshold and mud.
- The church door standing open, rain blowing across the flagstones.

## Setup

**If discovered Day 6 morning:** The church door is ajar, rain pooling inside. The closed pine coffin at the front is open — lid shifted, empty. A candle knocked over. Across the flagstones and out the door, a **wet drag trail** — heel-furrows in the mud — leads toward [%OLD_VILLAGE%](../locations/old-village-ruins.md) and the well. Dog prints alongside it. The village is not up yet. Whoever finds this finds it first.

**If players kept vigil (Night 5):** Rain hammering the roof. Candles guttering. Some time past midnight the door opens and Rezeń steps in, soaked, calm, dogs at the threshold. He doesn't rush. He looks at the coffin the way a man looks at a job that needs doing. If no one stops him, he lifts her without ceremony and starts for the door.

**Composure: 1**

## Opportunities

- **The drag trail** — heel-furrows and dog prints out the church door toward the well.
  - **Observation / Endurance:** Fresh — last few hours. One person dragging a body, not carrying it far; the load was set down and hauled. Straight line toward [%OLD_VILLAGE%](../locations/old-village-ruins.md). Easy to follow while the rain hasn't washed it out.
  - **Gives:** → [ciotka-body-taken](../clues/clues.md#ciotka-body-taken)

- **The empty coffin** — lid shifted, the body gone, the church otherwise undisturbed.
  - **Read:** No struggle, no theft of anything else, no vandalism. Whoever did this came for the body and nothing else. Not desecration for its own sake — a purpose.

- **The dogs** — if present, circling between the church and the forest path, agitated, unwilling to settle.
  - **Observation:** These are Rezeń's dogs. Everyone knows them.

- **Rezeń's calm** — in the vigil version, or later when confronted. No guilt, no evasion, no heat.
  - **Read:** He is steadier than he's been in days. Whatever he did, doing it settled something in him. The craving is quiet — for now.

## Actions

### Keep vigil over the body (Night 5)
- **Requires:** Players chose to stay with the coffin overnight (offered by the [postponed burial](funeral-mass.md#the-flood-postpones-burial))
- **Cost:** A night — no rest, exhaustion the next day
- **Outcome:** Past midnight, Rezeń comes for her. He's not expecting company. Caught, he doesn't fight and doesn't flee — he stops, considers the players, and explains himself plainly (see **Confront Rezeń** below). The body stays in the church. He walks out into the rain, unbothered.
  - **Intimidate / Law:** He can be made to leave and stay gone that night, but not made to feel wrong. He shrugs it off like a man told to move his cart.
  - **Prevents:** [rezen-fed-ciotka-to-well](../clues/clues.md#rezen-fed-ciotka-to-well) does not happen — the body is never put in the well.
- **Gives:** → [butcher-compelled-to-feed](../clues/clues.md#butcher-compelled-to-feed)

### Follow the drag trail (Day 6)
- **Requires:** Found the empty coffin and the trail
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** The furrows run to the well in [%OLD_VILLAGE%](../locations/old-village-ruins.md). At the rim: mud churned, her shawl or a shoe caught on the stone, the water below risen and dark. She's in the well now — with the 1947 dead and with [Edward Barnaś](../characters/soldier.md), the man she saved [Edek](../characters/glupek.md) from in 1954. If Rezeń's tracks are read, they lead back to [his house](../locations/butchers-house.md).
  - **Gives:** → [ciotka-body-taken](../clues/clues.md#ciotka-body-taken)

### Confront Rezeń
- **Requires:** Caught him at the church, or tracked the body to him
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Skills:** **Intimidate**, **Read**
- **Outcome:** He doesn't deny it. He tells it flat, like a man explaining a chore: *"Nobody was going to put her in the ground. Water's up, the yard's a swamp, she's been lying in that box for days. She was starting to turn — you could smell it. Somebody had to see to it. So I saw to it."* Where he put her he says the same way — the well is simply where things go. He does not say, and does not seem to know, that something pulled him there. Pressed harder, he offers nothing more; there is nothing more, in his mind, to offer.
  - **Read:** He believes every word of the practical reason. He is not lying and he is not ashamed. But the reason is a coat thrown over the compulsion — it *had* to be the well, and he can't tell you why. He's calmer for having done it. That calm is the tell.
  - **Gives:** → [rezen-fed-ciotka-to-well](../clues/clues.md#rezen-fed-ciotka-to-well)
- **NPC State Change:** If the village learns of this — through the players or the empty coffin — the false suspicion of Rezeń from the [funeral](funeral-mass.md#tadeks-outburst) hardens into certainty. It becomes fuel for the mob.
- **Ending Progress:** Feeds the Punishment / mob-justice ending against Rezeń. Also adds Janina to the well's unmourned dead — a body the endgame [ritual](the-ritual.md) must now also account for.

## If Missed

If the players don't guard the body and don't find the trail before the village stirs, the coffin is discovered empty by others — likely Helena or the priest. The village does not reach for the supernatural. It reaches for Rezeń. Tadek's graveside accusation becomes gospel; [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) and others move from grief to a hunt. Whether or not anyone can prove it, Rezeń is now the man who stole a dead woman from the church, and the valley wants him for it.

Janina stays in the well. If the players later find her there — a fresh body among old bones — it lands as the story's cruelest irony: the woman who tore the pillow off Edek in 1954 has been fed to the same well that holds the man she saved him from. Rezeń did it without knowing what he was completing.
