# The Hunters Cross Paths

**Location:** [@name](../locations/old-village-ruins.md)
**Present:** [@name](../characters/neighbour.md), [@name](../characters/butcher.md), players (with whichever hunter they followed)
**Available:** During either [@name](hunt-with-rezen.md) or [@name](hunt-with-dudka.md).

## Trigger

- [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md) and [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) are hunting the same wolf pack on the same day.
- [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md) finally gets a clear shot at the pack.
- [@surname](../characters/butcher.md)'s dogs enter the clearing and scatter the wolves.

## Hook

A rifleman confronting a knife-wielding hunter as dogs scatter through the clearing.

## Setup

- [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md) is hidden with his rifle trained on the wolves.
- The wolves are in the open and unaware of [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md).
- [@surname](../characters/butcher.md)'s three dogs crash through the brush and drive the wolves into the trees.
- [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md)'s shot is lost.
- [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md) confronts [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) with the rifle in his hands.
- [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) goes still, keeps his knife in hand, and lets the dogs fan out around him.
- Both men are close enough to violence that one wrong movement can start a killing.

## Opportunities

- **The standoff** `(noticed by: [Violence](../cards/violence.md))` — [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md) is louder, but [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) is the one ready to kill. → Gives: [`butcher-is-dangerous`](../clues/clues.md#butcher-is-dangerous)
- **[@surname](../characters/neighbour.md)'s fury** `(noticed by: [Empathy](../cards/empathy.md))` `(prompted by: [rezen-hunts-wolves](../clues/clues.md#rezen-hunts-wolves))` — [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md) openly names [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) as more dangerous than the wolves. → Gives: [`dudka-despises-rezen`](../clues/clues.md#dudka-despises-rezen)
- **[@surname](../characters/butcher.md)'s cold** `(noticed by: [Violence](../cards/violence.md))` — [@surname](../characters/butcher.md)'s breathing stays even, his eyes stay flat, and the knife is already out. → Gives: [`butcher-is-dangerous`](../clues/clues.md#butcher-is-dangerous)
- **The dogs** `(noticed by: [Survival](../cards/survival.md))` — if the standoff turns violent, it will be [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) and three trained dogs against [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md). → Gives: [`butcher-is-dangerous`](../clues/clues.md#butcher-is-dangerous)

## Actions

### Get between them
- **When:** A player steps into the gap or clearly orders both men down
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** The standoff ends before blood is drawn; [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) pockets the knife and [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md) is denied the public humiliation of losing to him.
- **Gives:** [`butcher-is-dangerous`](../clues/clues.md#butcher-is-dangerous)
- **Changes:** [The clash](#the-clash) — ends, both separated unhurt

### Let it burn
- **When:** The players choose not to intervene
- **Prompted by:** [dudka-despises-rezen](../clues/clues.md#dudka-despises-rezen)
- **Outcome:** [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) ends the standoff by mocking [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md) for failing to stop him in the past; [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md) is publicly humiliated and [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) leaves with his dogs.
- **Gives:** [`butcher-is-dangerous`](../clues/clues.md#butcher-is-dangerous), [`dudka-despises-rezen`](../clues/clues.md#dudka-despises-rezen), [`rezen-mocks-an-old-failure`](../clues/clues.md#rezen-mocks-an-old-failure)
- **Changes:** [Humiliated](../characters/neighbour.md#humiliated) — [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md) is Humiliated

## Mechanics

### The clash

- Runs until someone ends it: the players get between them, or it burns out with [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) the winner.
