# The Hunters Cross Paths

**Location:** Forest between the villages
**Present:** [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) (rifle), [Stanisław Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) (three dogs), players (with whichever hunter they followed)
**Available:** During either [The Hunt with Rezeń](hunt-with-rezen.md) or [The Hunt with Dudka](hunt-with-dudka.md) — both men are tracking the same pack through the same woods on the same day, and the forest eventually puts them in sight of each other.

## Trigger

Whichever hunter the players followed, the other one surfaces. A hundred meters off through the trees: one man with a rifle, one man with dogs, both after the same wolves. Neither closes the distance. Neither will.

## Setup

For a moment the whole forest seems to hold still around the two of them. [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) goes rigid, rifle tightening in his hands, and stares across the clearing. [Stanisław Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) glances over, reads him in a second, and keeps walking — dogs at heel, unhurried, as if Dudka were a stump. No words pass between them. Then the trees swallow one or the other and it's over.

The tell is in the asymmetry: all the heat runs one way.

## Opportunities

- **Dudka's reaction** (if the players are with him) — he doesn't hide it. Once Rezeń is gone he spits it out unprompted: *"That's not a hunter. That's a butcher who found a reason to carry a knife in daylight. He shouldn't be out here. He shouldn't be anywhere near people."* **Read:** the hate is visceral and old — deeper than losing a wolf hunt to the man. [`dudka-despises-rezen`](../clues/clues.md#dudka-despises-rezen)
- **Rezeń's reaction** (if the players are with him) — flat, without heat, if asked: *"He's angry he couldn't do it. That's his to carry, not mine."* **Read:** the contempt runs entirely one direction. Rezeń doesn't hate Dudka; he simply doesn't register him as a threat. [`dudka-despises-rezen`](../clues/clues.md#dudka-despises-rezen)

## Exits

- Back to whichever hunt the players were on — [Rezeń](hunt-with-rezen.md) or [Dudka](hunt-with-dudka.md)

## If Missed

If the players aren't in the forest, the two men still cross paths and still say nothing. Nobody learns anything from a near-miss no one witnessed.
