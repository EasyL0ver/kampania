# The Hunters Cross Paths

**Location:** Forest between the villages
**Present:** [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) (rifle), [Stanisław Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) (three dogs), players (with whichever hunter they followed)
**Available:** During either [The Hunt with Rezeń](hunt-with-rezen.md) or [The Hunt with Dudka](hunt-with-dudka.md) — both men are working the same pack through the same woods on the same day, and it comes to a head the moment Dudka finally has a shot and Rezeń's dogs blow it.

## Trigger

Both men have been closing on the same pack all day. It comes to a head when [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) finally gets his chance — bellied down in the brush, rifle up, the wolves grazing a clearing below him, the shot he's needed for weeks lined up at last. And that is the moment [Stanisław Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md)'s dogs come crashing through.

## Setup

Dudka is stone-still in the ferns, cheek to the stock, breath held. Below him the pack moves in the open — close, unaware, his at last. His finger is taking up the slack.

Then the brush erupts. [Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md)'s three dogs come tearing through downwind, driving straight at the wolves, and the clearing detonates — wolves scattering into the trees, dogs baying after them, the whole day's work gone in three seconds. Dudka's shot goes wide into empty air, or never comes at all.

He's on his feet before the echo dies, rifle swinging around — for one ugly heartbeat it isn't clear whether he'll put it on the dogs, or on the man who follows them out of the trees. [Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) steps into the clearing, takes in the wreck of the ambush, and doesn't so much as slow down. He whistles the dogs to heel and keeps walking.

## Opportunities

- **The near-shot** — Dudka trembling, rifle half-raised, the whole thing blown. **Read:** the rage isn't about wolves anymore. He wanted this one thing to go right, to prove he was enough, and *that man* took it from him without even trying. It's the wolf hunt in miniature — and everyone can see it.
- **Dudka's fury** (with him, after) — he doesn't hide it: *"He does that. Ruins a thing just by being near it. That's not a hunter — that's a butcher who found a reason to carry a knife in daylight. He shouldn't be anywhere near people."* [`dudka-despises-rezen`](../clues/clues.md#dudka-despises-rezen)
- **Rezeń's indifference** (with him) — his dogs did exactly what he sent them to do; that Dudka was lining up a shot is nothing to him. Flat, if asked: *"He's angry he couldn't do it. That's his to carry, not mine."* **Read:** the contempt runs entirely one direction. Rezeń doesn't hate Dudka; he doesn't register him at all. [`dudka-despises-rezen`](../clues/clues.md#dudka-despises-rezen)
- **The raised rifle** — **Observation / Milicjant:** for a moment the muzzle drifted toward Rezeń, not the dogs, before Dudka mastered it. A man that angry, that armed, this close to the man he hates. [`neighbour-has-rifle`](../clues/clues.md#neighbour-has-rifle)

## If Missed

If the players aren't in the forest, the dogs still blow Dudka's shot and the two men still say nothing. Dudka carries the humiliation home — one more log on the fire that pushes toward the [lynch](punishment-lynch.md). The players just don't witness how close the rifle came to swinging the wrong way.
