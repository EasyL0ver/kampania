# The Animals Flee the Valley

**Location:** %NEW_VILLAGE% — everywhere; worst along the forest edge and the old road toward [%OLD_VILLAGE%](../locations/old-village-ruins.md)
**Present:** No one is out on purpose. Village dogs, penned livestock, the things moving in the dark. [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) is awake at his window (the hunter always hears it first). [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) at [Ciotka's](../locations/ciotkas-house.md) window if the players are near.
**Available:** Night, Day 4 onward — after [the flood](the-flood.md) has peaked and the low valley has begun to fill. Fires once; the unease lingers into later nights.

## Trigger

The flood has been rising for days. Now the water has climbed high enough to swallow the floor of the low [%OLD_VILLAGE%](../locations/old-village-ruins.md) basin — the natural sink the whole valley drains into. The forest above it floods from below, and everything living in that ground is pushed out at once, uphill, through the only dry corridor left: %NEW_VILLAGE%.

## Hook

It starts with the dogs. Every dog in the village, all at once, then all of them at nothing — not the warning bark at a stranger but the high, steady, frightened kind that doesn't stop. Then, under it, other sounds in the dark: hooves on the road, small bodies in the grass, wings, the wet crash of something big moving through the brush too fast. Audible from any bed in %NEW_VILLAGE%.

## Setup

The rain has eased to a fine cold mist, and into that quiet comes a migration. In the dark the players can't see clearly — only shapes, movement, eyeshine caught in a lamp: deer picking through the yards, not bolting, just *going*; foxes and hares moving in the open past each other and past the houses as if the rules were suspended; mice and voles pouring through the grass in a grey tide; birds that should be roosting flushed up and gone over the roofs. All of it moving one way — up and out, away from the low ground, away from the old valley.

And here is the thing that puts the hair up: they will not cross toward the old village. The dry, short way out for any animal fleeing the water runs along the old road, past the ruins. Nothing takes it. The whole flood of life bends wide around that ground and takes the long climb instead — as if the shorter road were on fire.

The village dogs won't settle. In his pen, one of [Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md)'s hunting dogs has torn its own paws bloody at the wire. And at [Ciotka's](../locations/ciotkas-house.md) window, if the players pass, [Edek](../characters/glupek.md) stands perfectly still in the middle of it all — not frightened, not fleeing — facing the old village, calm as a stone in a river.

## Opportunities

- **The one-way flood of animals** — Everything alive is moving uphill and out. Base: it's eerie, wrong, unforgettable. **Agronomy:** nothing about it is unnatural — flood drives game out of low ground every wet year; you've read the reports. Knowing that doesn't make it feel any better in the dark. → Gives: nothing (atmosphere)
- **The ground they won't cross** — The animals refuse the short dry road past the ruins and take the long climb. Base: even the beasts won't go near that place. **Observation:** it's real and consistent — not one deer strays toward the old village all night. **Culture:** you think of the old belief that animals feel a haunted ground before people do, and won't stand on it. You don't have to believe it to feel it. → Gives: nothing (atmosphere — human projection, not fact)
- **Edek in the doorway** — He stands calm in the chaos, facing the ruins. Base: unsettling in a boy who spooks at everything. **Read:** this isn't his usual agitation — it's the opposite, a stillness, like something out there quieted him instead of scaring him. → Gives: nothing (atmosphere; his own thread lives in his file)
- **Dudka at his window** — The hunter is up, rifle across his knees, watching the exodus with a face the players won't forget. **Sweettalk / Read:** he's not scared of wolves — he's a man who has spent twenty years in these woods and has never once seen them empty themselves like this. *"Water's got the low ground. They're just running from the water."* He says it like he's trying to make himself believe it. → Gives: nothing (atmosphere)

## Actions

### Read the flight
- **Requires:** Players go out into it and study the movement (or press Dudka on what he's seeing)
- **Cost:** 1 card (night card — no sleep)
- **Outcome:** Track where the animals are coming *from* and it resolves into plain, useful fact: they're pouring up out of the low old-village valley, and they're doing it because that basin is filling — fast. The water isn't spreading thin across the district; it's pooling into the one lowest place, exactly where the maps say it should, and that place is going under first.
  - **Geology / Observation:** you fix it precisely — the old-village basin is the sink the whole valley drains toward; it will take the water whether or not anyone acts, and it is taking it now.
  - **Agronomy:** the flight pattern is textbook flood displacement — real animals, real water, no more than that. The dread is the village's, not the ground's.
- **Gives:** [`old-village-basin-is-the-low-sink`](../clues/clues.md#old-village-basin-is-the-low-sink)

### Calm the dogs / secure the livestock
- **Requires:** Nothing
- **Cost:** 1 card (night card — no sleep)
- **Outcome:** The players spend the small hours helping [Michał Pytlak](../characters/foreman.md) and whoever else is up keep the panicked stock from breaking pens and hurting themselves in the dark. Hard, wet, thankless work — but it's the kind of standing-with that a village remembers. Pytlak, soaked and grim, talks under the labour: the water, the losses, the sense that the place is being emptied out from underneath them.
  - **Endurance:** you're worth three men at it; the pens hold, nothing is lost to the panic.
- **Gives:** NPC State Change — [Michał Pytlak](../characters/foreman.md) warms to the committee (goodwill toward the flood-fight and evacuation threads)

## Exits

- Back to bed — but the night cards are spent and morning comes short on sleep and shorter on composure.
- Toward [%OLD_VILLAGE%](../locations/old-village-ruins.md) — players rattled enough to go looking find the low valley visibly drowning, the water climbing the ruins. (The ground the animals wouldn't cross.)

## If Missed

If the players sleep through it — or lie awake and stay in — it passes on its own. By morning the yards are churned to mud, a few pens are down, [Pytlak](../characters/foreman.md) has lost more stock to panic than to any wolf, and the village is jumpy and short-tempered, half-convinced the land itself has turned against them. The old road past the ruins stays quiet. The players hear about it over breakfast, secondhand, dread already curdling into village superstition — *even the animals ran from that place.*
