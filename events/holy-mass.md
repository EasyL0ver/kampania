# Holy Mass — Anti-Flood Prayer

**Location:** [The church](../locations/the-church.md) — Day 4 morning
**Present:** [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md) and the village come to pray the water away — [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md) (front pew, as every day), her husband [Emil](../characters/painter.md), [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) keeping up the sołtys's appearances. [Tadek](../characters/wujas.md) is **absent** — still in the bottle; he won't set foot in the church until his sister's funeral. **[Janina Gajda](../characters/ciotka.md)'s pew is empty** — she never misses Mass, and she is not here.
**Available:** Day 4. The first flood Mass — the baseline his later sermons climb from.

## Setup

He preaches on **Jonah** — the prophet sent to the city that was about to be destroyed:

> *"Yet forty days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown."* — Jonah 3:4

A deadline, he tells them, is a mercy. Nineveh was warned, and Nineveh had a number of days to turn back before the end came — and because the city repented, the end did not come. He sets the number against the rising water: the valley has been given its own count of days, and the flood is not yet inevitable. There is still time to turn.

He names no sin. This early, it is a general call — repent, all of you, whatever it is you carry, before the days run out. And he makes the call concrete: he tells them the confessional will stay open after Mass, all day, as long as anyone needs it, and he begs them — plainly, from the altar — to come and unburden themselves while there is still time. Praying the water away is not enough, he says; a soul has to be clean when the water comes. Come and confess.

The village hears a frightened parish priest telling them to pray harder and go to confession. Only later will they understand he already knew exactly what needed repenting, that the invitation was aimed at particular souls in those pews, and that he was giving them the first, softest chance to say it themselves.
