# Barbara Warns the Players

**Location:** Wherever the players are — [Barbara](../characters/barbara.md) comes to find them
**Present:** [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md), breathless. She seeks out the one player who has [earned her trust](../characters/barbara.md#bond).
**Available:** Day 6, late afternoon — a few hours before dark. **Requires:** at least one player has ticked a [Bond](../characters/barbara.md#bond) with Barbara. **If no one has, this event does not happen** — see [If Missed](#if-missed).

## Trigger

By Day 6 [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) has stopped talking and started moving — the drinking circle at [Tadek's](../characters/wujas.md) has turned purposeful, and his rifle is down off its pegs. Barbara lives on the other side of his fence. She has watched him her whole time in this village and she has never seen him like this. She's frightened, and she comes to the one committee member who ever treated her like a person.

## Hook

Barbara at the door, or catching a player by the sleeve in the yard — no cardigan, mud to the shins, out of breath from half-running. She never comes to *them*. Something is wrong.

## Setup

She's not her usual warm, unhurried self. She keeps glancing back toward the fence line, toward [Dudka's house](../locations/neighbours-house.md). She takes the player's arm — the way she always does — but this time her grip is tight.

She doesn't have facts. She has a neighbour she loves and a bad feeling she can't sit on. What she says comes out in pieces: Ryszard's been drinking, which he never does. Men have been at his place. He took the rifle down and cleaned it and didn't put it back. He said something about *someone finally answering for what they did* and wouldn't explain. He didn't come to the fence tonight for the first time in years.

*"He's a good man,"* she keeps saying, as if the players are the ones who need convincing. *"He's the best man in this valley. Something's wrong with him. Please — talk to him. He'll listen to you. He won't listen to me."*

She doesn't know she's the reason he knows what he knows. She has no idea that everything the players ever told her went over that fence. She thinks she's asking them to save her friend. She's asking them to disarm the gun she loaded.

**Composure: 1**

## Opportunities

- **What she actually knows** — the drinking, the men, the rifle, the phrase about someone answering. **Read:** She isn't guessing at a mood — she's describing a man making preparations. Whatever this is, it's tonight or close to it.
- **The phrase** — *"someone finally answering for what they did."* **Streetwise:** That's not grief talking. That's a verdict. He's decided, and he's naming a target in his head the players can't see.
- **Barbara herself** — frightened, loyal, pleading. **Read:** She loves him and she's more afraid *for* him than *of* him — afraid of what he's about to become, not what he'll do to her. She can't imagine the harm landing on anyone but Ryszard himself.

## Actions

### Get the timing out of her
- **Requires:** Nothing — she wants to tell them
- **Cost:** Free
- **Outcome:** Pressed gently, she gives what she saw and when: the men gathered at Tadek's tonight, the rifle gone, Ryszard not at the fence. It adds up to **tonight, after dark.** The players now know the [lynch](punishment-lynch.md) is coming and roughly when — hours to prepare instead of a mob already on the road.
- **Gives:** Advance warning of [the lynch](punishment-lynch.md) — the players can act before nightfall (reach Dudka, build the justice structure, position themselves) instead of meeting the mob cold.

### Ask who he means to answer for it
- **Requires:** Nothing
- **Cost:** Free
- **Outcome:** She doesn't know — he wouldn't say a name. But she reaches for the one everybody reaches for: *"Rezeń, I suppose. It's always Rezeń, isn't it?"* She says it the way the whole valley says it — a habit, not a fact. It tells the players who the *default* target is, not who Dudka has actually fixed on.
- **Gives:** Confirmation that the village's reflex target is [Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) — but not where Dudka's own aim has landed (that's the [targeting score](../characters/neighbour.md#lynch-targets)).

### Send her home / keep her clear
- **Requires:** Nothing
- **Cost:** Free
- **Outcome:** Tell her to go home, lock the door, keep Pawełek in. She'll go, reluctantly — she doesn't want to leave Ryszard to it, but she'll do what the players ask because she trusts them. **This keeps her out of [the lynch](punishment-lynch.md).** A Barbara who was warned and sent home does not stand in the mob that night.
- **NPC State Change:** Barbara stays clear of the lynch — she will not lend it her presence or her legitimacy.

## If Missed

**If no player ever bonded with Barbara, this event never happens.** The party that used her as a source and never as a person gets no warning — no running figure at the door, no window, no name. The first they know of it is lamps on the road and dogs in the dark ([the lynch](punishment-lynch.md) fires on them cold).

Worse: the Barbara who was never seen as a person has no reason to break from the man who *did* see her. When the mob forms, **she stands with [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md)** — silent, miserable, arms crossed, not meeting the players' eyes, but *there.* The one uncorrupted soul in the valley lends the lynch the one thing it can't manufacture: legitimacy. See [the lynch](punishment-lynch.md).
