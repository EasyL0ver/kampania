# Irena Confronts the Wojewoda

**Location:** [Zbigniew Gajda's house](../locations/wojewodas-house.md) — behind a closed door
**Present:** [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) (unmasked, coming apart), [Irena Gajda](../characters/wife.md) (the repression breaking, then setting into iron)
**Available:** When Irena pivots (trigger TBD — see [her mechanics](../characters/wife.md#mechanics)), evening or night. Fires when the household is private — most naturally if the players lodge at the house, or are visiting that evening.

## Trigger

This is Irena's [pivot](../characters/wife.md#mechanics). For thirteen years she investigated everything but the one thread that pointed at her husband. When it finally forces its way home, she sees that Zbigniew was one of the men who killed that night. She doesn't take it to the players, and she doesn't take it to the law. She takes it to him.

## Hook

Raised voices from behind a closed door — hers first, low and fast and wrong for a woman who never raises her voice; then his, and his is worse, because the sołtys's voice is the one coming apart. Perceivable from anywhere inside the house: the hall, the guest room, the top of the stairs. The kind of fight a household is not supposed to hear, in a house where nobody is supposed to hear anything.

## Setup

The office door, or the bedroom's — shut. Light under it. The pieces come through the wood, and they come in the wrong order for this house — her voice hard and fast, his slow and giving way. Hers first, the reconstruction she assembled years ago and buried in herself, thrown at him at last:

- *"You were there. You were one of them."*
- *"Don't you dare tell me you weren't. I've known for years. I made myself not know."*

A long silence — then his voice, and it isn't the level, valley-stilling voice he uses on everyone. It's tired. It gives everything up at once: *"...Yes. God forgive me. Maybe it's time they knew. Maybe I should just —"*

And here is the turn the players are not prepared for. It is not the wife who breaks. It is the husband. The man who ran this valley for thirteen years is reaching, out loud, to set the whole thing down — and she cuts him off before he can finish the sentence. Her voice drops and hardens into something colder than his has ever been:

- *"You'll do no such thing. You'll say nothing — not to them, not to anyone, not ever."*
- *"Sit down. Pull yourself together. If you can't hold this, I'll hold it for you."*

The fight was never going to end with her turning on him. It ends with her becoming his spine. The woman who spent thirteen years trying to make him see her has finally been seen — over a grave — and her answer is to lock the grave shut with both of them inside it.

## Opportunities

- **Overhear from the hall** — You catch enough to know a marriage is coming apart over something buried, that the sołtys said *"yes"* to something and reached to confess it, and that his wife shut him down cold. You don't get the shape of it without staying. → Gives: [`wife-protects-husband`](../clues/clues.md#wife-protects-husband)
  - **Read:** The wrong person is breaking. *He's* the one coming apart; *she's* the one going to iron. By the end she isn't accusing him — she's shielding him from himself.

## Actions

### Listen at the door
- **Requires:** Present in the house when the fight starts
- **Cost:** Free (but see below) — 1 composure to stay for the whole of it
- **Outcome:** You hold your place at the door and take in the entire exchange — her reconstruction thrown at him in full: the night, the drink, him and his brother and the butcher, the girl dragged out into the dark. He doesn't correct a single detail — he confirms it, and then reaches to confess it to the world, and she kills the impulse in his throat. Standing in a stranger's hallway hearing a man confirm a murder while his wife forbids him to admit it costs something to witness (1 composure).
  - **Read:** Every fact she names, she already had. She assembled this years ago and buried it with herself. Tonight she's not discovering it — she's saying it out loud, once, so that both of them can agree never to say it again.
- **Gives:** [`three-drunk-attackers`](../clues/clues.md#three-drunk-attackers) + [`wife-protects-husband`](../clues/clues.md#wife-protects-husband)

### Open the door
- **Requires:** Present in the house when the fight starts
- **Cost:** Free
- **Outcome:** You walk in on it. The fight stops dead. Zbigniew's mask is back before the door finishes swinging — *"A private matter. Goodnight."* — but you saw the half-second before it: him unguarded and wet-eyed, and her already on her feet, stepping between him and you, her body turned to shield the man she's just condemned. Nothing more is said tonight. But she knows you saw.
- **Gives:** [`wife-protects-husband`](../clues/clues.md#wife-protects-husband) + NPC State Change: Irena (investigator → protector; the parallel investigation shuts — traces dry up, Marek is pulled home)

### Approach Irena afterward
- **Requires:** Overheard or interrupted the fight; catch her alone afterward
- **Cost:** 1 card
- **Outcome:** The analyst is gone; so is the hostess. What's left is a wall. She won't unsay what you heard — she's past pretending — but she asks you for nothing and offers you nothing. No deal, no plea. She has decided, and a decided Irena does not move. What you confirm is only this: her cooperation is over, and she will spend everything she has keeping her husband upright and quiet.
  - **Read:** She isn't protecting a cause or bargaining for a report. She's a clever woman who has reasoned her way to shielding a murderer, and nothing you say will move her off it.
- **Gives:** [`wife-protects-husband`](../clues/clues.md#wife-protects-husband) + World State Change: Irena's cooperation ends — the parallel investigation closes, her leads dry up

## If Missed

If the players aren't in the house — or don't stay to listen — the fight resolves privately and nobody outside the marriage ever hears it. Once she's pivoted, Irena is Zbigniew's protector regardless (see [her Phase 2](../characters/wife.md#mechanics)): the household quietly closes ranks, the flour-tin traces dry up, Marek is pulled home, and she takes the confession out of her husband's reach. The players lose their parallel investigation and their best false lead in a single night and never learn why — and the one route where Zbigniew hangs himself with his own mouth quietly narrows. She never comes to them and never asks them for anything: after the pivot she acts only on him.
