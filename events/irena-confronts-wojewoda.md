# Irena Confronts the Wojewoda

**Location:** [Zbigniew Gajda's house](../locations/wojewodas-house.md) — behind a closed door
**Present:** [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) (unmasked), [Irena Gajda](../characters/wife.md) (the repression breaking, then setting)
**Available:** When Irena pivots (trigger TBD — see [her mechanics](../characters/wife.md#mechanics)), evening or night. Fires when the household is private — most naturally if the players lodge at the house, or are visiting that evening.

## Trigger

This is Irena's [pivot](../characters/wife.md#mechanics). For thirteen years she investigated everything but the one thread that pointed at her husband. When it finally forces its way home, she sees that Zbigniew was one of the men who killed that night. She doesn't take it to the players, and she doesn't take it to the law. She takes it to him.

## Hook

Raised voices from behind a closed door — hers first, low and fast and wrong for a woman who never raises her voice; then his, quieter, which is worse. Perceivable from anywhere inside the house: the hall, the guest room, the top of the stairs. The kind of fight a household is not supposed to hear, in a house where nobody is supposed to hear anything.

## Setup

The office door, or the bedroom's — shut. Light under it. Irena's voice comes in pieces through the wood:

- *"I always knew. I made myself not know."*
- *"You were there. You were one of them."*
- *"Tell me you didn't. Look at me and tell me."*

He doesn't tell her he didn't. There's a long silence — the temperature-drop stillness he uses on the whole village — and then his voice, level, giving nothing back: *"Yes."* No excuse. No plea. He has been ready for this conversation for thirteen years.

And here is the turn the players are not prepared for. She doesn't scream that she'll expose him. She doesn't leave. The fast, cracking voice slows. Something in her that was breaking decides, instead, to hold. When she speaks again she is quieter than he is — already somewhere new: *"Then no one else will ever know. Not from me. Not from them. I'll see to it."*

The fight was never going to end with her turning on him. It ends with her crossing to his side. The woman who spent thirteen years trying to make him see her has finally been seen — over a grave.

## Opportunities

- **Overhear from the hall** — You catch enough to know a marriage is coming apart over something buried, and that the sołtys said *"yes"* to something. You don't get the shape of it without staying. → Gives: [`wife-protects-husband`](../clues/clues.md#wife-protects-husband)
  - **Read:** Her voice doesn't do what a betrayed wife's voice does. It breaks — and then it *organizes.* By the end she isn't accusing him; she's protecting him. She has picked a side, and it's his.

## Actions

### Listen at the door
- **Requires:** Present in the house when the fight starts
- **Cost:** Free (but see below) — 1 composure to stay for the whole of it
- **Outcome:** You hold your place at the door and take in the entire exchange — her reconstruction thrown at him in full: the night, the drink, him and his brother and the butcher, the girl dragged out into the dark. He doesn't correct a single detail. Standing in a stranger's hallway listening to a man confirm a murder to his wife costs something to witness (1 composure).
  - **Read:** Every fact she names, she already had. She assembled this years ago and buried it with herself. Tonight she's not discovering it — she's finally saying it out loud, once, so she can agree never to say it again.
- **Gives:** [`three-drunk-attackers`](../clues/clues.md#three-drunk-attackers) + [`wife-protects-husband`](../clues/clues.md#wife-protects-husband)

### Open the door
- **Requires:** Present in the house when the fight starts
- **Cost:** Free
- **Outcome:** You walk in on it. The fight stops dead. Zbigniew's mask is back before the door finishes swinging — *"A private matter. Goodnight."* — but you saw the half-second before it: him unguarded, her tear-streaked and already stepping between him and you, her body turned to shield the man she's just condemned. Nothing more is said tonight. But she knows you saw, and she will come to you about it — sooner than she meant to.
- **Gives:** [`wife-protects-husband`](../clues/clues.md#wife-protects-husband) + NPC State Change: Irena (moves early to [the deal](../characters/wife.md#actions) — she now needs the players managed) + Scene Unlock: [Irena's deal](../characters/wife.md#actions)

### Ask Irena about it after
- **Requires:** Overheard or interrupted the fight; catch her alone afterward
- **Cost:** 1 card
- **Outcome:** The hostess is gone. What's left is calm and unmovable. She won't unsay what you heard — she's past pretending — but she reframes it with the argument she's already made peace with: *"Whatever he was, this village does not survive the water without him. You know that. I'm not asking you to forgive it. I'm asking you to be practical."* This is the deal, offered early, because you forced her hand.
  - **Read:** She believes it. That's the frightening part. She's not lying to protect a monster; she's a clever woman who has genuinely reasoned her way to shielding one, and she'll reason you there too if you let her.
- **Gives:** [`wife-protects-husband`](../clues/clues.md#wife-protects-husband) + Scene Unlock: [Irena's deal](../characters/wife.md#actions)

## If Missed

If the players aren't in the house — or don't stay to listen — the fight resolves privately and nobody outside the marriage ever hears it. Once she's pivoted, Irena is Zbigniew's protector regardless (see [her Phase 2](../characters/wife.md#mechanics)): the household quietly closes ranks, the flour-tin traces dry up, and Marek is pulled home. The players lose their parallel investigation and their best false lead in a single night and never learn why. If they later assemble the truth on their own, Irena still comes to them with [the deal](../characters/wife.md#actions) — just without the players ever having glimpsed the moment it was born.
