# The Disclosure

**Location:** %NEW_VILLAGE% — outside [Zbigniew Gajda's house](../locations/wojewodas-house.md), the yard and the muddy square in front of the sołtys office
**Present:** The whole village, gathered in the rain. [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) at the center — either at the front of it or barricaded behind his own door, depending on how the truth got out. [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) (loudest), [Michał Pytlak](../characters/foreman.md), [Zofia Pytlak](../characters/zofia.md), [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md), [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md), [Marek Gajda](../characters/junior.md), [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md). [Stefania Kopacz](../characters/babcia.md) is not here — she stays in her house.
**Available:** Day 4 onward, once the village learns %NEW_VILLAGE% is going under — after the flood proof exists and the truth breaks, by whichever route (see Trigger).

## Trigger

The village finds out it was lied to. It happens one of three ways, and *which one* sets the entire shape of the scene:

- **Enlisted** — the players convinced Zbigniew to reveal it himself (["Convince him to reveal the flood"](../characters/wojewoda.md#convince-him-to-reveal-the-flood)). **He** calls the gathering. → *Form A.*
- **Forced** — the players cornered him with the ultimatum (["Force the disclosure — the ultimatum"](../characters/wojewoda.md#force-the-disclosure--the-ultimatum)). He calls the gathering, but on his terms and against his will. → *Form A, colder.*
- **Broken** — the players announced it themselves over his refusal, or it leaked (a panicking villager, [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) piecing it together, an overheard word). No one controls it. The crowd assembles itself. → *Form B.*

Without the flood proof there is no disclosure — only clerks with a rumor nobody believes. The proof is the match.

## Hook

Voices in the rain. Not the organized flood-work of [Day 3](the-flood.md) — this is a crowd finding one another, doors opening, people calling across the mud toward the sołtys office. Audible from anywhere in %NEW_VILLAGE%. By the time the players arrive, most of the village is already there, and the rain hasn't let up.

## Setup

**Form A — the gathering Zbigniew called.** He stands on the step of his own office, above the crowd, the way he moves through the village like he owns it. He waited for the rain to ease enough to be heard and it hasn't, so he raises his voice — deep, unhurried, the temperature of a man who has decided exactly what will be said. He tells them the water is coming for %NEW_VILLAGE%, not just the lowlands. He does not say *how long it was known.* He tells them there is a plan — compensation, relocation, the state will provide — and that panic helps no one. If the players **enlisted** him, he names them as the ones who found the danger in time; if they **forced** him, he does not name them at all, and his eyes find them once, flat, across the crowd. The village listens to their sołtys. The containment holds — for now.

**Form B — the crowd that came for him.** No one is above the crowd. The door of the office is shut and Zbigniew is behind it, or shouldering out into a yard already turning on him. The word is loose and there is no one shaping it, so it curdles: *they knew, they were told we were safe, the government's clerks have been in our kitchens writing our names while the water came.* [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) is loudest, and he isn't wrong. The faces turn toward the players — the government, in the flesh, standing in the rain. This is the backlash. *You told us we were safe.*

Either way the water keeps rising behind all of it, and everyone knows the talking has a clock on it.

## Opportunities

- **Read the crowd** — it isn't one mood, it's a dozen. [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) wants someone to hit. [Marek](../characters/junior.md) is almost glad — a ticket out. [Zofia](../characters/zofia.md) is already grieving, ready to leave. [Pytlak](../characters/foreman.md) doesn't want to run at all — he wants to *fight* the water. [Barbara](../characters/barbara.md) is terrified — a single mother with a four-year-old and nowhere to go. [Helena](../characters/matrona.md) watches Zbigniew and takes her cue from him.
  - **Read:** The village will split, and the fracture lines are already visible. Whoever speaks next decides which way it tips — toward an orderly evacuation or a rout.
- **Watch Zbigniew** *(Form A)* — the composure is a performance and it's costing him. This is his life's work drowning and he's selling calm anyway.
  - **Streetwise:** He is spending authority he can't replace. If the crowd stops believing him — if someone contradicts him in front of them — the whole thing comes apart in his hands.
- **The unasked question** — sooner or later someone asks the thing Zbigniew steered around: *how long have you known?* In Form B it's already the whole accusation. In Form A it's the crack waiting to open.
  - **Read:** There is no good answer. The honest one — *since before we arrived* — is the one that turns the gathering into the mob.
- **The priest** — [Pająk](../characters/priest.md) says little, but the crowd checks him. The same authority that keeps the peace can break it; a word from him can bless an evacuation or bless a lynching.
  - **Streetwise:** He is deciding whether the government in the square is a partner or a target. Push him wrong and he can call a mob down with a sentence.

## Actions

### Back Zbigniew's line *(Form A)*
- **Requires:** Form A (he called it); the players standing with him
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** They reinforce his framing — there's a plan, the state will help, move in order. Coming from the outsiders *and* the sołtys together, it holds. The village tips toward cooperation: they'll pack, they'll gather, they'll take the evacuation seriously.
- **World State Change:** The disclosure lands as an **orderly evacuation footing** — the village is frightened but moving, not rioting. Sets up the evacuation with the crowd on-side.

### Tell the whole truth — how long it was known
- **Requires:** Being heard by the crowd
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** A player says the thing Zbigniew buried: the state knew, the state *said the opposite*, the reassurance was a lie to get them to resettle here. It is true and it is unsurvivable in the moment. The crowd turns — on the government, which means on the players, and in Form A on Zbigniew beside them. Whatever calm existed is gone. *You told us we were safe.*
  - **If Zbigniew was enlisted:** he steps in front of it — *these people found it in time, they're the reason we can still leave* — and absorbs the worst, shielding the players even now. He hates paying this, but he owns the room.
  - **If Zbigniew was forced or defied:** he does the opposite. He lets the rage roll onto the players and does not stand between. Better the mob blames the clerks than the sołtys — and better still, anger aimed at the outsiders never asks what *he* did at the well.
- **World State Change:** The disclosure curdles toward **panic / every-family-for-itself** unless something pulls it back. Cooperation for the census and an orderly evacuation is badly damaged.
- **NPC State Change:** [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) moves from furious to dangerous — the man most likely to throw the first punch.

### Face the crowd — take the anger *(Form B)*
- **Requires:** Form B (the mob came for them), or the truth has already broken in Form A
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** The players stand in it instead of running from it. There's no version where they talk their way clean — they *are* the state, and the state lied. What they can do is choose what to offer the rage: a target, a plan, or the truth about who really decided this valley would drown.
  - **Give them a plan** (an evacuation, army rescue, somewhere to go) → the fear has somewhere to go besides the players' faces. The crowd doesn't forgive, but it moves. Requires the players to actually have a route — otherwise it's another empty promise from the government and lands worse.
  - **Give them Skowron / the state above them** — name that the decision was made far above this valley, by the men who chose it for flooding (see [por. Witold Skowron](../characters/officer.md)). Redirects some heat off the players and onto the absent authority. It's true, and it's also the coward's aim — but it works.
  - **Give them nothing but the truth** — no plan, no deflection, just *yes, we knew, and it was wrong.* The most honest and the most dangerous. With [Pająk](../characters/priest.md) unfriendly and [Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md) primed, this is the line that can tip into violence against the players.
- **NPC State Change:** Where the anger settles — on the players, on Zbigniew, on the distant state — is set here and carries into the evacuation.

### Force Zbigniew to answer the crowd *(Form B)*
- **Requires:** Form B; the players want the sołtys held responsible, not themselves
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** They turn the crowd's question onto Zbigniew where he stands — *he's your headman, ask him how long he knew.* It's a real move and a cruel one. He is far better at this than they are; he'll deflect, redirect, invoke the plan, remind the village who held it together for twenty years. But every second the crowd looks at *him* is a second the well isn't being discussed — and he knows the players just showed they'll aim a mob at him.
  - **If it lands:** the heat splits — off the players, onto Zbigniew. He survives it (he's the superego; containing crowds is what he does), but he now treats the players as a threat, not a nuisance. Permanent grudge.
  - **If it doesn't:** he flips it back — *these are the government's people, not mine* — and the aim returns to the players, harder.
- **NPC State Change:** [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md) → open antagonist toward the players, whichever way it breaks.

### Calm it down — buy the evacuation
- **Requires:** A credible way out to point at (an evacuation plan, the [phone line to the army](operator-refuses-help.md), somewhere to go)
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** Instead of arguing about blame, the players convert the gathering into logistics — who goes first, the sick and the children, where they muster, when. It only works with a *real* route behind it; a plan the players can't deliver makes it worse. Done right, it pulls the crowd back from the edge and turns fear into movement.
- **World State Change:** Regardless of Form, the scene resolves toward **evacuation footing** instead of riot. This is the players spending the truth well — the village is angry and leaving alive, rather than angry and drowning in place.

## Exits

- **Toward evacuation** — if the crowd tipped toward a plan, the arc moves to getting people out, which runs through the [call to the operator](operator-refuses-help.md) and the army.
- **Toward the fight** — if [Pytlak](../characters/foreman.md) carried the room with *we don't run, we dig*, the arc bends to the engineering stand (see [Foreman Saves the Village](foreman-saves-village.md)).
- **Toward the water** — if it broke down into panic and every-family-for-itself, no one is coordinating when the lower village goes under. Straight into the climax with the worst footing.

## If Missed

If the players never surface the flood truth and never let it leak, there is no disclosure — the village works the flood as weather, cooperates with the census believing it's a temporary disruption, and is still inside the lie when the water comes. The reveal, if it happens at all, happens too late to organize anything: the truth arrives with the water, and the village learns it was lied to at the same moment it learns it has lost everything. The players leave the valley with the secret intact and the bodies to match.
