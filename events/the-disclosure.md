# The Disclosure

**Location:** [@name](../locations/wojewodas-house.md)
**Present:** [@name](../characters/wojewoda.md), [@name](../characters/neighbour.md), [@name](../characters/foreman.md), [@name](../characters/zofia.md), [@name](../characters/barbara.md), [@name](../characters/matrona.md), [@name](../characters/junior.md), [@name](../characters/priest.md), villagers
**Available:** Day 4 onward, after proof that [@name](../locations/new-village.md) will flood exists and the truth breaks.

## Trigger

- The village learns [@name](../locations/new-village.md) will flood.
- The truth breaks by one of three routes.
- **Enlisted:** players convinced [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) to reveal it through [Convince him to reveal the flood](../characters/wojewoda.md#convince-him-to-reveal-the-flood).
- **Forced:** players used [Force the disclosure — the ultimatum](../characters/wojewoda.md#force-the-disclosure--the-ultimatum).
- **Broken:** players announced it, the truth leaked, a villager panicked, [@name](../characters/neighbour.md) pieced it together, or someone overheard.
- Without flood proof, this event does not fire.

## Hook

- Voices gather in the rain.
- Doors open across [@name](../locations/new-village.md).
- People call toward the sołtys office.
- The sound is audible from anywhere in [@name](../locations/new-village.md).
- By the time players arrive, most of the village is present.

## Setup

- The scene happens in the muddy yard and square outside the sołtys office.
- **Form A:** [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) called the gathering.
- In Form A, [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) stands on the office step above the crowd.
- In Form A, he says [@name](../locations/new-village.md) will flood.
- In Form A, he does not say how long it was known.
- In Form A, he promises compensation, relocation, and state help.
- If enlisted, he names the players as the ones who found the danger in time.
- If forced, he does not name the players.
- In Form A, the village still listens to him.
- **Form B:** the crowd assembled itself.
- In Form B, [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) is behind a shut door or forced into a yard that is turning on him.
- In Form B, the crowd accuses the state and the committee of lying.
- In Form B, [@name](../characters/neighbour.md) is loudest.
- In either form, the rising water gives the scene a time limit.
- [@name](../characters/babcia.md) is not present; she stays in her house.

## Actions

### Back Wojewoda's line
- **When:** Form A and players standing with [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md).
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** Players reinforce his framing: there is a plan, the state will help, and people must move in order.
- **Changes:** [The disclosure](#the-disclosure) — TBD

### Tell the whole truth
- **When:** Being heard by the crowd.
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** A player says the state knew the danger and reassured the village anyway.
- **Changes:** [The disclosure](#the-disclosure) — TBD
### Face the crowd with a plan
- **When:** Form B, or the truth has already broken in Form A; a credible evacuation route, army rescue, or place to send people.
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** Players take the anger and give the crowd logistics instead of blame.
- **Changes:** [The disclosure](#the-disclosure) — TBD

### Face the crowd without a plan
- **When:** Form B, or the truth has already broken in Form A; no credible route or deliverable promise.
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** Players admit the lie or redirect blame without offering safety.
- **Changes:** [The disclosure](#the-disclosure) — TBD

### Force Wojewoda to answer the crowd
- **When:** Form B and players aim responsibility at the sołtys.
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** The crowd's question turns onto [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md).
- **Changes:** [The disclosure](#the-disclosure) — TBD

### Calm it down
- **When:** A credible way out: evacuation plan, the [@name](operator-refuses-help.md), or somewhere people can go.
- **Cost:** 1 time
- **Outcome:** Players turn the gathering into evacuation logistics: who goes first, where people muster, and when they move.
- **Changes:** [The disclosure](#the-disclosure) — TBD

- If players never surface the flood truth and it never leaks, there is no disclosure.
- The village treats the flood as weather and cooperates with the census.
- The village remains inside the lie until the water arrives.
- If the truth arrives too late, no orderly response forms.
