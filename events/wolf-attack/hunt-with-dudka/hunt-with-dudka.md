Stung and silent, [@surname](../../../characters/neighbour.md) sets off with his rifle. At the gate a farmhand calls after him that he is wasting his time: it is a curse, and no bullet stops a curse. [@surname](../../../characters/neighbour.md) turns on him: wolves are wolves, the rain drove them down off the high ground, and they are hungry.[](#wolves-are-wolves) On the walk he loosens a little and talks game: the boar glut two summers back, all of them rooting at one spot up past the farm where the PGR tipped a whole silo of spoiled grain.[](#the-boar-glut) Where the ravine path turns off, he stops. He has to see someone first. They can come or not.[](#go-with-him)
