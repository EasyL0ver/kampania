## Setup

[@surname](../../../characters/butcher.md) starts where the losses are. The flock bunches in terror at his dogs while he questions the shepherd, who rambles that the woman in the forest sends the wolves.[](#the-woman-in-the-forest) [@surname](../../../characters/butcher.md) laughs at that: wolves are wolves. But he keeps at the old man, pleasant and patient: where has he seen her, which way does she come, at what hour? The shepherd does not know. [@surname](../../../characters/butcher.md) does not let it drop.[](#it-is-her-he-wants) On his way out he presses a few cigarettes into the old man's hand: if he sees her, he is to send word to the edge house. Then he turns to the committee, lightly: they go all over, have they seen an old woman in the forest? The dogs have the scent at the treeline, and the hunt goes that way. Are they coming?[](#follow-him-into-the-forest)
