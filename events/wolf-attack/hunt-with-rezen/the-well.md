## Setup

He carries the wolf down into the ruins of Hołubie, to the old stone well, and tips it in. It is a long time before anything sounds from below.[](#he-feeds-the-well) "Saves digging," he says. There are burnt-down candles at the rim. His face sets. He sweeps them off the stones with the side of his boot and kicks them down after the wolf. Then, from the trees above the ruins, a wolf howls, close. The dogs erupt, straining toward the sound.[](#go-after-the-howl)
