## Setup

The way runs past collapsed dugouts and rusted metal in the undergrowth.[](#old-holes-in-the-forest)[](#partisan-hides) [@surname](../../../characters/butcher.md) does not slow down; he has walked here before. Walking, he chats easily: about his dogs and what each one is good for, the rain, the committee's city shoes on the wet roots. Then the dogs give tongue all at once. They have a wolf at bay against a fallen trunk, a lean grey thing snarling with its back to the wood, the three dogs holding it there. [@surname](../../../characters/butcher.md) walks up to it without hurrying, the knife already in his hand.[](#calm-with-the-blade) One step in, one movement, and it is over. He wipes the blade on the wolf's own fur. They cannot leave it for the foxes, he says, and the farm does not want it back; he knows just the place for it. Do they want to come?[](#follow-him-with-the-carcass)
