# Tadek Visits the Butcher

**Location:** [@name](../locations/butchers-house.md)
**Present:** [@name](../characters/wujas.md), [@name](../characters/butcher.md)
**Available:** Days 1–2, recurring until wolf authorization on Day 2 afternoon

## Trigger

- [@name](../characters/wojewoda.md) maintains contact with [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) through [@nickname](../characters/wujas.md).
- [@nickname](../characters/wujas.md) brings cigarettes or butchering work to [@surname](../characters/butcher.md).
- The arrangement has run for 13 years.

## Hook

- Players may see [@nickname](../characters/wujas.md) walking toward the village edge.
- Villagers may mention that he goes that way sometimes.

## Setup

- [@nickname](../characters/wujas.md) walks to the edge house.
- He brings cigarettes or a butchering job.
- He stays at the threshold for less than half an hour.
- He does not go inside.
- [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) takes what [@nickname](../characters/wujas.md) brings.
- The exchange is functional, not friendly.
- Villagers treat the visits as drinking-buddy business.
- [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) does not drink.
- [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) does not visit [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) himself.

## Opportunities

- **Regular pattern** `(noticed by: [Finesse](../cards/finesse.md))` — [@nickname](../characters/wujas.md) visits the edge house regularly.
- **No friendship** `(when: Follow [@nickname](../characters/wujas.md) to the edge house)` `(noticed by: [Empathy](../cards/empathy.md))` — the handoff is an errand, not a social visit.
- **[@nickname](../characters/wujas.md) deflects** `(when: Ask [@name](../characters/wujas.md))` `(noticed by: [Empathy](../cards/empathy.md))` — he cannot explain the visits without shutting down.
- **[@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) pauses** `(when: Ask [@name](../characters/wojewoda.md))` `(noticed by: [Empathy](../cards/empathy.md))` — he knows exactly what [@nickname](../characters/wujas.md) is doing.
- **[@surname](../characters/butcher.md) answers plainly** `(when: Ask [@name](../characters/butcher.md))` `(noticed by: [Empathy](../cards/empathy.md))` — the visits matter only as deliveries to him.
