# Third Flood Mass — The Breaking

**Location:** [The church](../locations/the-church.md) — water now reaching the door
**Present:** [ks. Władysław Pająk](../characters/priest.md) (breaking), [Helena Rzepka](../characters/matrona.md), [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md), [Ryszard Dudka](../characters/neighbour.md), [Tadek](../characters/wujas.md), the whole village crammed in. [Barbara](../characters/barbara.md) absent if [Pawełek is dying](pawelek-falls-ill.md). [Stefania Kopacz](../characters/babcia.md) does **not** come — she's gone back to the old faith.
**Available:** Day 7. The flood at its peak. The village has run out of everything except someone to blame.

## Trigger

Two masses failed. The water is at the lower houses and climbing. A child is sick — maybe dying. The village comes to church on Day 7 not to worship but to demand: make it stop, and tell us whose fault it is. ks. Pająk, whose faith has been eaten hollow by [the well](../locations/the-well.md), stands in front of them with nothing left — and the congregation in front of him has stopped being a congregation.

## Hook

No bell — the ringer never came, or the water reached the rope, or nobody could bear to hear it again. Instead: the sound of the whole village moving through the rain toward the church at once, and under the rain a rising noise that isn't prayer. Voices. Arguing. A crowd talking itself into something.

## Setup

The church is packed past its walls, bodies steaming, the air thick with wet wool and tallow and fear turned to anger. Water has come under the door — a black sheet spreading across the flagstones, rising over the soles of everyone at the back. The candles at floor level have drowned and gone out one by one, so the light dies from the edges inward.

ks. Pająk stands at the altar and does not begin. He looks at the crowd, opens the missal, and his hands won't move. When he finally speaks it is not the Mass — it comes out as pleading, then as something harder, a man arguing with God in front of witnesses. Then he stops arguing. Whatever holds a priest upright lets go, and the village watches him come apart.

And the village does not catch him. The room has curdled all the way through. This is no longer people praying together — it's a mob looking for a door to kick down. They talk over the priest. They say names out loud now, Rezeń loudest, and the hag, and the boy, and — turning, one row at a time — the strangers standing among them. The old kindnesses are gone. Neighbours who buried each other's parents are baring teeth. Whatever has been working on this valley for weeks is winning, and it's winning through them.

**Composure: 2** — the collapse of the one man who was supposed to hold the line, and a room full of good people going bad at once.

## Opportunities

- **The priest breaking** — arguing, pleading, then silence and shaking at the altar.
  - **Read:** His whole 13-year bargain is visible in his face — *I chose the living, and the living are drowning anyway.* The justification is gone. He knows his God isn't the one in this valley.
  - **Culture:** When he abandons the Latin, the fragments that come out aren't random. He's reaching for the old rite — the one he buried with the Lemkos — because it's the only thing sized to what's happening.

- **The room curdling** — voices over the priest, names said aloud, the crowd leaning one way like water about to break a bank.
  - **Read:** This isn't grief and it isn't panic. It's cruelty, surfacing all at once in people who never showed it before. The flood didn't make them like this — it uncovered it, and something is pulling it to the surface on purpose.
  - **Culture:** A valley soaked in unpunished murder, and now every soul in it is reaching for blood. This is what the [dead in the well](old-village-ruins.md) do to the living — the guilt of the place, handed back around as rage.
  - **Gives:** → [spirits-are-restless](../clues/clues.md#spirits-are-restless)

- **The water on the floor** — black, spreading, rising over the congregation's feet.
  - **Observation:** It's not rain runoff. It's coming up through the floor — the water table, the same water standing in the well. The flood is reaching into the church from underneath.

- **Who's stoking it** — Dudka half-shouting Rezeń's name, others taking it up; Helena over-performing devotion in the front pew, louder and more perfect as the room gets worse.
  - **Streetwise:** All the mob needs is a direction. Dudka is providing one. Helena is providing cover — the more visibly holy she is, the less anyone looks at her.
  - **Read:** Zbigniew has already read the room. He's edging toward the door — not to save anyone, to be gone before it breaks.

- **Tadek** — on his knees in the rising water, weeping openly, saying a name that isn't a saint's.
  - **Observation:** *"Hania."* Over and over, into his hands. Nobody else is listening — they've found louder names.

## Actions

### Reach the priest
- **Requires:** Nothing — he is past hiding anything
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** He grabs the players like a drowning man. He admits the prayers have never worked, that he has always known what lies under [%OLD_VILLAGE%](../locations/old-village-ruins.md), that his God has no authority here. Pushed with faith, respect, or the plain truth of the dead, he will say the thing he has refused for thirteen years: that the old rite — Paraskewia's rite — may be the only thing left. This is the permission the endgame needs.
  - See [priest character file](../characters/priest.md) and [the ritual](the-ritual.md).
- **NPC State Change:** ks. Pająk grants tacit permission for the Lemko rite.
- **Ending Progress:** Unlocks the priest's cooperation for the [ritual ending](the-ritual.md).

### Hold the room
- **Requires:** Standing (Composure) — the crowd is a shout away from a hunting party
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Skills:** **Intimidate**, **Law**, **Streetwise**
- **Outcome:** Step between the congregation and the name it wants to shout. Success buys time — the mob doesn't form *today*. Failure, or standing aside, and someone cries *Rezeń* into the water and the hunt begins straight out of the church.
  - **NPC State Change:** Success delays the [Punishment](punishment-lynch.md) mob. Failure launches it now.

### Turn the blame on the committee
- **Requires:** Players have made themselves hated — obstructed the village, sided against it, or been caught in something
- **Cost:** 1 action (defensive)
- **Outcome:** The mood finds the easiest target in the room: the outsiders. Fingers point at the pews where the committee stands. The players have to talk their way out, back out, or be driven out into the flood. How they handle it sets whether the village sees them as enemies for the rest of the game.
  - **NPC State Change:** World turns hostile to the committee, or is talked down — GM's call by how the players answer.

## If Missed

Nobody holds the room and nobody reaches the priest. He breaks with no one to catch him and comes out the other side either useless or fully into the old rite on his own — the GM's call by how far the well has taken him. And the room finds its door: the [Punishment](punishment-lynch.md) mob leaves the Day 7 mass looking for Rezeń — or for the committee. The one chance to turn this congregation toward the [ritual](the-ritual.md) instead of the rope is gone.
