# Performing the Ritual

**Type:** Event — ending, late game, one-shot
**Location:** [%OLD_VILLAGE%](../locations/old-village-ruins.md) — the [well](../locations/the-well.md); the [cerkiew](../locations/old-village-cerkiew.md) works too if the well is already underwater
**Present:** The dead, in the water. [Paraskewia Chyłak](../characters/hag.md) **if** she survived [the confrontation](well-confrontation.md) and will lead it. [Stefania Kopacz](../characters/babcia.md) **if** the players got her here (which means [Barbara Kopacz](../characters/barbara.md) brought her). [Edek Barnaś](../characters/glupek.md) may drift in on his own — the well pulls him.
**Available:** Late game, once the players hold all three components below. Night is traditional but not required. **Deadline:** the rising water. Once the flood takes the well ([the-flood](the-flood.md) onward), the site is gone and the rite must be spoken at the cerkiew on the hill instead — or not at all.
**Cost:** 1 action

## Trigger

The players choose to stand at the well and do what [Paraskewia Chyłak](../characters/hag.md) has done alone for twenty years: acknowledge the dead. This is the story's one counter-move to the [well's rising influence](../story-facts/well-influence.md) — not violence, not evidence, but the same tool she used. It fires only when the players have deliberately assembled the rite and walked out to the ruins to perform it.

## The Three Components

The rite needs three things, each from a different source (see [`ritual-requires-three-things`](../clues/clues.md#ritual-requires-three-things)). The players must hold all three when they begin:

1. **The Form — how.** [Paraskewia Chyłak](../characters/hag.md)'s cabin holds it: icons, candles, herbs, the shape of the rite ([`hag-has-the-form`](../clues/clues.md#hag-has-the-form)). If she's alive, she carries the form herself and needs no cabin.
2. **The Words — what to say.** The Lemko panakhyda. [Stefania Kopacz](../characters/babcia.md) has it ([`babcia-has-the-words`](../clues/clues.md#babcia-has-the-words)). Players can bring her to speak it, or carry the words themselves if they learned them from her (**Language**) or from Paraskewia.
3. **The Truth — what to acknowledge.** The players supply this, and there is no substitute ([`players-supply-truth`](../clues/clues.md#players-supply-truth)). They must be able to **name the dead** (see the roster below) and say what was done to them. Form and Words can be imperfect. **The Truth cannot be faked** — the rite is not a spell, it is acknowledgement.

## The Dead to Name

The rite works by naming the dead of this place and saying plainly what was done to them. The players don't need to know every name — most of the 1947 village is nameless to them, and that's acknowledged as "the people who lived here." But the rite is only as complete as the truth the players can speak, and these are the dead it must account for:

**Always in the well:**

- **The 1947 Lemko dead** — twelve villagers, killed in the [massacre](../story-facts/old-village-massacre.md) when the soldiers turned on the ones they cornered while the rest of the village fled. They are not nameless: [Paraskewia](../characters/hag.md) knew every one of them, wrote all twelve down in Cyrillic, and keeps the list ([Paraskewia's List of the Dead](../items/paraskewias-list.md)). [Stefania Kopacz](../characters/babcia.md) didn't know them — she's Lemko from another village — but she can read the names aloud and give them the prayer. The full [roster](../story-facts/old-village-massacre.md#the-dead-in-the-well) — Wasyl, Anna, Semen and Kateryna Koval; Petro and Pelagia Hnat; Mychajło Szafran; Fedir Mac; Olena Krywda; Hryhorij Pyś; Stepan Kiczura; Anastasija Sowa. Players who hold the list or have heard the names should speak them; players who don't acknowledge them as *the twelve who stayed and were killed here.*
- **[Dmytro Kosach](../characters/secondary-characters.md#dmytro-kosach)** — the UPA fighter, Paraskewia's lover, whose shot triggered the massacre and who died in it with everyone else. Held apart on her list, the thirteenth. The 1947 name the players are most likely to learn first.
- **[Edward Barnaś](../characters/soldier.md)** — the 1954 lynch victim. The *only* 1954 body in the well, and the death Paraskewia's twenty-year rite never covered because she never knew he was down there. Naming him is the piece only the players can supply.

**Conditional — name them only if it happened:**

- **[Janina Gajda](../characters/ciotka.md)** — *only if [Rezeń took her body to the well](rezen-takes-the-body.md)* ([`rezen-fed-ciotka-to-well`](../clues/clues.md#rezen-fed-ciotka-to-well)). The freshest dead, and the cruelest: the woman who saved [Edek](../characters/glupek.md) from Rezeń in 1954 now lies with the man she saved him from. If the players know she's down there, she must be named.
- **[Paraskewia Chyłak](../characters/hag.md)** — *only if she died at [the confrontation](well-confrontation.md)* and Rezeń dropped her in the well. Then the players are naming their own guide — the woman who tended these dead for twenty years, added to them.
- **[Stanisław Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md)** — *only if he died at [the confrontation](well-confrontation.md)* (shot as he retreated, he dragged himself over the rim and into the water on his own). He is in the well now — the auxiliary of 1947, the killer of 1954, the well's longest vessel, finally the thing put *in* it. The rite does not sort the guilty from the innocent; it names **all** the dead of this ground. Naming Rezeń is the hardest acknowledgement and the most complete — the players speak the name of the man who made half these dead, and let even him rest. A group that can do this has understood what the rite is for.

**Not among the dead:**

- [Hania Barnaś](../characters/jagna.md) fled the 1954 lynch and was never found ([`jagna-fled-the-lynch`](../clues/clues.md#jagna-fled-the-lynch)). She is *not* in the well and must not be named as dead — the players cannot know her fate, and the rite does not require it. If they name her anyway, believing her dead, it does no harm; the GM simply doesn't count it.
- [kpt. Henryk Ćwiek](../characters/kbw-officer.md) died here in 1947 — Dmytro's shot killed him and triggered the massacre — but he is *not* of this well. He was the commander of the unit that murdered the village; his body was recovered by his own soldiers and buried by the state, and his death is the thing the [cover-up](../story-facts/old-village-massacre.md#the-cover-up) was built to hide. The Lemko rite mourns the murdered village, not the officer whose killing set the slaughter off. He does not belong in the naming and the rite does not ask for him — the one death at this ground that stays outside it.

**Getting Babcia here** is its own chain and the hardest leg: she can't cross the forest alone, only [Barbara](../characters/barbara.md) can bring her ([`barbara-must-bring-babcia`](../clues/clues.md#barbara-must-bring-babcia)). The players don't strictly need her body at the well, but her being there is the most powerful version there is. *(Note: ks. Pająk no longer gatekeeps this — he does not bless or block the rite. See [spiritual-endings.md](../story-facts/spiritual-endings.md). If the rite is performed, he shows up to plead against it, not to permit it.)*

## Setup

The well clearing, or the ruined cerkiew if the water has already claimed the well. Rain, always the rain — but for once the players came here to give something instead of dig something up. If they brought Paraskewia's materials, they lay them out from the diagram: beeswax candles in a semicircle, an icon propped to face the water, bread on a cloth, a bowl of stream-water and herbs, church incense that won't quite catch in the wet. It looks like what they found at her cabin because it is.

The arrangement depends on who came:

- **Paraskewia is here** — she kneels at the rim where she always has and lets the players stand with her. She doesn't correct their clumsiness. Twenty years alone, and tonight she isn't.
- **Paraskewia is dead** — the players do it from fragments, from memory, from a cloth diagram they can barely read. Nobody who knows the form is left. They are replacing a twenty-year relationship with raw intent, and it shows.
- **Stefania Kopacz is here** — an old Lemko woman, hunched almost double, standing where her own people were killed, is the heaviest presence in the clearing. When the fog lifts and she begins the panakhyda, the words are older than anyone else present.

However it's staged, at least one living voice has to speak — the words, the names, the truth. The dead have waited twenty years. They can hear whoever is willing to say it.

## Opportunities

- **The well itself** — with the light and the quiet, the surface shows what the digging never made bearable: this is a grave, not a crime scene. **Culture:** the rite the players are attempting is a real Lemko tradition for the unquiet dead — fire, incense, offerings, the opening of a way for the souls to pass. Reinforces [`hag-tends-the-well`](../clues/clues.md#hag-tends-the-well), [`dead-never-mourned`](../clues/clues.md#dead-never-mourned).
- **Paraskewia's bearing (if alive)** — she isn't grateful and she isn't hopeful. **Read:** she is *relieved*. For the first time the weight is shared. Whatever the rite does or doesn't do, she is no longer the only one who knows.
- **Stefania's face (if present)** — the dementia fog burns off as she prays. **Read / Language:** she is entirely lucid here, more herself than the players have ever seen her. The words are coming back complete. She never knew these twelve — but she has carried the mourning for her people her whole life, and here, at last, she has dead to say it over.
- **Edek at the treeline (if he drifts in)** — the boy who was meant to die stands still and calm near the well, agitation gone. **Read:** something in him recognises what's happening. He isn't afraid of this. He belongs to it.
- **What's missing** — **Observation:** a player taking stock realises the rite doesn't require a body recovered, a name proven, or a court. It requires someone to stand here and mean it. That is either a relief or an indictment, depending on the player.

## Actions

### Perform the ritual
- **Requires:** All three components in hand — the Form, the Words, and the Truth (see above). At least one player willing to speak aloud.
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** The players name the dead, say what was done to them, and ask nothing in return — no justice promised, no explanation offered, just acknowledgement. There is no flash of light; the story never confirms whether it "works" in any supernatural sense. But:
  - **The dreams ease.** That night, for the first time since the well woke with the rising water, the players sleep. Not well. But they sleep. **World State Change:** the [well's nightmare pressure](../story-facts/the-well.md#the-dreams--the-only-supernatural-element) lifts — GM stops taking the nightly "bad dreams" composure hit.
  - **The atmosphere lifts.** The crushing dread over %NEW_VILLAGE% loosens. Not gone — breathable.
  - **The rain does not stop.** The flood is still coming. The dead are still dead. This is not a fairy tale. But the wound has been acknowledged for the first time in twenty years.
  - **Ending Progress — the Ritual ending.** This is the payoff of the [ritual path](../story-facts/the-ritual.md). Compatible with every other ending — a group can name every victim here and still cut a deal to bury the living guilty ([wife-junior-investigation](../story-facts/wife-junior-investigation.md)).
- **Branches:**
  - **Paraskewia leads it (alive):** the strongest form. Her twenty years of practice hold the shape while the players supply the truth she never had — the *1954* dead she didn't know about. Reinforcement, not reconstruction.
  - **Reconstructed (Paraskewia dead):** harder, rougher, assembled from her abandoned materials and the players' will. The pronunciation is wrong, the offerings substituted, the diagram unclear. **It still works** — imperfect is enough, because the dead need presence, not perfection.
  - **Stefania speaks the panakhyda at the well:** the most powerful version. An old Lemko woman giving her murdered people the prayers they were never given, on the ground where they died. If the players earned this, give it full weight.
  - **Players speak the words themselves:** they learned the panakhyda from Babcia (**Language**) or from Paraskewia. Clumsy, accented, but sincere. Works.
  - **Believer among the players:** a genuinely faithful player speaking over the dead lands harder — the rite meets real belief. Costs nothing extra; give the moment.

### Speak only the truth (incomplete rite)
- **Requires:** The Truth (players can name the dead and say what happened), but missing the Form and/or the Words.
- **Cost:** 1 action
- **Outcome:** No candles, no panakhyda — just people standing at a well saying out loud what was done here and to whom. It is bare and it is not nothing. **World State Change:** the atmosphere loosens slightly and the players sleep a little easier — but the full lift doesn't come, and the GM may keep the dream pressure at reduced weight. The core was always acknowledgement; the form only deepens it. A group with no way to reach Babcia or Paraskewia's cabin can still do this much.

### Try to perform it without the truth
- **Requires:** Form and/or Words, but the players cannot actually name the dead or say what happened (they don't know yet).
- **Cost:** 1 action (wasted)
- **Outcome:** Candles, incense, borrowed words over a hole they don't understand. **Nothing shifts.** The dreams continue, the dread stays. The rite is not a spell and it exposes that here: without the truth it is pantomime. **This does not advance the Ritual ending.** The players leave knowing they missed something — the well is not asking for a performance. (If a GM wants a nudge: the night after, the dreams are *worse* — the dead were reached for and not answered.)

## Exits

- Back down the forest path to the village / [PGR quarters](../locations/pgr-quarters.md) — to sleep, for once
- To [Hag's Cabin](../locations/hags-cabin.md) — to return her materials, or to grieve her if she's gone
- Onward into the endgame — [the flood](the-flood.md), [the report](the-report.md), and whether [Rezeń](../characters/butcher.md) is still out there

## If Missed

If the players never assemble the rite — or never choose to stand at the well and speak — the well is never acknowledged by anyone but Paraskewia, whose rite only ever covered the [1947](../story-facts/old-village-massacre.md) dead and can't hold a well the flood is prying open around the unmourned [1954](../story-facts/the-lynch.md) death she never knew was there. The dreams keep escalating to the end. The dead stay unquiet; the flood takes them unmourned, completing the [second erasure](../story-facts/old-village-massacre.md). The Ritual ending does not happen.

**If Rezeń is alive** and the rite *is* performed, it costs him: the compulsion that [Paraskewia's death satisfied](../story-facts/the-ritual.md#what-happens) is reopened — properly this time. His calm breaks, he stops sleeping again, and the well has new guardians undoing what he did. See [well-influence](../story-facts/well-influence.md#stanisław-rezeń-butcher).
