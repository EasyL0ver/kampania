# Wojewoda's Ask

**Location:** [PGR Office](../locations/pgr-office.md)
**Present:** [Zbigniew Gajda](../characters/wojewoda.md), players
**Available:** After Zbigniew knows the flood is possible ([tell about the flood risk](../characters/wojewoda.md#tell-about-the-flood-risk)) and knows the committee is assessing property for compensation.

## Trigger

Zbigniew learns the committee is putting property values on paper for flood compensation. He asks to speak with them alone.

## Setup

He closes the door. No performance this time — he asks, plainly, as the man who has run this village for twenty years. State compensation follows the deed, and by the deed half his people own nothing: the labourers live in PGR housing, the farm holds the paper, and the money will go to the state, not to them. He asks the committee to write those houses as the villagers' own, so the people who live in them are paid when the water takes them.

He names no one. He doesn't mention [Barbara](../characters/barbara.md)'s brick house or [Janina](../characters/ciotka.md)'s cottage, both of which the paper says aren't theirs. He leaves it to the committee's conscience — a false record that robs no one but the state, and puts money in the hands of people about to lose everything.

## Opportunities

- **Read:** He isn't scheming or skimming — nothing here comes to him. This is the leader covering his people the only way left to him.
- **Law:** What he's asking for is fraud against the state — deliberately false property records. Clean on paper, a crime in fact.
