- Its throat is torn.
- Blood is in the mud.
- Drag marks lead toward the treeline.
- The other animals crowd in the far corner.
- [@name](../characters/wojewoda.md) counts the damage.

- [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) blames [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md) in front of the workers.
