**Location:** [@name](../locations/new-village/pgr-farm/pgr-farm.md)

## Hook

- Workers gather at the pen at dawn.
- Crows work the carcass.
- The dead animal is visible from the farm approach.

## Setup

- A sheep lies dead in the pen.[](#animal-confident-inside-familiar-pen)
- Its throat is torn.
- Blood is in the mud.
- Drag marks lead toward the treeline.
- The other animals crowd in the far corner.
- [@name](../characters/wojewoda.md) counts the damage.[](#failure-real-fear-older-than-wolves)
- [@name](../characters/neighbour.md) stands nearby with his rifle.
- [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) blames [@surname](../characters/neighbour.md) in front of the workers[](#listen-to-the-farmhands).[](#workers-doubt-neighbour-praise-knife-skill)[](#neighbour-blamed-replacement-matters-more)
- [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) tells [@name](../characters/foreman.md) he will handle it.[](#bracing-to-authorize-long-avoided-act)
- [@first-name](../characters/wojewoda.md) later sends [@name](../characters/wujas.md) to the edge house with the request that [@surname](../characters/butcher.md) deal with the wolves; see [@name](wujas-visits-butcher.md).
