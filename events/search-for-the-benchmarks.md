# Search for the Benchmarks

**Location:** [Far-Ridge Streambed](../locations/far-ridge-streambed.md)
**Present:** Survey party
**Available:** Holding [dam-builders-surveyed-streambed](../clues/clues.md#dam-builders-surveyed-streambed)

## Trigger

- The party goes looking for the dam crews' benchmark markers rather than running a survey of their own.

## Setup

- The Solina dam crews set stamped geodetic benchmarks (repery) at the streambed col and beside %NEW_VILLAGE% years ago.
- The markers are old, weathered, and half-buried in undergrowth and slid earth; there is no map to their exact spots.
- The search is open-ended: it runs an unknown number of actions before anyone turns up both markers.
- Once found, anyone can copy the two stamped elevations off them, no skill needed.
- This is the no-geologist route compared to [surveying the col](surveying-the-streambed.md), but the hunt eats an uncertain amount of time.

## Opportunities

- **Read the ground for likely spots** `(requires: Survival)` — Survey crews set benchmarks on stable rock at clear sightlines; a woodsman reads the col and the village edge for where they would have driven them, narrowing the hunt. → No clue; shortens the search (GM's call).

## Actions

### Comb the ground for the markers
- **Requires:** Searching the far-ridge col and the village edge
- **Prompted by:** [dam-builders-surveyed-streambed](../clues/clues.md#dam-builders-surveyed-streambed)
- **Cost:** Open-ended: an unknown number of actions (GM's call), fewer if a **Survival** read narrows the ground.
- **Outcome:** You turn up the two stamped benchmarks, at the col and by the village, and copy the elevations off them.
- **Gives:** [streambed-parameters](../clues/clues.md#streambed-parameters)

## Mechanics

- The search has no fixed cost. The GM sets a hidden number of actions; a successful **Survival** read (opportunity above) lowers it.

## Exits

- Holding [streambed-parameters](../clues/clues.md#streambed-parameters), interpret them into [streambed-dead-ends](../clues/clues.md#streambed-dead-ends): read them as a surveyor with the [kit](../items/geologists-kit.md), phone [prof. Bieńkowski](../characters/professor.md), or show [Michał Pytlak](../characters/foreman.md).
